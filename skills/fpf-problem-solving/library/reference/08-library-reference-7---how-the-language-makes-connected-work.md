## Library.Reference:7 - How the language makes connected work intelligible

The Library connects understanding with what to do and what to develop next. Its pattern languages are declarative: their order of publication does not prescribe a project process. A current question selects a relevant contribution; a dependency requires another result only when the selected operation needs it. Existing adequate results can let the reader enter farther along the connection.

A supplying Method returns something of an identified kind, a receiving operation uses it, and the conditions on that result remain available at the join. A model with an error bound may support one decision but not another. A proposed contribution needs agreement and authority before it can be relied on as a commitment. An observed successful run can support a bounded performance claim while leaving transfer or independent capability open.

Establish what should become understandable or possible for the recipient and what would make that result sufficient. [FPF C.39](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/96-c-39---find-and-develop-a-way-to-obtain-a-result.md) develops a missing way where necessary. [FPF E.4.CM](../../sections/11-part-e---the-fpf-constitution-and-authoring-guides/06-e-4-cm---develop-connected-methods-as-framework-contribution.md) distinguishes a missing Method or connection from an adequate contribution whose explanation or access needs repair. The public accounts explain the connections; the full patterns supply the developed operations. A link to a related topic leaves an unwritten operation to be developed.

Connected use also depends on shared meanings across publications. [FPF.Reference:9](../../sections/04-first-principles-framework-architectural-reference/09-fpf-reference-9---thinking-through-writing.md) explains how writing can develop and revise the needed understanding; [FPF.Reference:10](../../sections/04-first-principles-framework-architectural-reference/10-fpf-reference-10---thinking-oriented-architecture-not-a-desc.md) explains the choice between direct assertions, classification and ontological coordination. Apply these grounds when a result or definition crosses publication boundaries.

For such a use, recover which identity, characteristic or relation each contribution relies on. Resolve a consequential difference through its defining pattern. A DPF can add domain conditions for using a Core result; retain those conditions with the receiving use. Shared spelling or a common card layout cannot establish that two local meanings agree. Written comparisons can expose the difference and preserve the grounds of a qualified connection. Keep a sufficient direct use instead of creating another shared definition merely because several publications are involved.

When a common rule changes, reconsider the dependent claims in the publications that use that rule, while preserving uses whose grounds still hold. A layout change can preserve the meanings and grounds needed by the receiver; omitting a condition or rationale can leave that use without a recoverable basis. The receiving explanation should make the dependency and the useful return clear, with the common definition maintained at its subject pattern.

There is also a connection within ongoing work. Checking the units in a calculation can participate in obtaining a model result, comparing a design and supporting a larger engineering decision at the same moment. The encompassing purpose determines which distinctions the check must preserve. [FPF B.1.5.EW](../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/07-b-1-5-ew---recover-how-constituent-actions-enact-encompassin.md) recovers this constituent-to-whole connection; [FPF B.1.5.RS](../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/08-b-1-5-rs---evaluate-a-method-replacement-in-its-encompassing.md) examines a proposed replacement in its encompassing uses. A missing intermediate capability or unavailable shared resource can defeat the whole even when elementary operations are individually available.

Advice connects an answer to work for its recipient. Following it can require action or inquiry, delay other work and consume means. [FPF C.11.DUA](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/24-c-11-dua---make-advice-and-evidence-demands-worth-their-burd.md) recovers that demanded work and relates three questions: what the present basis supports, what the recipient can reasonably do with it, and what additional inquiry could improve that use. A limitation on a claim does not by itself require a particular investigation. Equally, the difficulty of obtaining evidence cannot strengthen the claim. Give the supported answer and the continuation available to its recipient.

Compare following the advice with keeping the present way and with feasible narrower uses or smaller changes. Include the recipient's attention, learning and adaptation, coordination and execution, and what is delayed, displaced or put at risk. When proposed changes rely on the same means, compare their combined demand with what remains available alongside ongoing commitments. Judge the gain at the horizon of the intended result. New knowledge can itself be that result, before an operational intervention is selected. Use the domain research Method to establish how attainable inquiry could produce the knowledge or new possibility sought.

[FPF C.11.CRC](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/22-c-11-crc---configuration-relative-contribution-comparison.md) supplies a missing comparison of complete configurations, including their transitions and later options. C.11 selects among available alternatives when a local choice is ready; C.19.2 limits the effort of applying apparatus under the required result and guarantee. A cheaper route that drops that guarantee is a different proposal. DUA can appraise a requirement's purpose and burden; a binding requirement continues to govern until the relevant authority or agreement changes it. Inquiry can matter even when the physical action stays the same, because it changes the supported claim or admissible use.

When observations, conditional future choices and realization still need to be connected, [FPF C.43](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/127-c-43---connect-observation-choice-and-action-in-control.md) supplies that broader control construction. Explain which possible observation would change which later action, whether that action will remain available, and how the selected continuation can take effect. DUA appraises advice and demanded work within this construction. Changed consequences can make an earlier bounded action inadequate; lost access or time can make a previously useful inquiry unavailable. Reconsider the dependent continuation and preserve the uses whose grounds remain sound.

This account applies to human, AI and mixed arrangements. A supplied result and the recipient's own mastery are different goals. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) compares complete performance and provision, including coordination, checking, waiting and correction. Assignment and permission remain separate from capability. The Library supplies ways to reason about the arrangement; the actual situation supplies its facts, powers and available performers.

Finish the present undertaking when its intended result and the basis for its receiving use are sufficient, including an explicit limit or justified stop where needed. That result can also change what becomes worth attempting. [FPF C.40.OD](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/117-c-40-od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development.md) connects development of knowledge, Methods, capability and provision; [FPF C.40.NP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/119-c-40-np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems.md) uses the changed position to construct a further worthwhile attainable problem. Conducting one's own development additionally requires the ability to notice a consequential new difficulty or possibility and organize the next transition. [Section 9](10-library-reference-9---open-ended-development-belongs-across.md) develops these connections across the Library. A bounded undertaking can finish while its result supports that wider continuation.

### Library.Reference:7.1 - When a way works in the small but fails in the large

An analyst's personal tool can work because the analyst interprets its inputs, supplies missing context, checks unusual answers and decides what to do. Asking several units to use the visible calculation autonomously changes that arrangement. More examples of the successful calculation do not establish the meanings, provision, authority or recovery that the new use needs.

“In the small” and “in the large” are useful search expressions for this difficulty, not two sizes to which every Method belongs. One author can create dependencies that require coordinated change; several performers can work independently when the necessary conditions are provided. A larger batch for the same analyst can leave the original way sufficient. Begin with C.39:4.1 to recover what the receiving work needs and what the earlier success depended on. Its remaining Solution constructs the missing contribution and joins it to the useful whole.

### Library.Reference:7.2 - From available information to useful questions and possible actions

An engineer, manager or researcher may know that some understanding is needed while being unable to say which answer would change the work. [B.5:4.1.2](../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/23-b-5---choose-the-next-reasoning-contribution.md#b5412---find-useful-questions-and-possible-actions-from-incomplete-information) explains how to examine accessible material, propose an account of the subject and derive questions or possible actions from it. Its inputs can be ordinary material already at hand; its result can be a better question, a conditional explanation or a justified next attempt. It does not require a complete account of the future.

Start by examining something that can make the subject less opaque: an actual episode and the use of its result, an available artifact and the operation that produced it, an explanatory source or a proposed capability tried in a possible use. [B.5.PI](../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/42-b-5-pi---initiate-inquiry-from-work.md) obtains a question from ordinary work before a problem is felt. [F.1](../../sections/12-part-f---the-unification-suite-concept-sets-sensecells-and-system-role/05-f-1---find-and-select-sources-for-a-current-question.md) helps seek sources even when the vocabulary is unfamiliar; [A.15.9](../../sections/07-cluster-a-v---constitutional-principles-of-the-kernel/24-a-15-9---use-or-request-a-bounded-result-from-another-practi.md) obtains help with a formulation as well as a formulated question.

The encounter supplies material for a provisional account, rather than a conclusion by itself. A participant's demonstration can reveal the unit they need to distinguish. An operation can reveal a premise it silently relies on. A new capability can suggest a use not included in the original aim. [B.5.FM](../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/31-b-5-fm---construct-a-model-for-the-question.md) connects the relevant participants through a subject relation and derives a small consequence. Reasoning from intended use toward needed means and from available means toward possible use allows either side to change. A scheme or mantra can direct this examination toward a consequential relation before anyone complains, but its questions still need subject content.

Follow that relation far enough to construct a revealing contrast. If an operation requires a premise, examine what follows when the premise differs. If a representation merges two cases, try to derive whether their receiving uses can differ. If an attempt exposes an unavailable contribution, recover what it supplied and seek another way to obtain that result. These operations can produce a new possibility, a reason to change the question or a limit.

The attendance-report case in B.5:5.8 shows the whole connection. Reading the grouping operation and asking a leader to demonstrate the use exposes a distinction between a participant and a displayed name. Constructing two cases with the same report but different people reveals what that report cannot decide. A documented identifier then supplies a candidate repair. The relevant alternatives were obtained through the subject's operations and use.

Once a distinction has been constructed, ask which further inquiries or actions it supports. Keep an action, an uncertain condition, a consequence under that condition and a later action chosen after an observation distinguishable. [C.39](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/96-c-39---find-and-develop-a-way-to-obtain-a-result.md) develops an absent operation or a way to connect operations; [C.40.CD](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/106-c-40-cd---develop-problems-and-ways-of-solving-them-together.md) allows the question and the way to change together. [C.43](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/127-c-43---connect-observation-choice-and-action-in-control.md) connects the observations and choices of a control arrangement. These constructions preserve jointly necessary contributions and sufficient alternatives; they do not require every inquiry to become a branching plan.

Return the newly expressible difference to what the person needs to understand or obtain. Explain what changes in the result, supported claim, admissible use or research direction. [C.11.DUA](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/24-c-11-dua---make-advice-and-evidence-demands-worth-their-burd.md) then compares further inquiry with using the present basis, a smaller sufficient contribution or stopping. Include assistance, access, learning and delay. The same physical action can remain appropriate while the claim supporting it changes. Conversely, a discovered possibility can justify constructing a new action instead of refining a prediction over old options.

Unavailable help changes which inquiry or action can be carried out. In the report case, an analyst can derive the grouping limitation and prepare a repair while waiting for the data owner's qualification. Neither act establishes what the absent owner would have said or permitted. Keep the dependent claim conditional, use another qualified source if available, or defer that part. Preserve independent work that still contributes. When the result sought is the practitioner's own mastery, an externally supplied answer alone does not fulfil it.

Open research has a different sufficient result from selecting an intervention. The measurement case in B.5:5.9 begins with unexplained traces and inspection of their production; it obtains a previously unrecognized reference operation and a question that can distinguish explanations. The initial exploration was justified by access to the measurement process and an attainable kind of contribution, not a promise to discover that operation. Its later candidate explanation still needs appropriate evidence. A mathematical inquiry can likewise produce a new representation or an obstruction before an application is selected.

Re-examine the provisional account and its sources when an observation or a constructed case contradicts the account or shows that it omits a distinction needed for the intended use. If needed help is unavailable, seek another way to obtain its result or revise the work that depends on it. A newly discovered possibility may justify changing the question or aim. A sufficient result can finish the present inquiry. A first account remains open to later correction. The useful change in practice is that a request to judge significance now comes with ways to obtain the material and reasoning on which that judgement depends.
