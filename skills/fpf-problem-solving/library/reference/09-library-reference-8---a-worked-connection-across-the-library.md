## Library.Reference:8 - A worked connection across the Library

The following constructed case illustrates how these contributions fit together.

Three laboratories want a more dependable diagnostic measurement practice. An existing calculation works for familiar specimens but gives unexplained disagreements for a new class. The groups also want practitioners to handle further unfamiliar cases. They are considering better source access, an external specialist service, a shared support office and local provision coordinated by their leads.

### Library.Reference:8.1 - Different contributions to one useful result

Start with what the director needs. A working measurement arrangement, an account of how the discrepancy arose, a practitioner able to diagnose the next unfamiliar case and an agreement that all three laboratories can honour are different results. One may supply grounds for another. Decide which contributions are needed by tracing the director’s decision back to the evidence, work and authority it requires.

[Decision Design (PSDA)](../../dpfs/psda/_index.md) helps construct the question and compare alternatives without assuming at the outset that the answer is an office or a course. [Research Method Practice (RMP)](../../dpfs/rmp/_index.md) supplies inquiry when existing knowledge cannot explain the disagreement. If the issue is a model premise, [Foundational Thinking](../../dpfs/foundational-thinking-dpf-suite/_index.md) connects physical explanation, model correspondence and calculation. A faster or more accurate computation of the same inadequate model does not resolve that premise.

For example, one laboratory has a dated peak reading and another has the last value read after the instrument settled. To decide whether the specimen ever exceeded a limit during the test, check whether either record already answers. One reading tied to this specimen and test establishes an exceedance if its lower error bound lies above the limit. To certify compliance throughout, an upper bound must cover the physical maximum, including any motion between samples. A peak indicator with suitable response and a justified error bound can supply that result even when read later. Use both records when their verified coverage together bounds the complete interval, when the order asks for their comparison, or when a conflict between relevant readings needs resolving. If there is an uncovered interval, establish a bound for it before claiming compliance throughout. To decide only whether the two laboratories followed the agreed intervention, their records of performing the prescribed work may already suffice. [Foundational.Reference:7.3](../../dpfs/foundational-thinking-dpf-suite/suite-reference/08-foundational-reference-7---sources-and-conceptual-synthesis.md#foundationalreference73---keep-the-strength-of-each-ground-visible)

Suppose the director asks what information was available to each laboratory by Wednesday, when the first laboratory made its decision. One laboratory dated its discrepant reading on Tuesday, decided on Wednesday and sent the reading to the other two on Thursday. To reconstruct what each laboratory could have known on Wednesday, use [Narrative order (NSTD.2)](../../dpfs/nstd/17-nstd-2---choose-a-narrative-order-that-keeps-needed-source-r.md#nstd24---solution) and [participant viewpoint (NSTD.4)](../../dpfs/nstd/19-nstd-4---choose-narrative-voice-and-focalization-while-keepi.md#nstd44---solution). Recover the time of the reading, any channels through which it could have reached the decision holder, and when the choice was made. One dated receipt can establish access by that time. To establish that information was unavailable or give a complete account of what was knowable, cover the other relevant channels. To explain why a particular decision holder actually chose an action, identify the rule applied, the judgement made or other evidence of that choice. An inquiry into the cause of the measurement discrepancy may still be open.

The resulting explanation changes what must be obtained. If a usable method already exists and the only defect is access, [Implementation Facilitation (IS)](../../dpfs/is/_index.md) can supply the missing help. If the desired result is each practitioner's independent judgement, external answers alone are insufficient; [Human Capability Development (HCD)](../../dpfs/human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/_index.md) supplies the relevant learning and capability qualification. When organizational contributions or authority must change, [Organization Change Engineering (OCE)](../../dpfs/management-dpf-suite/oce/_index.md) constructs that realization while ongoing obligations remain in view.

For an explanation the laboratories will use, ask which specimens, conditions or measurements must be distinguished for their decision. Two laboratory reports can repeat the same reference reading. If the proposed cause depends on independent measurements, trace the reports to the measurements and check whether they share a source of error relevant to the proposed cause. Separate judgements about the same reading can still satisfy an order for two independent reviews. If two complete lists cover disjoint sets of specimens and use the same criterion, they can together establish the total for their union. A shared specimen must be accounted for when the unit is a distinct specimen. Use [EXD.Reference:3](../../dpfs/exd/03-exd-reference-3---obtain-and-carry-the-subject-explanation.md) to construct the explanation at the needed reach. A single adequate source can settle the original discrepancy.

The office alternatives concern how these transformations will be performed and supported. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) compares their complete performance and provision. A common office for several masteries is an alternative when the same contribution can serve them without erasing their different criteria, and the gain from sharing exceeds the extra coordination and interpretation burden. Specialized development support has a case when it supplies methodological inquiry that each workplace lacks while receiving subject expertise from the laboratories. Local leadership, distributed specialists or external provision can be sufficient when those arrangements supply the whole needed contribution under attainable conditions.

[STR.16](../../dpfs/management-dpf-suite/str/34-str-16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branc.md) compares shared and branch-specific support while preserving each branch's purpose. [LDR.2](../../dpfs/management-dpf-suite/ldr/09-ldr-2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions.md) obtains responses to a feasible joint proposal. Where independent groups require a common mandate, [CB.13](../../dpfs/management-dpf-suite/cb/23-cb-13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions.md) supplies that contribution; the participants retain powers they have not delegated.

Two activities are proposed for a specialist who has eight hours available in the week. Each activity on its own can use the five hours it needs before its deadline. All five hours, including preparation and return, are specific to that activity. The pair needs ten specialist-hours in the week. Shared administration does not supply the missing two hours. Moving both activities within those same eight hours still leaves two hours missing. Extra hours available before the deadlines, an authorized change of deadline that frees enough capacity, a qualified substitute or an agreed smaller commitment can make a different arrangement feasible. The choice depends on which change preserves the required result, not on the number of offices or participants. This is a condition on the whole combination, not a defect in either local Method.

For a guarantee about all three laboratories, establish that their conditions can hold together and that one proposed arrangement meets each laboratory’s promised use in every allowed situation. If an existing comparison establishes that every allowed explanation of the discrepancy leads to the same decision today, the adviser can finish the recommendation while its precise cause is open. The party authorized to commit the affected laboratories can decide. A decision can wait if each possible observation leaves the authorized holder time to take an action that meets the brief. The action can differ with the observation. If a guarantee was promised, the action chosen for an observation must work in every situation still consistent with it. Check access and deadlines for each branch, including any advance booking. A service allowed to miss an agreed fraction of deadlines needs evidence about that fraction or a sufficient bound.

### Library.Reference:8.2 - What a changed condition sends back

If the new specimen class defeats a model assumption, revise the assumption and the conclusions that depend on it. If the model is sound but the specialist withdraws, reconsider how the laboratories will obtain the service and what they can promise. Successful diagnoses show what the service delivered. To judge a practitioner's ability to investigate unfamiliar cases, examine their own work under the required conditions. If the records contain only the specialist's diagnoses, the practitioner's ability remains open. For a sound finding that a laboratory cannot use, [EXD.Reference:2](../../dpfs/exd/02-exd-reference-2---the-distinctions-that-change-the-work.md) helps identify what the practitioner needs. They may need access to the calibration table or an explanation of how its values justify a correction. If evidence identifies a skill the practitioner needs to develop, arrange practice in that skill.

For a commission to teach independent diagnosis, follow the practitioner's actual route through the explanation, coaching and assessment. Give the practitioner the available records and ordinary tools. If those already establish what caused the discrepancy, examine the practitioner's own inference. If competing explanations remain and a test would help, leave the commissioned choices of explanations, check and use of its result to the practitioner before giving the specialist’s solution. If the commission is instead to design the local support service, the specialist may supply the diagnosis and the local lead may still choose and justify the service. The criteria for credit follow the work assigned to each.

These branches show why the Library retains different languages. Research retains its independently useful knowledge result and its own field boundary when this engineering work uses it. The existing Methods preserve the changed premise, the receiving action and the relevant return.

Once the practice works adequately, one branch can finish. The new understanding may reveal a harder but attainable problem, such as recognizing an unfamiliar failure before it affects a measurement. [FPF C.40.NP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/122-c-40-np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems.md) develops that continuation. If several independent practitioners need to reconstruct and adapt the method, [FPF C.36.RP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/132-c-36-rp---sustain-and-renew-shared-ways-of-working.md) supplies cultural retention and renewal, with [Community Management (CB)](../../dpfs/management-dpf-suite/cb/_index.md) where participation or common work needs organizing. Neither a permanent office nor an endlessly open project is required by that continuation.

The same architecture can serve a question that has no laboratory or system-realization goal. A mathematical investigation may finish with a warranted construction; a narrative may finish with a source-faithful account suited to its purpose.
