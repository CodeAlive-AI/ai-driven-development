## Library.Reference:5 - Why some DPFs stand outside the Suites

A standalone DPF is an independently usable domain language whose own subject and useful results warrant its boundary, without a demonstrated constituent relation to one of the described assemblies in this Library edition.

The ten standalone languages have the following distinct contributions:

| DPF | Contribution that retains its own boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [Decision Design (PSDA)](../../dpfs/psda/_index.md) | Constructing a decision situation, ends and possible means, alternatives, consequence and uncertainty accounts and warranted recommendations, together with the engagement, participation and facilitation that make them usable. |
| [Research Method Practice (RMP)](../../dpfs/rmp/_index.md) | Inquiry and criticized knowledge contributions, including source recovery, observation, research design and evidence limits. |
| [Explanation Design (EXD)](../../dpfs/exd/_index.md) | Making warranted relations recoverable and usable by an intended recipient. |
| [Narrativization and Narrative Studies (NSTD)](../../dpfs/nstd/_index.md) | Constructing and interpreting narratives: forming working accounts and fictional or hypothetical events, choosing purpose, viewpoint and presentation, and examining their use with recoverable source relations. |
| [Implementation Facilitation (IS)](../../dpfs/is/_index.md) | Agreeing, constructing, adapting and continuing facilitation through which participants make an interpretable practice workable under actual conditions, including brokerage and their further facilitation capability. |
| [Financial Domain Modeling (FDM)](../../dpfs/fdm/_index.md) | Financial parties, positions, rights, obligations, events and flows with their domain meanings. |
| [Economic Reasoning and Coordination (ECO)](../../dpfs/eco/_index.md) | Scarcity, incentives, exchange, commitments, institutions and coordination under economic conditions. |
| [Defense and Transmission Modeling (DTM)](../../dpfs/dtm/_index.md) | How practices persist and spread, how they help or impair work, and how protective action changes those outcomes. |
| [Music and Dance Practice Engineering (MDPE)](../../dpfs/mdpe/_index.md) | Musical and dance creation, performance, learning, transmission and cultural continuation, including engineered performing configurations and changes to their supporting environment. |
| [Embodied Rhythmics (RHY)](../../dpfs/rhy/_index.md) | Performed rhythmic relations, their representation, transformation, coordination, practice and observation. |

Within Decision Design, construction of alternatives and professional engagement have different completion conditions. A sound comparison can exist while representation, consent or the conditions for useful advice remain unresolved. Conversely, a well-conducted engagement does not supply a missing mathematical or empirical argument. One language keeps these Methods independently selectable while explaining their necessary connections. Its warranted recommendation is not the authorized choice, implementation of that choice, or engineering of an operational decision service.

Narrativization supplies another case. A narrative can serve inquiry, management, teaching or artistic practice, but its purposes and viewpoint choices are not exhausted by any of those uses. Putting it under every consuming field would confuse use with composition. Leaving it standalone preserves the whole narrative question and its returns to source.

Standalone placement is open to substantive revision. A newly established larger discipline could include a field under [FPF C.20](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/39-c-20---composition-of-u-discipline.md); a changed problem family could justify repartition.
