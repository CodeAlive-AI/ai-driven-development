# FPF Library Architectural Reference

**DocID:** `Library` within **FPF Library**.

**Architecture and connected use of the Library's pattern languages**

- **Author:** Anatoly Levenchuk, with AI-assisted development
- **Date:** 24 October 2026
- **Use:** Understand the Library's architecture, select the appropriate level of explanation and preserve the relations between contributions from different fields.
- **Full publications:** [FPF Core](../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md), the [domain-framework catalogue](../../dpfs/_index.md) and the publications linked below.

FPF Library gives people and AI agents a connected language for reasoning about work, constructing useful results and developing better ways to obtain them. As a methodology of open-ended development, it can serve as a canon for a shared culture of thinking and acting. Its common distinctions and general Methods meet specialized knowledge in domain frameworks. A reader can use one sufficient contribution directly or follow a larger question across several publications.

This Reference explains how that language is organized and why its boundaries matter. It develops the shared account across individual frameworks: what the larger wholes are, how their contributions connect, which alternatives explain the arrangement and what changes when a relied-on premise changes. The full patterns supply the Methods, cases and source grounds. For practical search and reading, use [Using FPF Library](../USING-FPF.md); the [FPF Library overview](../_index.md) gives the short introduction.
