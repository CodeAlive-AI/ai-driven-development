## HRM.Source:2 - Culture, offices and contemporary agent use

The author's [customer-orientation post](https://ailev.livejournal.com/1739861.html), [role-community post](https://ailev.livejournal.com/1741032.html), [engagement post](https://ailev.livejournal.com/1741983.html) and [culture post](https://ailev.livejournal.com/1735885.html), September–November 2024, motivate non-linear trajectories, differing personal and corporate purposes, distributed contributions and culture as shared practice. Their historical genealogies, political analogies and AI forecasts are not empirical premises of this framework. Full qualification of all their embedded literature is not claimed.

The selected opening of Gagné et al.'s [2022 self-determination account](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/2022_GagneParkerEtAl_Understanding.pdf) and McMillan–Chavis's [1986 sense-of-community theory](https://www.drdavidmcmillan.com/sense-of-community/sense-of-community-a-definition-and-theory) inform the distinction between motivation, experienced belonging and enacted practice. Neither establishes mastery, an employment right or a universal benefit of conformity.

Selected passages of Ortner and Stur's *The Project Management Office: Setup and Benefits*, 2nd ed., 2024, identify a broad function catalogue and changing office scope; that catalogue supplies “what,” not every professional “how.” Danaher's [DBS account](https://www.danaher.com/how-we-work/danaher-business-system) includes innovation as well as improvement. Team Topologies' enabling interaction, the Consortium for Service Innovation's [KCS transition](https://www.serviceinnovation.org/the-next-era-of-kcs/) and Microsoft's [AI organization readiness](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/organization-people-readiness-plan) and [Cloud CoE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/organize/cloud-center-of-excellence) inform the distinctions among facilitation, enduring knowledge/platform services and workload responsibility. Provider descriptions are not independent causal evidence. The accessible KCS guide labelled 2027 retains that label; access in October 2026 does not establish completion of a future release.

Microsoft's [Work IQ retrieve description](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/microsoft365dev/work-iq-retrieve-october-2026/), 20 October 2026, provides a concrete example of authorized workplace retrieval with citations and metadata; retrieval and delegated action remain different operations. Local availability, instructional competence and personnel authority are not established by the provider description. Park et al.'s [2026 supervision framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24234v1) draws on 19 developers' short sessions and 102 selected Reddit threads, including accounts of delegated supervision. The selected qualitative passages make the allocation and maintenance of supervision relevant; they establish neither prevalence nor comparative effectiveness in HR.

Shen and Tamkin's [2026 study of coding-skill formation](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills) distinguishes immediate learning from task output in 52 mostly junior Python users learning Trio. Its quiz result is not a capability ceiling for autonomous agents or a long-term learning estimate. These bounded lines support comparing agent-performed context recovery, substantive help and checking with human provision while retaining a separate acquisition question when human learning is actually selected. The actual capability, access and authority must still be established for the receiving use; no dated benchmark defines every future job.
