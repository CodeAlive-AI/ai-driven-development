## FIN.Reference:2 - Problem

A corporation can be profitable and unable to pay, buy a valuable business at a destructive price, hedge a currency while retaining collection risk, or improve one financial model while creating incompatible commitments elsewhere. The difficulty is often the connection between valid local calculations and the choice they are meant to support.

A usable financial answer connects those consequences to the arrangement that produces them. Practitioners construct accounts and values, develop financial terms, compare capital uses, design protection and group financing, and prepare recommendations. Treasury uses its methods to execute permitted actions and reconcile their effects. When an attractive action is unavailable, the work can return to the financial or operating construction that determines availability.
