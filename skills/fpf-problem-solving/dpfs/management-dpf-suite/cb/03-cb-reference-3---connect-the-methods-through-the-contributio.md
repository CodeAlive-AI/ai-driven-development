## CB.Reference:3 - Connect the methods through the contribution needed next

Start from the working question and use any relevant results already obtained elsewhere. The following connections explain common choices.

[CB.1](11-cb-1---choose-a-useful-cultural-or-community-contribution.md) compares possible contributions and makes a credible offer. When mutual help is selected, [CB.2](12-cb-2---arrange-the-first-useful-exchanges.md) arranges a finishable exchange and obtains the recipient's account of its use. If the central contribution repeatedly cannot be obtained, reconsider the offer before recruiting more people. If the selected route is independent learning or a qualified service, obtain those contributions directly.

[CB.3](13-cb-3---make-entry-and-changes-of-participation-workable.md) makes a next participation action understandable and feasible, including a reduction, return or departure. [CB.4](14-cb-4---design-a-space-and-shared-activity-for-its-purpose.md) connects a space and shared activity to its intended result. [CB.5](15-cb-5---obtain-and-return-useful-contributions-from-participa.md) develops the actual contribution: a case clinic, a demonstration, a project review, joint work or another fitting form. A discussion setting supplies opportunities for interaction; prepared material, capable participants and the recipient's next use make that interaction useful.

[CB.6](16-cb-6---recognise-a-contribution-and-choose-a-meaningful-resp.md) connects recognition to the contribution and the person's circumstances. [CB.7](17-cb-7---prepare-and-sustain-distributed-organising.md) obtains capable, supported organising work, including temporary participant groups where shared attention is needed, and an absence arrangement where continuity matters. [CB.8](18-cb-8---make-participation-rules-usable-and-resolve-incidents.md) makes expectations usable and carries incidents through judgement, response and correction. These methods address different problems: praise does not pay a promised fee, a role title does not prepare an organiser, and a welcoming tone does not settle a contested incident.

[CB.9](19-cb-9---provide-for-community-work-and-agree-its-terms.md) connects a promise to qualified time, access, funds and agreed terms. [CB.10](20-cb-10---understand-the-value-of-participation-and-cultural-w.md) examines particular uses and outcomes, including burdens, failures and unrealised potential, and returns a useful change. [CB.11](21-cb-11---continue-change-transfer-or-end-a-community-arrangem.md) uses those results to compare continuation, reduction, transfer, separation, pause and ending. An agreed transfer becomes effective when the receiver can perform the work and the former arrangement's obligations are settled.

[CB.12](22-cb-12---support-a-useful-repeated-action-of-participation.md) supports a wanted repeated action through a fitting occasion, feasible action and useful return. [CB.13](23-cb-13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions.md) establishes a mandate where common decisions need one. Repetition is not the purpose of every participation arrangement; shared decision rights are not needed for every independent cultural use.

[CB.14](24-cb-14---develop-and-maintain-a-shared-repertoire-of-practice.md) develops a repertoire that practitioners can use, question and maintain. It can select simple documentation, reciprocal comparison or a properly provisioned development project. [CB.15](25-cb-15---connect-practices-through-a-useful-joint-contributio.md) connects practices through a useful joint contribution, keeping differences in meaning and working conditions visible. [CB.16](26-cb-16---form-and-carry-a-collective-voice-into-a-decision.md) carries a collective contribution into another party's decision with a position, mandate and returned answer. A useful idea can move between people without a joint method-development programme; an individual expert can speak without claiming to represent everyone.

When combining these contributions, establish their joint feasibility. An expert may be unable to advise on a case and welcome newcomers during the same hour; one room may not support two activities at the required time. Obtain another contribution, change timing or reduce the promise. An appeal concerning that expert's own response needs another reviewer; rescheduling alone cannot supply independence. Each local method may be sensible while the combination is impossible.
