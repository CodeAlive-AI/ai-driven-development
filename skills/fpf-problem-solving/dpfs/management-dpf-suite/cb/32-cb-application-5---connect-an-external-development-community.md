## CB.Application:5 - Connect an external development community with independent workplaces

A voluntary community brings together practitioners who want to become better at noticing limitations, constructing worthwhile problems and developing usable ways. Some are employees, some independent professionals. The community has capable development contributors but does not claim mastery of every participant's profession. It offers a shared repertoire and a bounded case exchange; a separate paid service can promise individual support under its own terms.

Use [CB.1](11-cb-1---choose-a-useful-cultural-or-community-contribution.md) to ask what each route should supply. A participant with a settled question may need only the existing explanation. Another wants a qualified specialist answer that the peer exchange cannot promise. A third wants help forming a next problem after a successful transition. Direct them to the contribution they need, including an external subject provider where necessary. Membership is not a condition for using a public resource.

For a first shared undertaking, two participants offer permitted contrasting cases about obtaining and interpreting expert advice. Use [CB.15](25-cb-15---connect-practices-through-a-useful-joint-contributio.md) to agree the joint work and useful return to each practice. Through [CB.13](23-cb-13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions.md), the participants give a small organising group a mandate for this activity and its shared resource; that mandate does not let the group commit their employers or speak for all practitioners. Use [CB.9](19-cb-9---provide-for-community-work-and-agree-its-terms.md) to obtain the contributors' time for preparation, participation and follow-up. A promised response that exceeds willing capacity must be narrowed or separately provided.

During the exchange, one participant reconstructs why a specialist's conclusion applied to one configuration and not another. A second participant needs further subject knowledge. The community returns that bounded question to a competent provider. Another participant understands the distinction but needs practice applying it under pressure; the human-development contribution through [HCD.6](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/22-hcd-6---design-representative-practice-tasks.md) and [HCD.9](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/26-hcd-9---perform-deliberate-practice-with-feedback.md) remains separate from the intellectual answer.

Use [CB.14](24-cb-14---develop-and-maintain-a-shared-repertoire-of-practice.md) to develop a reusable explanation from the cases, preserving the consequential differences and permissions. A qualified AI contribution can help recover sources or construct examples, with the required subject judgement and checks supplied by the actual arrangement. A further practitioner uses the explanation in a different permitted case, following [C.36.MC's reconstruction and adaptation (FPF C.36.MC:4.5)](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/130-c-36-mc---qualify-and-develop-a-method-culture.md#c36mc45---make-a-practice-recoverable-and-changeable-by-its-recipients). Observe which operation that practitioner performs and which help remains necessary. That result can support a bounded reconstruction or use claim; publication and attendance alone cannot.

After this useful result, the community notices a harder problem: can another organiser help participants form worthwhile questions without the founder choosing each one? [FPF C.40.NP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/119-c-40-np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems.md) supplies the continuation. Use [CB.7](17-cb-7---prepare-and-sustain-distributed-organising.md) to prepare a willing successor around an actual episode. When the successor needs to develop an ability, use the learning contribution through [HCD.6](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/22-hcd-6---design-representative-practice-tasks.md) and [HCD.9](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/26-hcd-9---perform-deliberate-practice-with-feedback.md); the community must also obtain the required time, access and qualified help with unresolved subject questions. The new organiser's performance, the participants' development and the repertoire contribution remain different results.

An employer may separately invite the community to support development at its workplaces. The agreement must connect the community's contribution with the employer's actual work opportunities and decision rights through [OCE.18](../oce/34-oce-18---arrange-capability-development-and-receiving-work-i.md) and [LDR.2](../ldr/09-ldr-2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions.md). Funding alone does not supply permission to use confidential learning material in identifiable personnel reports. Stop the activity that depends on missing permission; independently useful public guidance can remain available.

The community can remain a specialised development provider with outside professional suppliers. It can also obtain shared administration or learning infrastructure from a common service. If nobody can maintain the promised repertoire or response service, use the [continuation and withdrawal question in CB.11](21-cb-11---continue-change-transfer-or-end-a-community-arrangem.md) to obtain a successor, reduce the promise or end it while settling accepted commitments.
