## MKT.Reference:4 - Composition and performance - Preserve the conditions of the whole

Some connections use an earlier result: qualification informs an offer, and evidence from a performed trial informs a later choice. Others are simultaneous. During a customer meeting, a facilitator may be eliciting a useful result, helping the person understand an offer and performing part of a larger programme. The same worker may also be responsible for an existing service. Those descriptions identify different contributions and constraints of the work occurring then. Account for the shared worker’s time against the whole programme.

For a proposed combination, name one intended result, the included opportunities, the period and the permitted conditions. Identify the preparation, capability, assignments, access, performance, support, coordination, updating and exception handling that the result would require, retaining any unresolved supply conditions. Count common work once across the cases that share it and local work for each case that needs it. Preserve different resource kinds and time windows. Compare complete ways of producing a comparable result, including a smaller or later programme where the original promise cannot be supported. If implementation is selected, obtain the effective arrangements needed before dependent work begins.

An offer gives customers particular choices. It can promise one delivery on a known order, service to whichever customer presents a single entitlement, or service to every customer who can independently call on it. Establish what customers can choose, which combinations of their choices are allowed, and what those allocating the work will know before they must commit. One shared session can serve several requests if it meets each undertaking. Separate sessions compete for the same person's time. Use a schedule for known orders or a rule that can meet the permitted calls in time. An unresolved commitment can change the terms of the offer before it is published.

Apply this whole-combination condition to the applications below. An apparently cheaper message can increase the number of unsupported responses. A common demonstration can save preparation while increasing coordination. An AI-generated explanation can reduce drafting time while leaving a subject judgement, an individual learning action or a timely human check unsupplied. Inspect those differences before replacing one constituent in a larger arrangement. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) develops the method and performance comparisons; engineering, organisation and operations supply the actual arrangements.

[MKT.13](28-mkt-13---test-commercial-model-repeatability-and-deployment.md)’s four-trial calculation and [MKT.14](29-mkt-14---develop-and-change-a-programme-for-selected-custome.md)’s programme comparison concern different constructed situations. The latter serves four opportunities involving three organisations. A department can form part of an organisation. An opportunity relates participants to work they may undertake together. To establish the composition of the programme, identify the results the customers can accept separately or only together and the work the provider must arrange to supply them.
