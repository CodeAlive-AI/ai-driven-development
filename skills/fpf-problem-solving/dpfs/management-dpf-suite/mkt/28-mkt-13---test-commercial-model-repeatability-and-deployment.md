## MKT.13 - Test Commercial Model Repeatability and Deployment Limits

> **Type:** Marketing Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### MKT.13:1 - Problem frame

Use this pattern when deciding which part of a commercial way of working can be repeated or expanded, and which part still needs discovery, repair or a bounded trial. Typical situations include a successful founder-led sale, a pilot dependent on unusual help, a new channel with uncertain purchasing behaviour, or a proposed expansion whose receipts arrive after the next acquisition payments.

The result is a bounded deployment decision: what can be offered, to whom, through which means and at what volume under supported delivery and economic conditions. It also identifies the significant unresolved premise and the work needed to settle it, change the model or stop.

The intended reader can describe the customer contribution and coordinate customer inquiry, delivery and economic analysis. An established arrangement continuing within unchanged supported limits need not repeat discovery. A small viable business need not seek rapid growth; repeatability is useful whenever recurring promises depend on ordinary provision, including deliberately limited volume.

### MKT.13:2 - Problem

A prototype, enthusiastic interview or exceptional sale may establish something useful while leaving most of the commercial arrangement untested. The founder may supply reputation, custom work or access that ordinary staff cannot reproduce. Increasing promotion can then multiply unresolved delivery problems rather than a viable service.

Treat the model as connected claims about obtaining customers, supplying a useful result and sustaining that supply. Examine the consequential uncertain claim under conditions relevant to the intended deployment. Use the resulting evidence to retain, change or bound the arrangement, preserving ordinary work and existing commitments during the search.

### MKT.13:3 - Forces

| Tension | What the decision must preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Early work needs personal initiative and adaptation. | The difference between useful discovery and the conditions of ordinary repeated provision. |
| Evidence is uneven across customers, channels and model components. | The scope of each supported claim instead of one overall label of validation. |
| A larger deployment can reveal problems but also multiply obligations. | A useful bounded increment with a way to stop or recover. |
| Search and current service need different decisions and support. | A workable organisation of both, including their shared scarce contributors. |
| Future margin can be positive while present cash is insufficient. | Timing, capacity, continuing obligations and actual funding. |

### MKT.13:4 - Solution

#### MKT.13:4.1 - Recover the whole commercial proposition and the intended deployment

Explain the proposed customer contribution, actual alternatives, audience, way of reaching and acquiring customers, customer work, provision, support and exchange. [MKT.1](16-mkt-1---qualify-a-market-problem-and-differences-between-cus.md) and [MKT.2](17-mkt-2---construct-and-test-an-offer-and-its-positioning.md) supply the qualified problem and offer. Include partners, specialised work and later obligations when they are needed for the result.

State the deployment decision: repeat the present service next month, employ another team, enter another segment, use a new channel, or increase volume. Define the proposed period and service conditions. Without these, a model can be called repeatable while every repetition changes the essential offer or consumes exceptional help.

Distinguish the claims and their present support. A working artifact may support technical feasibility; a customer conversation may clarify a problem; an actual choice may support willingness to obtain a specified contribution on particular terms. None alone establishes the full combination of acquisition, delivery, benefit and sustained economics. Keep uncertainties connected to their consequence rather than treating every box in a business-model diagram as equally urgent.

Use [Relate Capabilities, Dependencies, Business Models and Initiatives (STR.8)](../str/26-str-8---relate-capabilities-dependencies-business-models-and.md) to explain how the contribution would be obtained and sustained. Its result exposes a missing method, capability, access, arrangement or funding condition at the right subject. A market problem need not be repaired with a product feature, nor an access gap with another interview.

For a distribution arrangement, recover the exchanges and observations separately. A merchant's order can support an upstream purchasing claim while leaving downstream acquisition and use unknown. Include stock or reserved capacity, replenishment, returns, partner support and their cash timing where they determine repetition. Use MKT.11's actual arrangement to explain these dependencies. A growing upstream count can coexist with unsold stock or unresolved access, so choose the evidence needed for this deployment instead of treating all channel activity as final demand.

#### MKT.13:4.2 - Select the uncertainty that can change the next commitment

Ask which unsupported claim could defeat the proposed deployment and what alternative explanation fits the evidence. Customers may like the demonstration but lack a usable purchase route. The channel may reach interested users but no payer. A price may cover visible execution while omitting preparation and support. A pilot may work only because the founder silently completes customers' work.

Choose the next inquiry or trial by its ability to distinguish the consequential possibilities at reasonable cost. State what would make the proposed commitment acceptable, smaller, different or unavailable. A fixed number of interviews, a minimum conversion percentage or a prototype milestone is useful only when justified for this decision.

If the issue is the meaning of the customer's situation, use [MKT.12](27-mkt-12---investigate-a-customer-or-market-question-for-a-dec.md). If it is a concrete choice and transition, use MKT.4 with real conditions. If the decision needs an intervention effect, use [MKT.10](25-mkt-10---evaluate-the-contribution-and-cost-of-a-marketing-i.md). If the missing contribution is the service itself, request the relevant engineering result through MKT.9 and MKT.11. Keep these different questions distinct even when one trial informs several of them.

Use the smallest adequate means of inquiry. A sample output, terms of a limited service or a manual trial may answer a purchasing or use question more directly than a finished product. Conversely, a simulation that conceals the crucial operational difficulty may be inadequate. The artifact serves the question; its availability does not establish that the question has been answered.

#### MKT.13:4.3 - Make search possible alongside ordinary work

Arrange the trial as real work. Obtain the needed people, time, inputs, permitted access, technical support and authority to change the proposal. Explain what current service must continue and how failures or premature commitments will be handled. Use [Coordinate Change Work with Continuing Service (OCE.11)](../oce/33-oce-11---coordinate-organization-change-work-with-continuing.md) to reconcile the whole burden, including learning, supervision, temporary duplication, exception handling and the eventual handover.

Compare a few feasible ways to organise the search: a bounded effort within the current team, a separately supported small team, or a qualified external contributor. Compare how each arrangement would answer the selected question and obtain the same useful customer result. Also compare effects on continuing work, access to scarce specialists and freedom to revise assumptions; identify who will receive the result. Separation is useful when it resolves a real conflict; it is not an automatic remedy for uncertainty.

Give the responsible people a legitimate way to return disconfirming evidence and change or stop the proposal. A team measured only by immediate sales may conceal evidence that the offer is unsuitable. A search team rewarded only for prototypes may leave acquisition and ordinary delivery unexplored. Select expectations appropriate to the actual question without exempting the work from cost, commitments or accountable decisions.

Name the receiving work before the trial finishes. The operating team needs more than a favourable presentation: it needs a usable offer, performance conditions, remaining exceptions, training or access, and the support obligations it would inherit. The search can produce a useful negative answer or a smaller complete service; neither should be converted into a promise of the original scale.

#### MKT.13:4.4 - Observe a complete attempt under relevant conditions

Follow a real attempt far enough to answer the selected question. For an ordinary provision claim, follow suitable customer selection, contact, choice, agreed terms, inputs, performance, use and support. Preserve where the work stops and why. An inquiry about purchase alone need not wait for years of later use, but its conclusion must remain about purchase.

Record material assistance and deviations. Identify whether the founder supplied a personal introduction, unusual discount, manual repair, omitted invoice or unplanned night work. These can be legitimate ways to learn; their cost and availability must enter the proposed repeatable arrangement if it still depends on them. Remove the dependency before claiming ordinary provision without it.

Compare attempts across the differences relevant to deployment. Repetition by the same expert for similar friendly customers may leave another team or channel untested. Select cases that can expose a consequential variation rather than merely accumulating successes. Preserve refusals and ineligible cases; distinguish whether an opportunity was eligible from whether an attempt succeeded.

Return the observed result and its limit. Distinguish “this attempt succeeded”, “the ordinary team repeated this bounded service under these conditions”, and “the proposed larger deployment is supported”. A few successful attempts can justify a limited next commitment under uncertainty; they need not establish a stable population rate or unlimited scale.

#### MKT.13:4.5 - Test whether the proposed repetition can be sustained

Join the customer result to acquisition, complete provision, continued use where relevant, and the means of funding the next period. Use [Account for Customer and Product Economics Over Time (MA.8)](../ma/_index.md) for the population, dated flows, future obligations and prospective alternatives. Retain expenses that the pilot concealed, including founder time valued on an appropriate basis and the cost of replacing exceptional assistance.

If continuation depends on a growing base, use STR.8's period account: define who is active, reconcile entries and exits, explain the source and delay of the next contributions, and locate the receipts that could fund them. Repeated visits are not new customers; referrals are not acquisitions until the relevant condition is met. A lifetime contribution forecast and a count of invitations do not finance next month's work.

Check shared capacity at the proposed volume. Preparation may be reusable while qualification, adaptation, support or exception handling still grows with each customer. Several individually feasible promises can jointly exceed a specialist's available hours. Compare retaining the current volume, narrowing the service, pacing entry, adding supported capacity or obtaining a contribution externally. A sufficient total of hours does not resolve an impossible simultaneous assignment.

Separate an economically attractive model from a currently feasible commitment. Unavailable funds, ineffective access or an unprepared receiving team can limit deployment even when expected contribution is positive. Return the precise missing condition and allow independent useful work to continue under its existing limits.

#### MKT.13:4.6 - Decide what to deploy and what to learn next

Return one coherent decision: continue the bounded arrangement, expand a supported part, revise a consequential claim, perform another discriminating attempt, pause the dependent commitment or stop. Identify the customer and offer scope, channel, volume, support, economic conditions and receiving team that qualify the decision.

Keep the unresolved part separate without making it disappear. A file-based supported trial can be offered while continuous integration remains an engineering question, provided the offer makes the difference clear. A new segment reopens the affected assumptions; it does not erase evidence about the old one.

Arrange observation and response at the limits that matter. New exceptions, acquisition costs, delayed payment, support queues or changed customer work can invalidate the next repetition. Return to the responsible method with that change. Stable adequate provision can continue without performing the whole discovery sequence again.

### MKT.13:5 - Archetypal Grounding

#### MKT.13:5.1 - Three founder sales do not yet define the ordinary service

In this constructed case, a founder sells analysis trials to three acquaintances. All pay and receive useful reports. Each trial also consumes substantial undocumented night work by the founder to repair the input data. A proposal to advertise broadly treats the three sales as proof of a repeatable model.

The team reconstructs the result and discovers two consequential claims: suitable customers can choose the offer without the founder's personal relationship, and ordinary staff can deliver it from stated inputs within the available burden. It narrows the trial to a supported batch-file analysis with explicit input conditions. Continuous integration remains outside this offer because no adequate service contribution has been obtained.

Six independently reached organisations are considered. Two can supply the permitted input and choose the limited trial; two need continuous integration; two cannot yet meet the input conditions. This is useful qualification, not a general conversion-rate estimate. MKT.2 keeps the offer narrow, MKT.4 makes its terms and transition usable, and MKT.11 obtains the analyst's actual allocation and support arrangement.

The ordinary analyst completed both eligible trials with the agreed support and no founder repair. Execution and customer support take four hours per trial against a six-hour planning allowance; separate qualification and preparation work is also recorded. These two completed trials do not establish that every new data source, operator or volume will behave likewise.

For the next month, the analyst's supported envelope is 48 hours. Current commitments consume 14 hours, shared preparation and qualification for the proposed trials require six, and four hours remain protected for unexpected current-service support. Each trial retains the six-hour planning allowance for execution and ordinary trial support; the two observed four-hour executions do not replace that allowance.

| Demand on the same analyst in the next month | Four trials | Five trials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current commitments | 14 hours | 14 hours |
| Shared preparation and qualification | 6 hours | 6 hours |
| Protected current-service support | 4 hours | 4 hours |
| Trial execution and ordinary trial support | 4 × 6 = 24 hours | 5 × 6 = 30 hours |
| Total against the 48-hour envelope | 48 hours | 54 hours |

Four trials fit this local burden comparison; a fifth exceeds the envelope by six hours. In this constructed case, the other contributors, calendar compatibility and cash conditions have separately adequate supply answers. Their unlike resources are not added to the analyst's hours. The responsible decision maker therefore permits at most four compatible trials with explicit review of exceptions. The calculation supports that bounded commitment, not a statistically proven population rate or scalable demand.

If compulsory trial preparation increases by three hours, four trials would require 51 hours. Three would require 45 while preserving the protected four hours. The team must reduce or reschedule the new work, or obtain additional supported capacity, before promising the fourth trial. Broad promotion waits because it could create unsupported integration expectations and exceed the receiving team's capacity.

When a new customer needs a different input repair, the team does not restore hidden founder work. It either obtains that service contribution and its cost, changes the offer, or declines the incompatible trial. The original batch-file result remains valid within its conditions.

#### MKT.13:5.2 - Positive future contribution arrives too late

For an independent constructed expansion, acquiring and beginning service for each of 100 customers requires 12 monetary units now. A qualified forecast gives 15 units per customer of receipts net of later provision and support payments, collected after 60 days. The first group has separate funding. A proposed second group would begin after 30 days using only the first group's receipts, with no other available cash after existing obligations.

The prospective three-unit contribution per customer does not fund the second group at day 30. The team compares a later start, a feasible change in payment terms and separately obtained funding. Each alternative must preserve the offer, customer feasibility and current obligations. Until one is supported, deployment stays within the first group's funded limit. Another enthusiastic interview cannot settle this timing gap.

#### MKT.13:5.3 - A ready artifact changes what remains to be learned

An AI-assisted team can produce an impressive prototype immediately. That artifact may be useful for explaining an offer, but its existence does not establish customer need, purchasing feasibility, the team's ability to operate the service or its economics. The team returns to the consequential untested claim and chooses an appropriate inquiry or trial. It keeps the artifact where useful and changes the means of learning when the artifact no longer discriminates the relevant possibilities.

### MKT.13:6 - Bias-Annotation

Founder reputation, selective pilot recruitment and hidden exceptional effort favour early success. Growth incentives can treat a viable small arrangement as failure, while fear of uncertainty can prevent a justified limited deployment. Preserve the actual purpose, disconfirming attempts and the distinction between evidence of a result and willingness to take a bounded risk.

### MKT.13:7 - Conformance Checklist

- The proposed repetition names the customer contribution, audience, acquisition means, provision, period and volume.
- The next inquiry addresses a significant uncertainty whose answer can change a commitment.
- Search has adequate support and preserves continuing work and existing promises.
- Observed attempts retain material assistance, refusals, exceptions and their actual evidential scope.
- The sustaining account includes complete burden, shared capacity, dated flows and remaining obligations.
- The decision states what may proceed, what remains unresolved and which change requires reconsideration.

### MKT.13:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

| Anti-pattern | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Treating a finished prototype as validation of the whole business. | Recover the customer, acquisition, provision and sustaining claims that remain untested. |
| Counting founder heroics as ordinary delivery. | Make the exceptional contribution explicit and obtain a repeatable provision condition. |
| Launching more promotion to solve a missing service. | Construct or bound the service before multiplying its promises. |
| Separating a search team without support or a receiver. | Compare organisational alternatives against the actual inquiry, current work and handover. |
| Financing next month with receipts due later. | Place flows at their dates and obtain a feasible timing or funding arrangement. |

### MKT.13:9 - Consequences

Deployment can advance in useful supported portions while unresolved assumptions remain visible. The method can prevent spending on acquisition that multiplies an unworkable promise and can protect a viable limited business from an unjustified growth requirement. It also requires honest accounting of assistance, unsuccessful attempts and the work of transferring a result to ordinary provision.

### MKT.13:10 - Architectural Rationale

Repeatability concerns a connected commercial arrangement. Customer inquiry, offer construction, service engineering, organisation and financial accounting retain their distinct results. MKT.13 joins them for a deployment decision and detects when a change invalidates that decision. It does not replace their specialist constructions or impose a universal sequence on every organisation.

### MKT.13:11 - SoTA-Echoing

For search before expansion, **adapt** the historical [Blank and Dorf Customer Development Manifesto](https://growthorientedsustainableentrepreneurship.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/en-customer-development-manifesto.pdf): a consequential business-model assumption needs discriminating evidence, and ordinary repeated acquisition and provision differ from an exceptional sale. The present method bounds deployment without adopting a universal growth requirement or fixed trial quota.

For changed learning conditions, **adapt** [Blank's 2026 account of AI-generated initial products](https://steveblank.com/2026/09/25/ai-killed-the-mvp-long-live-the-iup/): easy artifact production weakens the inference from an artifact to prior learning or competence. Retain the substantive question and choose a means that can answer it. Renaming the artifact alone supplies no new method.

STR.8, MA.8 and OCE.11 supply developed sustaining, economic and organisational comparisons. Use them where the corresponding condition can change deployment; an adequate existing professional answer can be reused within its scope.

### MKT.13:12 - Relations

MKT.1/2/3/4/6/7/9/11 supply the commercial proposition and its actual continuation. MKT.12 resolves a focused customer or market uncertainty; MKT.10 supplies a bounded intervention evaluation. MKT.14 may organise repeated work with named customer organisations while using this method to qualify a proposed expansion.

Strategy selects the wider direction and commitment; service and method engineering construct missing provision; organisation and operations obtain and perform the work; management accounting qualifies the temporal economics. A new customer promise requires their actual contributions where the model depends on them.
