## Management.Reference:2 - Constituent fields and their contributions

The Suite connects the following fourteen DPFs. Each develops knowledge and reusable practices for a particular management subject. The entry patterns below show how their results participate in the larger work; they are selected entries, not a required sequence.

| Constituent field | Its own question and useful entry | Connection to the management whole |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Strategy (STR)](../str/_index.md) — STR | Which contribution and direction are worth pursuing, and which commitments remain defensible? [STR.8](../str/26-str-8---relate-capabilities-dependencies-business-models-and.md), [STR.11](../str/29-str-11---select-strategy-portfolio-commitments-and-assumptio.md) and [STR.16](../str/34-str-16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branc.md) connect capability, choices and a feasible portfolio. | Direction selects the work worth supporting. Operating, financial and participation conditions can defeat an attractive portfolio or change its scope. |
| [Goal Construction and Regulation (GOALS)](../goals/_index.md) — GOALS | Which goals are worth pursuing, what would establish achievement, and how can their pursuit remain feasible and revisable? [GOALS.1–4](../goals/09-goals-1---choose-the-goal-and-the-kind-of-reliance-it-create.md) connect the common construction; [GOALS.5](../goals/13-goals-5---organize-focused-joint-work-with-a-complete-okr-pr.md) supplies full OKR/CFR, with direct human and AI applications in GOALS.6–7. | Desired results and their evidence constrain work and reliance; strategic, operating, financial and participation conditions constrain the goal arrangement and return changes to it. |
| [Operations Management (OPS)](../ops/_index.md) — OPS | How can the needed production or service be arranged, provided and revised, and what can ongoing work admit, continue and deliver? [OPS.Reference:1](../ops/02-ops-reference-1---start-from-a-difficulty-in-continuing-oper.md) explains the field and [OPS.11.1](../ops/44-ops-11-1---construct-and-reconcile-operating-models-across-s.md) develops the operating construction. [OPS.10](../ops/40-ops-10---qualify-operating-capacity-under-variability.md), [OPS.13](../ops/46-ops-13---align-commitments-resources-and-service-outcomes.md) and [OPS.22](../ops/56-ops-22---interpret-operating-performance-and-decide-the-resp.md) qualify capability, agree service and interpret performance. | Its capacity and service results constrain promises; actual delivery and unresolved obligations return to the responsible management decision. |
| [Organization Administration (ADM)](../adm/_index.md) — ADM | Which institutional condition must a participant actually receive, and under which rules? [ADM.8](../adm/18-adm-8---establish-and-exercise-the-required-permission-for-a.md), [ADM.9](../adm/19-adm-9---provide-a-usable-administrative-result.md) and [ADM.11](../adm/21-adm-11---join-providers-around-the-administrative-result.md) connect permission, usable provision and providers. | A selected undertaking needs effective access, payments and other enabling services at the required time. A record of provision can be insufficient for the participant's work. |
| [Corporate Governance (CGOV)](../cgov/_index.md) — CGOV | Which corporate powers, rights and duties govern the act? [CGOV.3](../cgov/13-cgov-3---establish-authority-for-corporate-appointments-remo.md), [CGOV.11](../cgov/21-cgov-11---make-and-record-a-corporate-decision.md) and [CGOV.13](../cgov/23-cgov-13---monitor-corporate-performance-and-require-an-accou.md) connect authority, decision and accountable response. | Corporate undertakings need acts made by eligible participants under applicable rules. This is the corporate branch; a voluntary community uses its own mandate. |
| [Management Accounting (MA)](../ma/_index.md) — MA | How do activity, resources and money relate, and what arrangement could meet the economic requirement? [MA.1](../ma/08-ma-1---build-the-resource-consumption-and-cost-model.md), [MA.4](../ma/11-ma-4---reconcile-operating-reporting-and-cash-accounts.md) and [MA.10](../ma/17-ma-10---develop-an-activity-design-from-an-economic-requirem.md) connect resource and monetary accounts to activity design; [MA.9](../ma/16-ma-9---design-and-examine-the-use-of-management-accounting-i.md) designs and examines the use of information. | These relationships explain and constrain operating and financial choices. An unmet economic requirement can call for different offers, products, processes or provision; information use can alter what participants choose and report. |
| [Corporate Finance (FIN)](../fin/_index.md) — FIN | How do the activity's financial relations constrain value and payment capacity, and which investment, financing or group arrangement should change? [FIN.Reference](../fin/01-fin-reference-1---problem-frame.md) explains the subject; [FIN.2](../fin/15-fin-2---assess-liquidity-and-funding-needs-by-date.md) supplies dated cash; [FIN.9](../fin/22-fin-9---compare-capital-investments-and-allocations.md) compares capital uses; [FIN.10](../fin/23-fin-10---design-financing-instruments-and-terms.md) constructs financing terms; [FIN.23](../fin/28-fin-23---construct-financing-arrangements-across-group-entit.md) connects financing across entities; [FIN.16](../fin/32-fin-16---prepare-a-finance-recommendation-and-return-it-for.md) makes the resulting recommendation usable. | Value and funding conditions constrain corporate commitments. The same money, dates and outstanding obligations must survive the joins to strategy and operations. |
| [Marketing (MKT)](../mkt/_index.md) — MKT | Which customer result and offer are useful, obtainable and commercially continuable? [MKT.Reference](../mkt/01-mkt-reference-1---problem-frame---useful-offers-in-actual-cu.md) explains the field; [MKT.1](../mkt/16-mkt-1---qualify-a-market-problem-and-differences-between-cus.md) obtains customer differences; [MKT.11](../mkt/26-mkt-11---align-promises-partners-and-execution.md) constructs supported promises and distribution arrangements; [MKT.14](../mkt/29-mkt-14---develop-and-change-a-programme-for-selected-custome.md) connects customer programmes. | Customer evidence can change direction; provider capability and terms limit the offer; actual customer use can reopen the promise or commercial model. |
| [Customization and Productization Management (CUST)](../cust/_index.md) — CUST | Which client differences fit an existing offering, need a local answer or a changed family, or should be declined? CUST.1 connects client work, repeatable offerings and continuing obligations; CUST.3 constructs an offering family and CUST.8 changes it while existing obligations continue. | Local application can change the general offering, its permitted variations and its provision. Those changes constrain promises, operating capacity and the burden of maintaining the offering. |
| [Engineering Asset Management (EAM)](../eam/_index.md) — EAM | What value should be obtained through engineered assets over time? [EAM.10](../eam/19-eam-10---compare-engineered-asset-portfolio-combinations.md), [EAM.11](../eam/20-eam-11---time-asset-interventions-and-manage-dependencies.md) and [EAM.12](../eam/21-eam-12---integrate-specialist-results-for-an-asset-recommend.md) connect portfolio choices, intervention timing and qualified specialist results. | Asset options compete for funding and operational availability. Service need and technical evidence constrain the management choice; engineering and maintenance supply their own results. |
| [Leadership (LDR)](../ldr/_index.md) — LDR | How can people direct their own action, develop joint direction and participation, and obtain feasible contributions? [LDR.Reference:1.6](../ldr/01-ldr-reference-1---the-working-situation-and-useful-result.md#ldrreference16---direct-ones-own-contribution-and-development) explains the personal branch; [LDR.5](../ldr/11-ldr-5---develop-joint-direction-and-participation.md) develops direction and participation when these are not yet adequate. [LDR.1](../ldr/08-ldr-1---obtain-and-continue-the-leadership-contribution-need.md), [LDR.2](../ldr/09-ldr-2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions.md) and [LDR.4](../ldr/10-ldr-4---prepare-a-proposal-for-decision-and-first-use.md) connect capable help, feasible agreement and a proposal's passage to decision and first use. | A proposal becomes usable through actual contributions and legitimate decisions. A title or an attractive plan leaves that work unanswered. |
| [Community Management (CB)](../cb/_index.md) — CB | How can useful participation, relationships and common contributions be arranged and sustained? [CB.7](../cb/17-cb-7---prepare-and-sustain-distributed-organising.md), [CB.9](../cb/19-cb-9---provide-for-community-work-and-agree-its-terms.md) and [CB.13](../cb/23-cb-13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions.md) connect organising, provision and shared mandates. | Community purposes and voluntary terms constrain commitments and support. Participants' experience can require changes in the offer, burden, representation or continuation. |
| [Organization Change Engineering (OCE)](../oce/_index.md) — OCE | Which organizational relations and capability need deliberate construction or change? [OCE.3](../oce/25-oce-3---generate-and-compare-organization-concepts.md), [OCE.4](../oce/26-oce-4---design-an-organization-s-contribution-architecture.md) and [OCE.9](../oce/31-oce-9---realize-a-bounded-organization-capability-increment.md) connect concepts, contribution architecture and realization. | Specialization, information, decision rights, participation and support determine which commitments can be met. Their consequences can reopen the arrangement or the commitment. |
| [Human Resource Management (HRM)](../hrm/_index.md) — HRM | Which workforce arrangement and personnel decisions are warranted and obtainable? HRM.1 connects the configuration; HRM.2 assessment and selection; HRM.3 reward; HRM.4 voice and contested decisions; HRM.5 continuing participation and transitions. | Work and its terms shape one another. Selection, reward, protection and participant response constrain promises and organizational choices; effective acts and provision remain with their owners. |

Corporate Governance and Corporate Finance retain their corporate subject and institutional prerequisites. Other undertakings use the applicable financial and governing practices for their setting. Membership of the Suite does not make every constituent necessary in every case.

Community Management includes valued relationships, mutual help and enjoyable shared activity. Commercial return is only one possible concern. Independent people carrying a practice need not form a managed community or delegate any common authority. Use CB for an actual participation or common-work question; the wider cultural question belongs to [FPF C.36.RP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/129-c-36-rp---sustain-and-renew-shared-ways-of-working.md).
