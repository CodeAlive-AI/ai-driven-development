[MNT.2](../../engineering-dpf-suite/mnt/13-mnt-2---select-and-reopen-the-maintenance-policy.md), [MNT.7](../../engineering-dpf-suite/mnt/18-mnt-7---coordinate-maintenance-intervention-and-continuing-o.md) and [MNT.13](../../engineering-dpf-suite/mnt/24-mnt-13---coordinate-the-maintenance-programme-and-fleet-lear.md) connect policy, operation and programme learning. OCE's organizational construction is already a constituent described in section 3.1; a management act can also change the undertaking's current Work or resource conditions without requiring a new product or platform design. |

Management provides a receiving work need and feasible conditions when it relies on that capability. Assignment or attendance alone leaves the capability question open. A person supplying coaching and a manager arranging collective Work can have different direct subjects even when they support the same team. |

A management use receives the relevant result under its conditions; these fields also serve other purposes. |
| Obtain professional decision support | [Decision Design’s professional-support Methods (PSDA)](../../psda/_index.md) supply engagement, representation, protected participation and advice. The receiving management decision retains its authority and implementation duties. |

| Obtain help in applying a practice | [Implementation Facilitation (IS)](../../is/_index.md) supplies suitable assistance and its continuation or stop. Management can procure or allocate this help. Successful application alone establishes neither acquired capability nor open-ended development. |

Management can support such work while its development result remains separately judged. |

These rows identify useful supplies outside the constituent assembly described in section 2. Using another field's full Method, whether performed by the manager or by a specialist, retains the field's own subject and conditions. In particular, maintenance policy and programme work remain available without making the complete MNT field a Management constituent. OCE's organizational construction belongs to the constituent assembly for the subject reasons in section 3.1. The general construction of opportunities, composition of Methods and choice under constraints remains in FPF Core.

Management could participate in a broader discipline concerned with organising and changing collective activity, connecting its engineering contributions to wider system engineering and inquiry into social institutions and behaviour. It would contribute knowledge and practices for justified direction, legitimate commitments, provision and accountable continuation. It would receive qualified accounts of system capability and of the social conditions that affect those commitments. The broader assembly would need to connect a worthwhile collective result to both technically realizable and institutionally supportable action, and return failed premises to the corresponding practice. Management would retain its subject and the joint-supportability characteristic described above; engineering qualification and the grounds of social inquiry would remain with their respective disciplines. This is a possible assembly whose own field construction must be established before claiming that these disciplines are its parts.
