## Management.Reference:5 - Connected uses

### Management.Reference:5.1 - A voluntary practice-development network

Several independent groups want to develop and exchange a better diagnostic practice. Their useful result is a repertoire that practitioners can apply; their members also value mutual help. They are considering a shared development office, a small coordination service and separate provision by each group.

[STR.16](../str/34-str-16---construct-a-feasible-portfolio-with-shared-or-branc.md) compares those arrangements over the same period while preserving the distinct inquiry and practitioner uses. [CB.14](../cb/24-cb-14---develop-and-maintain-a-shared-repertoire-of-practice.md) organises the joint work on the repertoire; [RMP](../../rmp/_index.md) supplies a needed research result, and [FPF C.40.OD](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/117-c-40-od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development.md) supplies the larger development construction when continuing development is the purpose. Management must make the chosen contribution supportable without deciding its scientific conclusion by agreement.

In this constructed situation, a qualified reviewer has eight available hours in the relevant week. Each of two proposed activities requires five of those hours, including preparation and return. Each fits alone; together they require ten. [MA.2](../ma/09-ma-2---explain-the-cost-and-use-of-capacity.md) keeps the relevant capacity distinct from nominal availability, [CB.9](../cb/19-cb-9---provide-for-community-work-and-agree-its-terms.md) exposes the participants' other commitments and support terms, and [LDR.2](../ldr/09-ldr-2---agree-feasible-joint-contributions.md) constructs a feasible whole. A qualified external contribution, different timing or a smaller promise can resolve the shortage. Shared administration alone supplies none of the missing review hours.

[CB.13](../cb/23-cb-13---establish-a-workable-mandate-for-shared-decisions.md) establishes who may make the common allocation, where a mandate is missing. Each group retains the powers and commitments it has not delegated. [LDR.4](../ldr/10-ldr-4---prepare-a-proposal-for-decision-and-first-use.md) helps carry the chosen proposal into effective decisions and first use. Existing adequate access and arrangements can be retained; [ADM.9](../adm/19-adm-9---provide-a-usable-administrative-result.md) supplies a missing administrative condition. A no-office arrangement can be sufficient. Help from a qualified person or AI can supply a contribution without requiring every participant to learn its Method; consent, information permissions and the actual means to rely on that help remain conditions.

If a volunteer withdraws an offered contribution, the management question is which promises depend on it and what can now be agreed. The available research findings remain available within their grounds. [CB.11](../cb/21-cb-11---continue-change-transfer-or-end-a-community-arrangem.md) can support reduction, transfer or ending if no feasible continuation remains. This preserves voluntary participation while making the common undertaking dependable within its stated limits.

### Management.Reference:5.2 - A new order in an operating business

The constructed order in [MA.1](../ma/08-ma-1---build-the-resource-consumption-and-cost-model.md) requires 26–29 rig-hours for 100 accepted units. Twenty usable hours and a purchasable ten-hour block supply enough capacity under the case's conditions. The block can be booked for 240 currency units; other additional payments are 300. [OPS.14](../ops/47-ops-14---relate-throughput-cash-and-operating-consequences.md) compares the 1,200 receipt with 540 of additional payments, giving 660 under the stated absence of displaced work.

That positive difference leaves the dated funding question open. Without the order, the associated example supplies 500 of unrestricted cash through day 28 after all other receipts and obligations. The order requires 440 now, leaving 60; another 100 is due on day 7, while its receipt arrives on day 28. The day-7 funding gap is therefore 40. [FIN.2](../fin/15-fin-2---assess-liquidity-and-funding-needs-by-date.md) is the entry when that corporate liquidity question needs further construction. The responsible participants can obtain funding, agree changed payment terms, revise timing or refuse the proposed order; the favorable total does not select the response.

[MKT.11](../mkt/26-mkt-11---align-promises-partners-and-execution.md) makes the offered delivery conditions match the supported arrangement, and [OPS.13](../ops/46-ops-13---align-commitments-resources-and-service-outcomes.md) preserves the obligations already made to other customers. [ADM.8](../adm/18-adm-8---establish-and-exercise-the-required-permission-for-a.md) and [ADM.9](../adm/19-adm-9---provide-a-usable-administrative-result.md) obtain any missing permission and usable provision. Use [CGOV.3](../cgov/13-cgov-3---establish-authority-for-corporate-appointments-remo.md) and [CGOV.11](../cgov/21-cgov-11---make-and-record-a-corporate-decision.md) if the proposed act exceeds an existing delegation or requires a corporate decision. Ordinary work within an adequate delegation can continue directly.

If the choice instead turns on keeping, leasing or replacing the rig, [EAM.10](../eam/19-eam-10---compare-engineered-asset-portfolio-combinations.md) and [EAM.12](../eam/21-eam-12---integrate-specialist-results-for-an-asset-recommend.md) add the asset comparison and its qualified technical inputs. Engineering supplies the needed design or functioning result. Management selects and supports the contribution and follows what it actually makes possible in the service. The calculation illustrates the joins; real capacity, terms, safety and authority require the evidence applicable to the actual order.

### Management.Reference:5.3 - An AI-assisted experimental research programme

A laboratory is considering AI-assisted research on an enzyme. It could seek a useful variant with a specified property, a better predictive model or an explanation of a mechanism. Those are different results: finding a good variant need not distinguish two explanations of why it works. RMP.2 constructs the inquiry appropriate to the intended claim; STR.7 asks whether obtaining that knowledge warrants the resources and delay. Management must preserve that distinction when agreeing the programme's result.

Suppose the immediate commitment is a bounded search for variants that retain a required activity after a specified treatment. The assay, admissible variation, uncertainty and independent confirmation must come from qualified research and engineering work. OCE.8 compares complete available arrangements: a scientist using existing tools, a specialist optimizer connected to laboratory automation, an LLM with scientific tools, and a mixed arrangement. It includes preparation, failed attempts, laboratory time, checking and interpretation. Adding AI to existing job descriptions is only one candidate. OCE.3 constructs other divisions of the work; LDR.2 obtains feasible contributions rather than assuming that an organization chart supplies them.

The distinction is supported by two different research lines. [Co-Scientist, Gottweis and colleagues, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10644-y), develops hypotheses through generation, criticism, ranking and revision, with scientists and separate experimental validation. A hypothesis's ranking is not experimental evidence for its truth. [SAMPLE, Rapp and colleagues, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44286-023-00002-4), instead demonstrates a specialized model–experiment loop for a bounded protein search. It models functionality as well as the property being optimized. These are candidates for different contributions, not two interchangeable demonstrations that an autonomous laboratory is generally superior.

Here is a constructed capacity example, not a report of either study. A week's provision permits twelve assay slots. The chosen protocol requires two control slots and reserves two for independent confirmation, leaving eight for exploratory trials. Raising candidate generation from twenty to eighty variants does not create another assay slot. MMP.16 supplies the comparison of obtainable observations and their value for the selected unknown; RMP.3 makes the chosen design executable. OPS.9 and OPS.18 relate that design to the shared bench and other commitments. The next useful commitment may be eight selected trials, a different experimental design, extra qualified capacity or a decision not to proceed. The counts alone do not select among them.

Different returns reopen different contributions. If a dispensing failure prevents one assay, RMP.4 preserves the failed execution and its conditions; the optimizer must not receive an invented zero-activity measurement. The bench arrangement is repaired or the run is repeated when warranted. If a valid assay falls below the activity threshold, it is evidence about that variant under those conditions, not by itself a refutation of a wider mechanism. If an attractive scalar score conflicts with the raw observations, RMP.6 and the measurement construction must resolve whether the observation is unusable, the transformation is inappropriate or the model needs revision. An unusual signal cannot be discarded merely because it disagrees with the current model.

That last distinction matters in autonomous search. [Harris and colleagues, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-024-01485-2), show how checking raw observations and their reduction to a scalar can change the next experiment selected by Bayesian optimization. Their synthetic and surrogate-data comparisons support a design alternative for oversight, not a demonstrated improvement of this laboratory. A qualified observer, its access to raw data and its consequences for the next proposal belong in the complete arrangement being compared.

The chosen workflow therefore connects hypothesis or candidate proposal, experiment selection, physically obtainable execution, interpretation of the actual return, model or claim revision, and separate confirmation. SYSE.47 constructs a tool-using procedure where automation is needed; SYSE.46 tests the complete configuration under representative conditions. A protocol that can call an instrument still needs the instrument's qualified behavior and the authority to use it. ADM.8 applies an adequate existing permission or obtains a missing one; it does not supply scientific validity. STR.7 and the responsible management decision then use the qualified research return to continue, change or stop the commitment.

This case explains why the Suite connects these management contributions and receives research, mathematical and engineering results without absorbing their disciplines. It does not provide an assay or a universal laboratory Method. A usable local arrangement still needs the complete supplying Methods, its domain models and explicit treatment of each consequential return. A successful variant, a better model and an explained mechanism remain separately warranted achievements.

### Management.Reference:5.4 - Personal and corporate development in a software company

A software company wants two teams to improve answers prepared for customer onboarding. The company wants usable answers sooner. Participating employees want transferable capability in diagnosing unfamiliar customer situations and constructing supported responses. HR wants development access and recognition to fit the company's staffing and career arrangements. An existing employee community shares difficult cases. This is a constructed application of sections 4.4–4.7, not a report of a client's results.

Initially, a central expert and AI produce acceptable answers while employees mostly copy them. The proposal calls this both automation and development. Managers expect unchanged delivery from fully occupied weeks, and community helpers prepare material in their own time. The customer can receive a useful answer, but the plan has not provided the work needed to acquire the promised human capability. Nor has it provided the further participation on which cultural reproduction depends.

Using C.40.OD and C.40.JD, the participants distinguish the new possibilities from the contributions needed to obtain them. The revised arrangement retains useful AI drafting and the expert's subject contribution. It selects a human contribution that matters to the employees' intended later work: recognizing when available evidence does not support a customer's situation, obtaining the missing basis and proposing a justified continuation. The company would use that contribution in the relevant work, while the employees can value it beyond this employer.

Subject specialists supply the diagnostic Method and acceptance criteria. HCD.2 connects the employees' preparation to the selected work and needed practice. The managers release time by revising named current commitments: in this case, one team postpones a lower-priority internal documentation task and the other reassigns a rota duty after obtaining the necessary decisions. They supply suitable work and retain the required subject checking before a customer answer is used. The community helper's preparation and feedback receive an explicit time allocation. These changes make the proposal different from adding a course to unchanged weeks.

Through LDR.2, participants consider the complete contribution, its burden and their own trajectories. HR obtains the necessary decisions about development access, recognition and possible role movement. The parties also establish how practice observations may be used. Feedback for learning does not silently become a sufficient basis for another employment decision. A missing professional or institutional premise narrows the dependent commitment until it is obtained.

Assume that the existing helper's Method, the participants' preparation and access are adequate for this bounded work. Each team needs two qualified helper-hours per week, including preparation and feedback. The helper has four such hours. With the agreed work and HR conditions, the existing organization can provide the arrangement without a new office.

Suppose an employee's first answer meets the customer criterion because the helper supplies the decisive diagnosis. HCD.9 preserves both the acceptable answer and the help that made it possible. Independent recognition remains unestablished. After feedback and practice, the employee recognizes and resolves the issue without a diagnostic cue from the helper or AI in another case from the familiar task family. The relevant observation supplies the earlier-performance basis needed for a transfer question.

A later customer's configuration is unfamiliar to that employee and makes the previously used source inadequate. The task retains the diagnostic contribution and permits ordinary source access. Suppose the employee notices the mismatch before a diagnostic cue, finds the relevant configuration evidence and justifies a bounded answer using the learned Method. The new source supplies facts about the configuration; the assessor has not supplied the diagnosis or a new Method. HCD.12 supports the contribution demonstrated under this particular change. It does not establish adaptability to every customer problem. If the employee instead required instruction in another Method, the appropriate conclusion would concern learning with that instruction.

After this success, the employee identifies a repeated source-access difficulty and proposes a small supported experiment to improve how the team obtains the material. C.40.NP connects the result to that attainable next problem. A colleague uses the shared account to reconstruct the reasoning and adapt it to another situation. CB.14 and C.36.RP connect those further uses to the shared repertoire and its improvement. These observations concern local continuation and cultural reproduction. Whether answers arrive sooner at acceptable whole cost requires comparison with the earlier arrangement; the observations above do not settle that claim.

Now the helper's available time falls from four to two hours per week. The two unchanged promises still require four. C.40.JD, STR.16 and LDR.2 return the shortage to provision and dependent commitments. Compare qualified external help, changed timing, preparing a local helper and reducing scope. Suppose an external helper can supply two hours under the same subject criteria and access conditions within the agreed budget, while a new local helper cannot be prepared for this week's need. Choose the external contribution for one team and retain the existing helper for the other. Revise the agreement and access arrangements while retaining both teams' promised results. If the external contribution is unavailable, timing or scope must change. The existing diagnostic Method and the earlier human performance remain supported.

A recurring wider shortage could warrant a common development group if its construction and continuing cost improve the obtainable arrangement. It would need capable helpers, preparation, subject access and the means to serve the teams. HR's responsibility to obtain suitable conditions also remains meaningful when the selected performance is external.

Consider a different change. A new AI arrangement can now adequately perform the operation selected for human acquisition. Reopen the choice of later work and personal purpose rather than automatically removing practice or retaining the old programme. The human capability may remain valuable in other conditions, or another action may become a better acquisition. In this variation, suppose the company no longer needs the selected human operation, the employee still values it for an independent professional trajectory, and no mutually acceptable work or support for it is available inside the company. They end that acquisition commitment as a joint undertaking. The company retains the adequately supplied service; the employee's separate development purpose and earlier mastery remain. Any resulting role change or exit needs the corresponding HR and institutional contribution before that act can be supported.

The original conflict therefore produces changed work and provision, and the later changes produce a different supplier or a different commitment.
