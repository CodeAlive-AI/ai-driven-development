# Management DPF Suite Architectural Reference

**DocID:** `Management` within **FPF Library**.

> First Suite edition.

This Suite explains how direction, commitments, resources and collective work fit together. Its methods can support the Library’s [canon for a shared human–AI culture of thinking and acting](../../../library/reference/02-library-reference-1---one-connected-language-several-kinds-o.md#libraryreference13---a-canon-for-shared-humanai-culture), oriented toward the open-ended development of people, AI agents and their joint practice. Management connects their different contributions and the conditions under which they can be made. The working arrangement supplies roles and authority; community participation, human employment and AI provision have different conditions. The repertoire becomes culture through its use, continuation and improvement in actual work.
