## CUST.5 - Run Field Learning and Return a Productization Candidate

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### CUST.5:1 - Problem frame

Use this method when work in a customer setting can resolve an uncertainty that matters to local delivery and to a possible shared contribution. It applies to a bounded probe and to repeated inquiries within a continuing field function.

Start with what the local customer and the shared-development recipient need to understand, test or decide. A customer site is not automatically a research setting, and a field engineer’s presence does not authorize every experiment or reuse.

Use a known method or a qualified finding when it fulfils the commission. If the cause remains uncertain, report what the evidence establishes about the particular delivery and what the agreed inquiry leaves open.

### CUST.5:2 - Problem

Local delivery produces rich experience but often returns only requests, anecdotes or a working artifact. Product development cannot recover which conditions caused the result or what can transfer. Conversely, a probe optimized for general learning can leave the local customer with an unfinished obligation. Specify what the customer and the product team each need from the work, and protect both uses.

### CUST.5:3 - Forces

| Tension | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Learning and delivery | The inquiry must not silently replace the promised customer result. |
| Fast adaptation and interpretable evidence | Changes made during the attempt need enough context to explain their effect. |
| Continuing field work and bounded inquiry | A permanent function can contain finite questions and stopping decisions. |
| Useful feedback and information restrictions | The product recipient needs a permitted, qualified contribution, not unrestricted access to customer material. |
| Local success and generalization | A successful intervention may still be nontransferable. |

### CUST.5:4 - Solution

#### CUST.5:4.1 - Establish the live question and protected work

Establish what each receiver is asking. Their questions can coincide, or each may need a different answer. “Can this private environment support the monthly service?” is local. “Which setup and support conditions can define a repeatable private-hosting profile?” is shared.

For a choice, identify the unresolved condition that could change the choice or its grounds. For an explanation, identify what would distinguish the possible causes of the observed result.

A customer and the family owner ask why a row is missing from the first delivery report. Both will keep the installed profile through Friday. The selection record from that delivery may show that the applicable policy excluded the row before conversion. That gives the customer a reason for the omission and the family owner an instance of the policy operating as intended. Alternatively, the converter may have received the eligible row and omitted it from its output despite the mapping that required it. That locates a loss in this conversion. The final report alone looks the same in both cases. A later trial can show what happens under its test conditions. Using it to explain the first delivery also requires grounds that connect the finding to that delivery.

Protect the operating baseline: current service, support commitments, access conditions and participants whose work the attempt could disrupt. For field work still needed, agree on resources, an observation window and when to stop. The answer may already be established, the permitted work may be exhausted or continuing may endanger current service. Revisit the bounds if the commission changes.

[IS.1 — Agree a Useful Implementation-Support Undertaking](../../is/13-is-1---agree-a-useful-implementation-support-undertaking.md) supplies an agreed implementation-support undertaking; [IS.2 — Find the Implementation Difficulty and Obtain the Next Useful Contribution](../../is/14-is-2---find-the-implementation-difficulty-and-obtain-the-nex.md) supplies inquiry into the difficulty and selection of the next useful contribution. Obtain those results where needed, then state how the proposed field action will serve both receivers.

#### CUST.5:4.2 - Construct a discriminating attempt

Compare the plausible explanations that give different answers to the receiving question. For a deployment failure, candidates might include missing access, inadequate preparation, an incompatible interface, an unsuitable family assumption or insufficient support. Choose observations or small changes that can distinguish them.

Use [MKT.12 — Investigate a Customer or Market Question for a Decision](../mkt/27-mkt-12---investigate-a-customer-or-market-question-for-a-dec.md) for a customer or market question and its evidence/permission conditions. Use the relevant engineering method for a technical trial. For a question about feasibility or cause, an applicable trace, simulation or analysis may already give the answer. When the assignment calls for a new performance or an independent judgement, carry out the specified work under the applicable conditions. Use earlier material for what it establishes and preserve the choices entrusted to the performer.

Preserve the conditions needed to interpret the result: relevant configuration, input meaning, performed actions, observed outcome, help supplied and significant deviations. Record enough to distinguish a product limitation from exceptional rescue. Do not accumulate personal or sensitive information merely because the setting makes it accessible.

The connected example in [Engineering.Reference:22 — How can a team organise search while keeping current service workable?](../../engineering-dpf-suite/suite-reference/24-engineering-reference-22---how-can-a-team-organise-search-wh.md) explains developing a new customer integration beside current service. Use its complete treatment when that is the live engineering situation; receive the bounded technical and operating result, then perform the additional local/shared interpretation below.

#### CUST.5:4.3 - Arrange people, decisions and permitted information use

Use the appointed or existing field team when it can do the commissioned work. If the choice is open, compare the current team, a bounded field team and an outside contributor against the same result and protected work. Include access, domain understanding, time away from existing delivery and the ability to return learning. A separate FDE unit is one possible arrangement.

Use the decision-bearing contributions in CUST.1:4.3. Field performers may propose a common change without having authority to admit it; the product holder may reject a shared form without cancelling a separately supported local service. An assurance contribution should have the independence required by the consequential claim.

Distinguish permission for local performance, observation, use of findings, disclosure and artifact transfer. State the next recipient and purpose before collecting or transmitting material. Where the product team cannot access underlying customer material, obtain an adequate permitted statement, independently obtained evidence or a qualified contribution. If that cannot be done, leave the shared inference open.

#### CUST.5:4.4 - Perform the attempt and keep deviations interpretable

Carry out the permitted work with the support and stop conditions established. When a deviation occurs, preserve what it changes in the question. If a specialist quietly performs the hard part, record that contribution; do not later describe the result as independent repetition.

Address urgent local failure through its proper operating response. Do not delay it for a convenient research report. Conversely, a repair that restores service need not answer the shared hypothesis. State the narrower result when that is all the evidence supports.

If successive improvisations change the object of the trial, re-establish the question and conditions. Otherwise even a successful result may tell the reader little about the original question.

#### CUST.5:4.5 - Produce two qualified answers

For the local receiver, return the answer about the observed delivery or its continuation. Where feasibility is in question, explain whether the intended result is obtainable and what help or conditions it requires. State any consequential uncertainty.

If the family owner requested an explanation, state what this delivery establishes about the shared profile on the permitted evidence. For a proposed transferable contribution, recover the consequential deltas, the proposed transferable function or method, the supporting conditions, the local residue and what establishes the proposed range. A contrary case can expose a false boundary. When the argument already covers the permitted combinations, give its conditions. Include hidden work and permission limits. Identify the next construction or inquiry if the commission calls for it. CUST.3 receives a family candidate; CUST.6 receives a delivery-method candidate.

| Local answer | Shared answer | Valid continuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | Supported candidate | Continue local provision and construct or qualify the common contribution. |
| Supported | Not transferable or not permitted for the next use | Retain the local result; seek a different common basis only if worthwhile. |
| Not yet supported | Useful negative or limiting knowledge | Protect or end the local attempt; revise the shared assumption without claiming delivery success. |
| Uncertain | Uncertain | Seek evidence that could resolve the commissioned question, narrow the promise or stop within the agreed boundary. |
| Unsupported | No useful further answer | End the attempt and preserve the customer's feasible remaining continuation. |

Stopping can protect resources and expose a boundary that prevents a broader false claim.

#### CUST.5:4.6 - Return the result into actual product and practice work

Return the field finding in a form its recipient can use to revise a family boundary, build a member, change delivery, keep a local service or reject a proposed common form. A supported explanation or recommendation delivered by the agreed time can complete an advisory assignment. For adoption, establish the effective decision of the authorised owner. For actual use, trace the finding to the action that used it, including action under authority already in force.

A permanent field function repeats this method across changing questions. It needs ongoing connections with product and operating work. Review recurring results for general knowledge implications: does the pattern of cases challenge a claim about transfer, support or scale? Keep that knowledge revision distinct from a change to one customer's configuration.

### CUST.5:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A private-hosting attempt initially appears to fail because installation is difficult. The field team recovers two explanations: the deployment tool is inadequate, or the customer's access policy prevents the ordinary setup path.

A permitted simulation and one supervised setup show that the tool worked with the access supplied. The author corrected the input manually during the setup. Retain the access, correction and assistance in the account of that run. The private service can continue locally with those provisions. To decide whether another team could prepare and run it independently, examine how it could obtain valid inputs and resolve the errors itself. This run leaves the need for the same help elsewhere open.

CUST.3 receives the access and input constraints; CUST.6 receives the observed input correction and the question of how ordinary delivery would handle it. For another private environment, the maintainer still needs grounds that its access, preparation and support conditions fit.

Now suppose the customer cannot grant the access specified by this private profile. That excludes this profile in this environment and the team stops before live deployment. For an answer about the customer’s order, consider any other permitted way to meet its requirements. A hosted alternative is available only where the customer’s terms allow it. If a necessary condition fails for every permitted route, the provider can give a definitive negative answer.

For a public service, field inquiry might compare whether a difficulty comes from translated instructions, device access or missing facilitator help. Arrange participation with the required permission and give the local recipients a useful answer. Evidence from one session may leave open which of the three difficulties caused the outcome.

### CUST.5:6 - Bias-Annotation

Field accounts can overrepresent accessible, cooperative or commercially prominent customers. Choose differing cases for the question and avoid treating a convenient sample as the target population.

The method can favor what product teams can incorporate. Preserve local benefits and negative knowledge even when neither becomes a product feature.

### CUST.5:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Is it clear what each receiver needs to know or decide and which findings each is permitted to use?
- Is current work protected by actual resources and stop conditions?
- Do the available grounds distinguish the explanations that matter to the question? If a fresh attempt was commissioned, what did that attempt establish?
- Are deviations, assistance and evidence limits recoverable?
- Are collection, use and transfer permitted for the receiving purpose?
- Can the inquiry end with a local-only, negative, uncertain or stopped result?
- Can the recipient use the answer for the stated purpose by the time it is due? If a change or actual use is claimed, what decision or action establishes it?

### CUST.5:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Demo success becomes production evidence.** Preserve the difference between the demonstrated conditions and ordinary use.

**Every field engineer is a product approver.** Obtain the actual common-admission decision.

**Only success is reported.** Return the supported negative boundary and its implication.

**Permanent field presence means no stopping point.** Bound each inquiry while maintaining the continuing function.

### CUST.5:9 - Consequences

The method can turn field experience into usable local and shared knowledge while protecting customer work. It adds the cost of interpretable observation and receiving interaction.

It can conclude that a promising local solution should remain local. That result may be better than supporting an inadequately generalized product.

### CUST.5:10 - Architectural Rationale

Local delivery and shared development can use the same field finding for different purposes. Those purposes set the boundaries on delivery and learning. A single “handover to product” output loses local continuity; a local-only report loses the transferable conditions.

### CUST.5:11 - SoTA-Echoing

Official FDE descriptions from Palantir and OpenAI describe work spanning customer implementation and feedback to product teams. The full-text practitioner account by akshayk emphasizes continuing field/product learning rather than a temporary deployment squad. These sources establish described practice, not comparative effectiveness.

CUST adopts the continuing feedback connection and qualifies it with bounded questions, permissions, protection of current service, and local and shared answers that may be positive, negative, inconclusive or limited to one use. Use the Implementation Facilitation methods and Engineering.Reference:22 for the relevant inquiry and support work.

Compared with a success-only pilot or a feature-request pipeline, the selected line changes what returns: conditions, hidden assistance, exclusions and the next receiving action. Reopen it when field inquiry repeatedly fails to produce a usable or permitted contribution.

### CUST.5:12 - Relations

CUST.5 can start from an unresolved delivery question without a full CUST.1 cycle. CUST.2 supplies recovered differences where available; CUST.3 and CUST.6 receive qualified common candidates; CUST.7 bounds resources; CUST.8 protects dependent customers when a shared change follows.

[IS.5 — Sustain, Transfer, Extend or End Implementation Support](../../is/16-is-5---sustain-transfer-extend-or-end-implementation-support.md) helps decide whether to sustain, transfer, extend or end the support. Use that conclusion and its conditions when closing the field attempt.
