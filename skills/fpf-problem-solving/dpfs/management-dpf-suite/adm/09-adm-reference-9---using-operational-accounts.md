## ADM.Reference:9 - Using operational accounts

Use an account suited to the operating question. Relate workload and capacity to their financial consequences where relevant, and revisit assumptions if their grounds or the intended use change. Preserve other accounts for their respective purposes. [OPS.14 through OPS.15][OPS] describe the corresponding comparison and observation methods.

For ADM.15, choose the receipts, payments, time and participant burdens relevant to the question and period. In a cash comparison, use the receipts and payments that differ between alternatives. For an investment return or a customer-value claim, use the corresponding finance or accounting method and its comparison basis. An allocated cost matters when its allocation or underlying payment bears on the question.
