## OCE.Application:4 - APP-OCE-04 - OCE practice across a practitioner population

This constructed application examines how eight OCE practitioners in three organizations use one OCE.2 move: recovering actual contributions and the relations that govern the receiving decision. The available material includes permitted case extracts, practitioner explanations, a shared example and the questions peers use to recognize the work. The observations concern practice across the group. Describing the working culture of any one organization would require evidence about that organization.

The old “organization mapping” label is fading. Four observed cases still recover actual Work, evidence-return and decision relations under different titles. Two newer cases provide filled charts but do not recover the relevant crossing; their explanations do not yet close it. Two practitioners have no suitable permitted case in the period.

Using OCE.17, the group first separates three results: continued operative use in the four observed cases; a bounded gap to investigate in the two chart-only cases; and unobserved use for the two without opportunity. The group compares a usable crossing with the shared deficient example and finds that its recognition question rewards completed department boxes. The group has grounds to examine the example used in teaching and the criteria for recognizing the move. Establishing why a particular practitioner struggled would require evidence about that practitioner’s work and conditions.

The existing qualified case-help arrangement is sufficient: within the group's permission, the facilitator and willing practitioners critique the cases, retain usable variants and replace the deficient example with a source-linked case and counterexample. Later feedback asks for the usable contribution and decision relation. The material owners prepare authorized extracts. Participants work through the cases with qualified help, and the group provides a later opportunity to use the move, interpret what happened and maintain the revised example. A permitted opportunity still comes from its owner; employment, learning assessment and evidence-access decisions keep their own authorized and qualified providers.

In the fictional follow-up, one practitioner independently recovers the crossing and receiving authority in a different case; another lacks permitted evidence access. The first is an observed later use, while the second is an access gap. Establishing an effect of training or retention across the profession would require evidence beyond these two follow-up cases.

Use an applicable access decision or refer the outstanding permission to the party empowered to grant it. If the answer depends on distinguishing a human capability, misconception or behaviour limit from an access or work-condition gap, HCD.3 can help. Return a specific reusable-Method defect to OCE.15 or Method Engineering when one is found. A correction to the example may suffice. OCE.10 addresses the working culture of the target organization. OCE.17 includes the association case and the mentor’s departure. In the latter, the group compares adequate continuing provision with an additional local handover. It retains common qualified work and accepts the additional preparation only while its full cost can bring the required benefit in time.
