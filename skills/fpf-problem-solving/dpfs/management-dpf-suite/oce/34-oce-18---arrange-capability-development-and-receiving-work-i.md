## OCE.18 - Arrange Capability Development and Receiving Work in an Organizational Transition

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### OCE.18:0 - Use this when

An organizational change calls for a person to take on different work, but a course, a capability profile or an encouraging mentor has not made the transition possible. The future work, capable help, protected practice, current obligations and decision authority must fit together.

Use this Method to construct an attainable arrangement for the person to prepare for and enter the receiving work, or to identify which contribution still prevents that transition. It applies to an employee changing work, a participant entering a continuing role, or a transition across units or employers where no single manager controls all the conditions.

Begin with the person's intended direction and a representative receiving task. A training request is not yet evidence that missing human capability is the difficulty. If a qualified person already has access, time and an effective assignment sufficient for the work, use those conditions directly. When the missing result is general joint action rather than this work transition, use the Leadership Methods. Personal development without an organizational receiving arrangement returns to Core's C.40.OD and the Human Capability Development DPF. Clinical care, employment decisions and domain assessment remain with the appropriately qualified and authorized practitioners.

### OCE.18:1 - Problem frame

The person, current work owner, receiving work owner, learning provider and provider of shared personnel services can be different participants. A mentor may understand the task without power to change assignments. HR may arrange access without knowing what the work requires. The future work may itself still be designed. Any of these contributions can already be sufficient; the difficulty lies in the missing or incompatible contribution, not necessarily in the person's motivation.

The useful result is a feasible connection between preparation, current work, a real receiving opportunity and the decisions that permit each. A later observation can support a bounded capability claim. Appointment, learning, work performed and organizational development are separately established results.

### OCE.18:2 - Problem

A person is sent on a course because an organization needs different work. The course finishes, but the manager still allocates all available time to old delivery. A mentor recommends investigation while the performance regime rewards only rapid closure. Another unit has a suitable task but has made no commitment to supply it. An attractive training description masks a mismatch between its exercises and the later contribution.

Sending the whole question to HR, the current manager or a coach leaves it unresolved when that participant cannot provide the missing knowledge, opportunity, time or authority. Conversely, a new programme adds cost when an adequate assignment or a known access repair already permits the work.

### OCE.18:3 - Forces

| Force | Tension |
| --- | --- |
| Real receiving work | A meaningful capability target needs an actual or defensible prospective use; a job being designed cannot provide settled demand. |
| Person and organization | A useful organizational contribution must be related to the person's aims and accepted participation, without treating one as proof of the other. |
| Present and future work | Preparation needs time and support while current recipients still depend on delivery. |
| Capability and powers | A competent mentor may lack assignment authority; a manager with authority may lack instructional competence. |
| Learning and personnel decisions | Practice needs useful observation and feedback; those observations support only agreed and justified inferences. |
| Whole burden | Learning can be worthwhile despite adding work; an available competent provider can be enough when the person's own capability is not the intended result. |

### OCE.18:4 - Solution

#### OCE.18:4.1 - Establish the transition and the result that must belong to the person

Recover what changes in the person's work, who needs its result, and why this transition is being considered. Inspect a representative task with the person and the receiving work owner: what must be produced, what judgement is needed, what conditions must be handled and what help will remain available? HCD.1 and HCD.4 supply capability demand and the supported profile. A title, course name or unobserved general trait does not answer those questions.

Keep an emerging work design provisional. OCE.3–OCE.6 can supply the organizational concept, contribution relations, position and enabling relations when these are still unresolved. Do not turn an uncertain prospective job into a mandatory learning target. A bounded exploration of the work can be the next useful result.

Distinguish two receiving purposes. An organization needing a competent result may obtain it from an already qualified person, external provider or qualified AI. A person becoming able to perform and organize the work needs evidence of that person's contribution under the intended support conditions. Compare alternatives against the selected purpose; outsourced success does not satisfy a capability target by relabeling it.

#### OCE.18:4.2 - Make current work, practice and receiving opportunities jointly possible

Recover the current assignments, available time, relevant performance expectations and the receiving opportunity. Identify who understands each work contribution and who can change its conditions. In a matrix, federation or move between employers, these relations may cross several organizations. No title supplies the missing authority.

Start with a competent arrangement already available. Retain adequate help, work access and evidence. If another contribution is needed, compare the complete alternatives over the period in which the receiving work matters. Include preparation, learner and provider time, participation, access, supervision, assessment, retained fallback, delays and displaced work. Developing another participant can add work while competent delivery continues. State the additional result sought rather than pretending that every learning arrangement saves time.

Where practice displaces current work, identify the actual contribution and recipient affected, preserve required checks, and obtain an accepted change from those able to decide it. Use LDR.2 when contributions must be negotiated; use an adequate ordinary allocation directly when it already answers the question. OCE.11 supplies coexistence of change and continuing work. Obtain an actual receiving trial, supervised task or other opportunity with its conditions; a possible vacancy or a friendly promise is not yet a supplied task.

If the current work owner cannot change an adverse condition, return the request to its actual owner. A mentor can demonstrate, diagnose and critique without being made the person's manager. More managerial power does not supply missing instructional expertise. Use OCE.4 and OCE.6 to connect and establish the required organizational relations; use OCE.5 only when a continuing position is needed.

#### OCE.18:4.3 - Obtain qualified preparation and its workplace conditions

When capability is missing, give a qualified learning provider the target: who needs to perform which contribution, in what later situation, under which conditions and with what evidence of useful performance.

Obtain a learning design for that target. It may combine a demonstration, observation of a competent colleague, practice in performing the contribution in a difficult situation, feedback against stated criteria and later use without coaching. Have the provider explain how the chosen activities address the target.

Use [HCD.7](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/24-hcd-7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-hu.md) to establish whether the proposed provider can supply the particular help with the needed preparation, time, access and assessment. Examine a representative learner difficulty: can the mentor demonstrate the operation, recognize the relevant error and give useful feedback? Agree how practice observations may be used and who may receive them. Obtain capable help, prepare the provider through suitable learning or revise the arrangement when that contribution is unavailable. A mentoring title or an available meeting slot does not establish it.

HCD.1 helps establish what the person's later work requires. HCD.3 helps identify a changeable human limitation supported by evidence, or return the question for a different remedy or missing evidence. HCD.4 helps build a capability profile across the person's simultaneous work. Obtain the learning, assessment and transfer Methods still needed from competent practitioners. An assessment of one episode supports only the capability claim its conditions and evidence justify.

With the people responsible for workplace assignments and resources, obtain protected practice time, usable tools and information, a task in which to use the capability, feedback and support. If people are penalized for making the contribution, use OCE.10 to investigate the cause or take a known adverse condition to the person authorized to change it.

Competent AI assistance can be one supplied contribution when it has the needed capability, task context, access and permitted use. Establish who supplies the remaining feedback, assessment or decision; do not retain human duplication merely because the former arrangement used it. Conversely, a fluent explanation or a generated assessment is not evidence that the help can diagnose this learner's difficulty. HCD.7 governs the provider and support question; the applicable professional Method governs its actual performance.

#### OCE.18:4.4 - Connect the career or role transition to its receiving work

Bring the development and work decisions together. HR can supply shared personnel policies, access to learning and work with the employee community. Line managers or other work owners contribute knowledge of their actual jobs and processes, opportunities for practice and the assignment decisions within their authority. Establish who controls the receiving work when the present manager does not. In a matrix or across employers, recover these relations from the actual arrangement.

For a proposed course, have the work owner identify the later task and what suitable performance would look like. A capable learning practitioner examines whether the teaching, practice and assessment address that task. HR can arrange access and procurement under the applicable rules. An administrator's preference for an attractive or familiar presentation does not settle its instructional value; complexity does not settle it either.

Construct a development path together with a realizable work opportunity. Obtain protected practice and feedback under 4.3, the receiving owner's conditions for a trial or appointment, and a feasible change to current work under 4.2. A capability profile informs the decision; it does not appoint the person. Preserve the person's aims and the agreed limits on using learning observations in employment decisions.

If no receiving work is available, retain worthwhile learning while keeping the career transition prospective. If a place exists but the required capability is unsupported, obtain the needed development or change the proposed assignment.

#### OCE.18:4.5 - Obtain the later use and distinguish its conclusions

Perform the agreed practice with its qualified support, then obtain the later receiving contribution under the intended conditions. The learning practitioner judges what the observations support about capability; the work owner judges the work result within their remit; the person with the applicable powers decides an appointment or changed assignment. A course completion record, a good trial and an appointment have different grounds.

If the person succeeds only with prompts or another performer supplying a critical judgement, retain that assistance in the account. It can be an adequate arrangement for current work without showing independent performance. Decide whether further learning is worth its cost for the desired continuation or whether competent supplied help is sufficient.

A failed attempt first locates the missing contribution: task understanding, capability, usable tools, information, time, provider help, assessment, or a work condition. Return it to HCD, the actual domain practitioner or the organizational owner. Do not interpret every failed use as resistance or every absent workplace opportunity as failed learning.

#### OCE.18:4.6 - Continue or revise the arrangement at its actual scope

Reconsider a dependency when the receiving work, assignment, time, provider capability, support or permitted observation use changes. Preserve earlier valid observations and completed contributions; they do not make the next occasion feasible by themselves. Revise the affected arrangement or defer what lacks a supplied condition.

This Method can end with a feasible transition, a bounded capability result, a sufficient supported arrangement, or an identified obstacle. None alone establishes indefinite development. Where the larger purpose is the person's or organization's continuing development, return to C.40.NP from the changed position to form the next worthwhile attainable question. Repeating the same course or assessment is not that continuation.

### OCE.18:5 - Archetypal Grounding

#### OCE.18:5.1 - Make mentoring usable in an employee's working week

In this constructed case, an engineer wants to move into evidence integration. A mentor asks the engineer to investigate unsupported source links, but the line manager assigns forty hours of delivery and rewards package closure without distinguishing a warranted return of an unsupported claim. HR has enrolled the engineer in a generic leadership course.

The engineer and receiving work owner examine a representative integration package. The required contribution includes recognizing a configuration mismatch, returning an unsupported claim to its supplier and assembling a traceable package. They use HCD.1 and HCD.4 to establish the demand and the capability evidence still needed. A learning practitioner examines whether the proposed course teaches those operations. If it does not, they obtain suitable instruction and practice; HR arranges the corresponding access.

The manager and engineer propose thirty-six hours of delivery and four hours of protected practice within the same forty-hour week. They identify a four-hour report-presentation task that can be deferred while retaining every required evidence check. Its receiving team accepts the later date. The manager changes the allocation and establishes how a justified return of an unsupported claim will be treated in performance evaluation. If the receiving team needs the original date, obtain another feasible allocation, negotiate a different scope or postpone the practice.

The mentor prepares a configuration-mismatch case, demonstrates the operation and observes the engineer's attempt. Before counting that help as available, HCD.7 establishes that the mentor can distinguish a real mismatch from an irrelevant objection, give usable feedback and supply the preparation and observation time. The participants agree which practice observations may be used for learning and any separate employment assessment. The receiving work owner provides a supervised assignment and states what would justify a later appointment. Safety retains its separate acceptance decision.

At the joint session, the proposed acceptance claim concerns configuration C7, but its cited test used C6. The engineer exposes the changed interface and asks the responsible specialist whether the evidence remains applicable. Where applicability is unsupported, they develop a joint-test proposal: identify the relevant interaction, load, configuration, observation needed and the decision that would use it. The connected result now explains the evidence gap and a way to answer it. Agreement that everyone should communicate better would leave that result missing.

Suppose the laboratory later withdraws the required window. The account shows which tests and acceptance claims depend on it, so the engineering and allocation owners can reconsider the test, schedule or proposed release. The discussion's conclusions do not silently remain adequate under the changed condition.

Now remove the manager's four-hour practice allocation. The earlier observations and mentor preparation remain useful within their scope, but the next workplace learning episode lacks its conditions. Reallocate work, obtain a feasible later opportunity or retain only independent practice that remains worthwhile. Absence of this performance does not establish a motivation deficit. Conversely, if the time remains but the mentor cannot diagnose the task, obtain capable help or develop the provider's capability. More authority does not supply the missing instruction.

The constructed result is an arrangement that permits the needed help to reach work, followed by observations at their stated scope. Neither the arrangement nor one successful episode establishes a general capability, an appointment or a causal effect of mentoring.

#### OCE.18:5.2 - A receiving role across organizational boundaries

In this constructed variation, the same engineer wants to move to a partner's evidence-integration role. The current manager can release four weekly hours but cannot promise the partner's work, source access or appointment. The partner's work owner supplies a representative non-sensitive task and defines the contribution sought. A qualified learning practitioner can use that task to refine preparation, but protected production sources require a separate permitted arrangement.

The person, both work owners and the learning provider compare a permitted simulation with a supervised partner assignment. The simulation can develop the missing operation but does not by itself establish performance under the partner's actual constraints. The partner can provide a supervised assignment only next month. They retain useful preparation now and keep the work transition prospective. If the timing defeats the person's purpose, they reconsider the direction or another receiving opportunity rather than inventing an appointment.

A qualified AI can now help construct varied practice and diagnose the bounded error. They replace those formerly human contributions only after establishing the capability and permitted task access. The remaining supervised assignment and appointment conditions do not disappear. If the person's own integration judgement is the target, an agent completing the package alone supplies a different result. The changed arrangement is compared for its actual preparation, supervision and access burden.

### OCE.18:6 - Bias-Annotation

The account can overvalue formal courses, the current manager's knowledge or an organization's immediate demand. Recover the person's aims, actual receiving work and qualified help rather than treating any one participant as the whole owner. The opposite error is to describe self-development without the real work opportunity, resources and powers needed for this transition. A useful no-transition outcome can preserve learning while leaving the appointment prospective.

### OCE.18:7 - Conformance Checklist

- The person, intended receiving work, current obligations and sought capability or supplied result are distinguished.
- A prospective job remains a demand hypothesis where its relevant conditions are undecided.
- The help is specified as a contribution and has a capable, available provider; a title is insufficient.
- Practice and later use have feasible time, means and conditions alongside current work, including accepted displacement where needed.
- Learning observations, work judgement and appointment decisions have their actual purposes and powers.
- A sufficient existing arrangement can end the construction without an added course or mentor.
- A changed condition reopens its affected reliance while preserving valid earlier results.
- Any continuing-development claim uses the further development construction, not course completion or one successful episode alone.

### OCE.18:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

| Failure | Why it leaves the work unresolved | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send the problem to HR | Shared personnel services may control neither actual work nor domain instruction. | Obtain the work owner's, learning provider's and personnel-service contributions that are actually needed. |
| Add practice to a full week | The plan counts time already committed to other recipients. | Establish an accepted displacement or another feasible opportunity. |
| Appoint through a capability profile | Evidence of capability does not supply an effective assignment decision. | Obtain the decision under the actual powers and receiving conditions. |
| Treat absent use as low motivation | Access, capacity, provider help or the receiving task can be missing. | Locate the consequential unmet condition before selecting its remedy. |
| Replace the learner with AI and claim learning | The immediate output can improve without the person's capability changing. | Compare against the intended holder's result and retain appropriate evidence. |
| Turn a local transition into the whole of development | One changed assignment or mastered Method does not supply continuation. | Return the larger development question to C.40.OD when it is the actual purpose. |

### OCE.18:9 - Consequences

The learning and work plans constrain each other early enough to expose a missing receiving opportunity or incompatible allocation. Adequate existing provision can be retained. The arrangement can also cost more than current competent delivery because it intentionally develops another performer. Its value depends on that further result and its actual use, not on having arranged training.

The Method makes the conditions and separate decisions obtainable and revisable at the scope of the transition.

### OCE.18:10 - Rationale

The same career or role transition crosses learning, work design and organizational powers. Keeping these direct responsibilities does not separate the problem into unrelated referrals: the intended receiving task connects the preparation, opportunity and decision. Qualified instruction can be sufficient for learning while organizational provision still blocks its use. A usable workplace opportunity can exist while the needed capability remains unsupported. Those different failures need different repairs.

This Method constructs the organizational arrangement for the transition. Leadership supplies general joint-action Methods; HCD supplies the learning Methods.

### OCE.18:11 - SoTA-Echoing

The practice question is how to connect capability preparation with a real organizational work transition. **Adapt** task-specific development with qualified help, workplace opportunity and support. An appropriate course can be sufficient when the other needed conditions already exist; when they are missing, obtain them as part of the arrangement. A competent current provider is sufficient when the receiving purpose is only current delivery, rather than this person's new capability.

R10, *Systems Management*, 3:6 and 11:2, contributes the problems of mentoring without control over assignments, conflicting HR and line-management contributions, and administrative course selection. The construction preserves those practical difficulties while rejecting universal organizational preferences. It connects the actual required contributions rather than prescribing that HR, a manager or a mentor must always own the whole.

The [2024 LOCI trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.josat.2024.209437) studies a combined clinical-services intervention with leadership feedback, training, continued coaching and support from higher organizational levels. It supports examining the whole support arrangement as an intervention choice; it does not establish a causal effect for the engineer's career transition. A qualified programme combining the needed contributions remains an alternative to assembling them separately.

[Making soft skills stick](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1359432X.2024.2376909), 2024, examines conditions for later work use; the [reverse training transfer study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2025.106920), 2025, examines feedback from workplace experience to training. Their scoping-review and exploratory maritime-case grounds guide this conditional construction, not a universal effect estimate. Adapt their workplace-support and return contributions through HCD and §§4.3–4.6. Reopen the arrangement when the receiving task, support or evidence for transfer changes.

The constructed cases and arithmetic illustrate conditional choices. They are not observations of intervention effectiveness.

### OCE.18:12 - Relations

HCD.1 supplies capability demand; HCD.3 tests the proposed human limitation; HCD.4 supplies the supported capability profile; HCD.7 supplies provider, access, tools and AI support, with HCD.8 when that provider needs development. HCD's actual learning, assessment and transfer Methods remain the source of those contributions.

OCE.3–OCE.6 supply organizational design and enabling relations; OCE.9 realizes the bounded organizational contribution; OCE.10 investigates obstacles in work; OCE.11 reconciles the transition with continuing service. OCE.17 governs development of organization-change engineering practice itself. Leadership's LDR.1, LDR.2 and LDR.4 supply leadership contributions, negotiation and proposal preparation through first useful work. FPF B.5.SD supplies substantive discussion. An adequate direct contribution may finish the local question.

Core's C.40.DC supplies direction, Decision Design's PSDA.19 supplies bounded advising engagement, and C.40.OD supplies the connected undertaking when the subject must continue beyond this transition. C.36 governs cultural-continuation claims; C.28 governs causal claims. Applicable governing and professional practices decide their actual authorizations and judgements; the organization-change Method does not grant them.
