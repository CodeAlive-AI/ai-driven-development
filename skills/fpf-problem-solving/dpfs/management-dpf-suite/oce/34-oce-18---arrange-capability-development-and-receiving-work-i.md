## OCE.18 - Arrange Capability Development and Receiving Work in an Organizational Transition

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### OCE.18:0 - Use this when

An organizational change calls for a person to take on different work, and the receiving work, required preparation or support needs to be established. The future work, capable help, protected practice, current obligations and decision authority must fit together.

Use this Method to construct an attainable arrangement for the person to prepare for and enter the receiving work, or to identify which contribution still prevents that transition. It applies to an employee changing work, a participant entering a continuing role, or a transition across units or employers where no single manager controls all the conditions.

Begin with the person's intended direction and a representative receiving task. Use the task and available evidence to identify any human capability that still needs development. If a qualified person already has access, time and an effective assignment sufficient for the work, use those conditions directly. When the missing result is general joint action rather than this work transition, use the Leadership Methods. Personal development without an organizational receiving arrangement returns to Core's C.40.OD and the Human Capability Development DPF. Clinical care, employment decisions and domain assessment remain with the appropriately qualified and authorized practitioners.

### OCE.18:1 - Problem frame

The person, current work owner, receiving work owner, learning provider and provider of shared personnel services can be different participants. A mentor may understand the task without power to change assignments. HR may arrange access without knowing what the work requires. The future work may itself still be designed. Use the contributions that already suffice and identify any gaps or incompatibilities that still matter.

The useful result is a feasible connection between preparation, current work, a real receiving opportunity and the decisions that permit each. A later observation can support a bounded capability claim. Appointment, learning, work performed and organizational development are separately established results.

### OCE.18:2 - Problem

A person is sent on a course because an organization needs different work. The course finishes, but the manager still allocates all available time to old delivery. A mentor recommends investigation while the performance regime rewards only rapid closure. Another unit has a suitable task but has made no commitment to supply it. An attractive training description masks a mismatch between its exercises and the later contribution.

Sending the whole question to HR, the current manager or a coach leaves it unresolved when that participant cannot provide the missing knowledge, opportunity, time or authority. Conversely, a new programme adds cost when an adequate assignment or a known access repair already permits the work.

### OCE.18:3 - Forces

| Force | Tension |
| --- | --- |
| Real receiving work | A meaningful capability target needs an actual or defensible prospective use; a job being designed cannot provide settled demand. |
| Person and organization | A useful organizational contribution must be related to the person's aims and accepted participation, without treating one as proof of the other. |
| Present and future work | Preparation needs time and support while current recipients still depend on delivery. |
| Capability and powers | A competent mentor may lack assignment authority; a manager with authority may lack instructional competence. |
| Learning and personnel decisions | Practice needs useful observation and feedback; those observations support only agreed and justified inferences. |
| Whole burden | Learning can be worthwhile despite adding work; an available competent provider can be enough when the person's own capability is not the intended result. |

### OCE.18:4 - Solution

#### OCE.18:4.1 - Establish the transition and the result that must belong to the person

Establish what would change in the person’s work, who will use the result and why the transition is being considered. Examine a representative task, the judgements it calls for, its conditions and the help that would remain. Use suitable task evidence and existing accounts. When the person’s aims or experience are still unclear, seek their account; where a receiving opportunity or commitment is in question, establish it with the party able to provide it. HCD.1 and HCD.4 can supply the capability demand and an evidence-based profile.

Keep an emerging work design provisional. OCE.3–OCE.6 can supply the organizational concept, contribution relations, position and enabling relations when these are still unresolved. When the receiving job is still being designed, a bounded exploration can establish what work is likely to be needed and whether there is a current basis for a learning target.

Distinguish two receiving purposes. An organization needing a competent result may obtain it from an already qualified person, external provider or qualified AI. A person becoming able to perform and organize the work needs evidence of that person's contribution under the intended support conditions. Compare alternatives against the selected purpose.

#### OCE.18:4.2 - Make current work, practice and receiving opportunities jointly possible

Recover the current assignments, available time, relevant performance expectations and the receiving opportunity. Identify who understands each work contribution and who can change its conditions. In a matrix, federation or move between employers, these relations may cross several organizations. Use the decision rights that apply to the transition.

Start with a competent arrangement already available. Retain adequate help, work access and evidence. If another contribution is needed, compare the complete alternatives over the period in which the receiving work matters. Include preparation, learner and provider time, participation, access, supervision, assessment, retained fallback, delays and displaced work. Developing another participant can add work while competent delivery continues. State the additional capability sought and the burden the arrangement requires.

Where practice displaces current work, identify the actual contribution and recipient affected, preserve required checks, and obtain an accepted change from those able to decide it. Use LDR.2 when contributions must be negotiated; use an adequate ordinary allocation directly when it already answers the question. OCE.11 supplies coexistence of change and continuing work. For a transition that depends on a receiving trial, supervised task or other opportunity, establish its availability and conditions. If it is still a proposal, keep the transition prospective.

If the current work owner cannot change an adverse condition, return the request to its actual owner. A mentor can demonstrate, diagnose and critique without being made the person's manager. Use OCE.4 and OCE.6 to connect and establish the required organizational relations; use OCE.5 only when a continuing position is needed.

#### OCE.18:4.3 - Obtain qualified preparation and its workplace conditions

When developing the person’s capability is the selected response, make the target clear to the learner. Identify the contribution they will make, the conditions of later work and what will show useful performance. Give each helper what they need to support that work. Obtain a qualified provider when the learning or assessment calls for one.

Use a suitable learning design for that target or obtain one if needed. It may combine a demonstration, observation of a competent colleague, practice in performing the contribution in a difficult situation, feedback against stated criteria and later use without coaching. Use the design’s grounds to establish how the activities address the target. Resolve a material gap from an adequate explanation or a qualified contribution. Seek the provider’s judgement when that judgement is required.

Use [HCD.7](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/24-hcd-7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-hu.md) to establish whether the proposed provider can supply the particular help with the needed preparation, time, access and assessment. Qualify each provider for the contribution they will supply. If one mentor will demonstrate the operation, recognize errors and give feedback, use a representative learner difficulty to examine those abilities together. Establish the agreed uses of practice observations and who may receive them. Obtain any new agreement needed for a proposed use. Obtain capable help, prepare the provider through suitable learning or revise the arrangement when that contribution is unavailable. A mentoring title or an available meeting slot does not establish it.

HCD.1 helps establish what the person's later work requires. HCD.3 helps identify a changeable human limitation supported by evidence, or return the question for a different remedy or missing evidence. HCD.4 helps build a capability profile across the person's simultaneous work. Use applicable learning, assessment and transfer Methods. Seek a qualified contribution for a question that remains open. An assessment of one episode supports only the capability claim its conditions and evidence justify.

Establish the practice conditions the transition needs, including protected time, usable tools and information, receiving work, feedback and support. Obtain a missing resource, service or assignment from the party able to supply it. If people are penalized for making the contribution, use OCE.10 to investigate the cause or take a known adverse condition to the person authorized to change it.

Competent AI assistance can be one supplied contribution when it has the needed capability, task context, access and permitted use. Identify who supplies any remaining feedback, assessment or decision. Where the proposed help must diagnose a learner’s difficulty, qualify its performance on that task. HCD.7 governs the provider and support question; the applicable professional Method governs its actual performance.

#### OCE.18:4.4 - Connect the career or role transition to its receiving work

Bring the development and work decisions together. HR can supply shared personnel policies, access to learning and work with the employee community. Line managers or other work owners contribute knowledge of their actual jobs and processes, opportunities for practice and the assignment decisions within their authority. Establish who controls the receiving work when the present manager does not. In a matrix or across employers, recover these relations from the actual arrangement.

For a proposed course, identify the receiving task and criteria for useful performance from a suitable work specification or the receiving work owner. Use a qualified learning assessment of how the teaching, practice, feedback and assessment address performance of that task. If the assessment is missing, obtain it. HR can arrange access or procurement under the applicable rules.

Connect development, where needed, to a realizable work opportunity. Use the existing practice conditions, receiving assignment and allocation of current work where they suffice. Obtain protected practice or feedback, a receiving trial or appointment, or a change to current duties only where the transition depends on it. If an appointment is required, the capability profile informs the authorized decision. Preserve the person’s aims and the agreed limits on using learning observations in employment decisions.

If no receiving work is available, retain worthwhile learning while keeping the career transition prospective. If a place exists but the evidence for capability is insufficient, seek available performance evidence or report the limit. Where a real capability gap is established, consider development, qualified assistance or a changed assignment.

#### OCE.18:4.5 - Obtain the later use and distinguish its conclusions

When practice and later use are part of the transition, use adequate observations already available or conduct the agreed work with its qualified support. For a new capability conclusion, establish what the person performed under the intended conditions. If recognizing a need for action or choosing what to do is part of the capability, establish who recognized the occasion and who chose the action. Help can supply one and leave the other to the person. Use a qualified assessor when an assessment is required. The applicable mandates determine who may accept the work result and who may make the appointment, including an organ acting collectively. Establish the appointee’s own acceptance where it is required. A course completion record, a good trial and an appointment have different grounds.

To conclude that the person gained a capability, compare the present performance with evidence of what the same person could do earlier, allowing for changes in the task, access and help. If their earlier capability is unknown, report the supported present result. If the person succeeds with prompts or another performer supplying a critical judgement, retain that assistance in the account. It can be an adequate arrangement for current work without showing independent performance. Decide whether further learning is worth its cost for the desired continuation or whether competent supplied help is sufficient.

When an attempt fails, use the observations to locate the difficulty as far as they allow. Consider the task, capability, tools, information, time, help, assessment and work conditions. Use or obtain the relevant HCD, domain or organizational contribution. Where the cause remains unresolved, report the observed failure and the limit on its interpretation.

#### OCE.18:4.6 - Continue or revise the arrangement at its actual scope

Reconsider a dependency when the receiving work, assignment, time, provider capability, support or permitted observation use changes. Preserve earlier valid observations and completed contributions; they do not make the next occasion feasible by themselves. Revise the affected arrangement or defer what lacks a supplied condition.

This Method can end with a feasible transition, a bounded capability result, a sufficient supported arrangement, or an identified obstacle. For a further question about the person’s or organization’s continuing development, use C.40.NP from the changed position to identify the next worthwhile, attainable contribution.

### OCE.18:5 - Archetypal Grounding

#### OCE.18:5.1 - Make mentoring usable in an employee's working week

In this constructed case, an engineer wants to move into evidence integration. A mentor asks the engineer to investigate unsupported source links, but the line manager assigns forty hours of delivery and rewards package closure without distinguishing a warranted return of an unsupported claim. HR has enrolled the engineer in a generic leadership course.

The engineer and receiving work owner examine a representative integration package. The required contribution includes recognizing a configuration mismatch, returning an unsupported claim to its supplier and assembling a traceable package. They use HCD.1 and HCD.4 to compare the demand with the engineer’s earlier work under the support allowed in the new role. If it already shows the required capability, the receiving owner can consider the assignment. In this case the engineer can assemble a package but has yet to distinguish which configuration changes defeat a cited test. The generic course offers no practice in that judgement. The mentor and engineer arrange a case that calls for it, and HR secures access.

The manager and engineer propose thirty-six hours of delivery and four hours of protected practice within the same forty-hour week. They identify a four-hour report-presentation task that can be deferred while retaining every required evidence check. Its receiving team accepts the later date. The manager changes the allocation and establishes how a justified return of an unsupported claim will be treated in performance evaluation. If the receiving team needs the original date, obtain another feasible allocation, negotiate a different scope or postpone the practice.

The mentor prepares a configuration-mismatch case, demonstrates the operation and observes the engineer's attempt. Before counting that help as available, HCD.7 establishes that the mentor can distinguish a real mismatch from an irrelevant objection, give usable feedback and supply the preparation and observation time. The participants agree which practice observations may be used for learning and any separate employment assessment. The receiving work owner provides a supervised assignment and states what would justify a later appointment. Safety retains its separate acceptance decision.

At the joint session, the proposed acceptance claim concerns configuration C7, but its cited test used C6. The engineer exposes the changed interface and asks the responsible specialist whether the evidence remains applicable. Where applicability is unsupported, they develop a joint-test proposal: identify the relevant interaction, load, configuration, observation needed and the decision that would use it. The connected result now explains the evidence gap and a way to answer it.

Suppose the laboratory later withdraws the required window. The account shows which tests and acceptance claims depend on it, so the engineering and allocation owners can reconsider the test, schedule or proposed release.

Now remove the manager's four-hour practice allocation. The earlier observations and mentor preparation remain useful within their scope, but the next workplace learning episode lacks its conditions. Reallocate work, obtain a feasible later opportunity or retain only independent practice that remains worthwhile. If time remains available but the mentor cannot diagnose the task, obtain a capable provider or develop the mentor’s capability.

The constructed result is an arrangement that permits the needed help to reach work, followed by observations at their stated scope. A general capability, an appointment and an effect attributable to mentoring each need evidence specific to that claim.

#### OCE.18:5.2 - A receiving role across organizational boundaries

In this constructed variation, the same engineer wants to move to a partner's evidence-integration role. The current manager can release four weekly hours but cannot promise the partner's work, source access or appointment. The partner's work owner supplies a representative non-sensitive task and defines the contribution sought. A qualified learning practitioner can use that task to refine preparation, but protected production sources require a separate permitted arrangement.

The person, both work owners and the learning provider compare a permitted simulation with a supervised partner assignment. The simulation can develop the missing operation but does not by itself establish performance under the partner's actual constraints. The partner can provide a supervised assignment only next month. They retain useful preparation now and keep the work transition prospective. If the timing defeats the person's purpose, they reconsider the direction or another receiving opportunity rather than inventing an appointment.

A qualified AI can now help construct varied practice and diagnose the bounded error. They replace those formerly human contributions only after establishing the capability and permitted task access. The remaining supervised assignment and appointment conditions do not disappear. If the person's own integration judgement is the target, an agent completing the package alone supplies a different result. The changed arrangement is compared for its actual preparation, supervision and access burden.

### OCE.18:6 - Bias-Annotation

The account can overvalue formal courses, the current manager's knowledge or an organization's immediate demand. Establish the person’s aims, the receiving opportunity, qualified help, resources and powers from the sources that can supply them. A useful no-transition outcome can preserve learning while leaving the appointment prospective.

### OCE.18:7 - Conformance Checklist

- The person, intended receiving work, current obligations and sought capability or supplied result are distinguished.
- A prospective job remains a demand hypothesis where its relevant conditions are undecided.
- Where the transition needs help, identify the contribution and establish its availability and the provider’s capability, or report the remaining gap.
- For a feasible practice or receiving opportunity, establish the required time, means and conditions alongside current work, including accepted displacement where needed. An unavailable condition keeps the dependent opportunity prospective.
- Learning observations, work judgement and appointment decisions have their actual purposes and powers.
- A sufficient existing arrangement can end the construction without an added course or mentor.
- A changed condition reopens its affected reliance while preserving valid earlier results.
- A claim that development continued is supported by what occurred in the stated interval. If the task is to continue development, identify the next undertaking and the conditions that make it possible.

### OCE.18:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

| Failure | Why it leaves the work unresolved | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send the problem to HR | Shared personnel services may control neither actual work nor domain instruction. | Obtain the work owner's, learning provider's and personnel-service contributions that are needed. |
| Add practice to a full week | The plan counts time already committed to other recipients. | Establish an accepted displacement or another feasible opportunity. |
| Appoint through a capability profile | Evidence of capability does not supply an effective assignment decision. | Obtain the decision under the actual powers and receiving conditions. |
| Treat absent use as low motivation | Access, capacity, provider help or the receiving task can be missing. | Locate the consequential unmet condition before selecting its remedy. |
| Replace the learner with AI and claim learning | The immediate output can improve without the person's capability changing. | Compare against the intended holder's result and retain appropriate evidence. |
| Turn a local transition into the whole of development | One changed assignment or mastered Method does not supply continuation. | Return the larger development question to C.40.OD when it is the actual purpose. |

### OCE.18:9 - Consequences

The learning and work plans constrain each other early enough to expose a missing receiving opportunity or incompatible allocation. Adequate existing provision can be retained. The arrangement can also cost more than current competent delivery because it intentionally develops another performer. Its value depends on that further result and its actual use, not on having arranged training.



### OCE.18:10 - Rationale

The same career or role transition crosses learning, work design and organizational powers. The intended receiving task connects the preparation, opportunity and decision, each under its own responsibility. Qualified instruction can be sufficient for learning while organizational provision still blocks its use. A usable workplace opportunity can exist while the needed capability remains unsupported. Those different failures need different repairs.

This Method constructs the organizational arrangement for the transition. Leadership supplies general joint-action Methods; HCD supplies the learning Methods.

### OCE.18:11 - SoTA-Echoing

The practice question is how to connect capability preparation with a real organizational work transition. **Adapt** task-specific development with qualified help, workplace opportunity and support. An appropriate course can be sufficient when the other needed conditions already exist; when they are missing, obtain them as part of the arrangement. A competent current provider is sufficient when the receiving purpose is only current delivery, rather than this person's new capability.

R10, *Systems Management*, 3:6 and 11:2, contributes the problems of mentoring without control over assignments, conflicting HR and line-management contributions, and administrative course selection. The receiving task determines which contributions the personnel service, work owner and learning provider need to supply.

The [2024 LOCI trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.josat.2024.209437) studies a combined clinical-services intervention with leadership feedback, training, continued coaching and support from higher organizational levels. It supports examining the whole support arrangement as an intervention choice; it does not establish a causal effect for the engineer's career transition. A qualified programme combining the needed contributions remains an alternative to assembling them separately.

[Making soft skills stick](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1359432X.2024.2376909), 2024, examines conditions for later work use; the [reverse training transfer study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2025.106920), 2025, examines feedback from workplace experience to training. Their scoping-review and exploratory maritime-case grounds guide this conditional construction, not a universal effect estimate. Adapt their workplace-support and return contributions through HCD and §§4.3–4.6. Reopen the arrangement when the receiving task, support or evidence for transfer changes.



### OCE.18:12 - Relations

HCD.1 supplies capability demand; HCD.3 tests the proposed human limitation; HCD.4 supplies the supported capability profile; HCD.7 supplies provider, access, tools and AI support, with HCD.8 when that provider needs development. HCD's actual learning, assessment and transfer Methods remain the source of those contributions.

OCE.3–OCE.6 supply organizational design and enabling relations; OCE.9 realizes the bounded organizational contribution; OCE.10 investigates obstacles in work; OCE.11 reconciles the transition with continuing service. OCE.17 governs development of organization-change engineering practice itself. Leadership's LDR.1, LDR.2 and LDR.4 supply leadership contributions, negotiation and proposal preparation through first useful work. FPF B.5.SD supplies substantive discussion. An adequate direct contribution may finish the local question.

Core's C.40.DC supplies direction, Decision Design's PSDA.19 supplies bounded advising engagement, and C.40.OD supplies the connected undertaking when the subject must continue beyond this transition. C.36 governs cultural-continuation claims; C.28 governs causal claims. Establish each required authorization and professional judgement from the source competent to supply it.
