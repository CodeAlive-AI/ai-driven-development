## OCE.Sources:1 - Product-series constitution and edition continuity

**Product series.** The 12 October 2026 edition is the first admitted member of the Organization Change Engineering product series. Use the series to design, realize or reconsider a change to organizational relations or capability while work continues, or to develop Organization Change Engineering practice itself. Start with the change question and identify which contributions the work requires. An authorized unit can carry a change through itself or obtain contributions it lacks. For routine operations, joint-work leadership or individual capability development, use a suitable existing result or obtain the specialist contribution the change needs. Replacing this purpose with general leadership or organization operation identifies another series.

Admit a later edition through an explicit decision when its relation to the actual source edition is established and the complete language is qualified for this purpose. Preserve independently useful operations, needed results and qualified supplier boundaries when refining, splitting or redistributing contributions. Correct false claims and mistaken boundaries.

Review the affected admission or use when a relied-on foundation or source changes, a required contribution becomes unavailable, or use exposes a consequential failure. Loss of qualification calls for a warning and a decision about repair, withdrawal of recommendation or retirement. Superseded, unavailable or retired editions retain their historical membership while the series continues; a false admission claim requires correction. Explicit retirement ends the series and its current belonging occurrences while preserving past facts. A later active collection requires a new constitution.

A change of title, publication home, Suite membership or maintenance does not by itself change the identity of the series. The constitution establishes no maintenance service, future edition or uninterrupted availability.
