## OCE.Reference:5 - How the contributions connect

The patterns connect organization design and realization with consequence comparison, revision, Method development and continuation of OCE practice. The [Table of Contents](_index.md#table-of-contents) identifies the specific question addressed by each pattern.

Use OCE.13 to compare the consequences relevant to the question. A claim about what caused a change needs evidence for that attribution. If a revision is under consideration, use OCE.14 to decide which relations to retain or change within the decision-maker’s authority and identify any work that remains. OCE.9–OCE.11 retain the local observation, correction and continuation tests used in realization, participation and service work. OCE.18 connects capability preparation with actual receiving work in an organizational transition. LDR.1 supplies the leadership contribution when that is the missing result.

Use `OCE.10` for a participation or adoption gap in the target organization's working culture, with `LDR.1` for leadership contributions and `C.36` for culture mechanics. Use OCE.17 to follow how OCE Methods are encountered, criticized, selected, enacted, retained or lost among practitioners. Distinguish that practice from its description and carrier, and identify the actual uses within the practitioner population. Treating that population as one System or capability holder requires a separate basis.

Continuing organization development can combine these contributions with Strategy, Operations, Administration, human learning, research and product engineering. A comparison can remain useful when a proposed revision cannot proceed. An authorized revision still needs realization and later observation.
