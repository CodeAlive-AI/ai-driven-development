## GOALS.1 - Choose the goal and the kind of reliance it creates

**Type:** Method pattern.
**Status:** Stable.

### GOALS.1:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** a wish, target or assignment must become an intelligible basis for action, or participants are using the same goal words with different expectations. The object is the meaning and intended use of the goal. The result is an aim that can guide its next construction or execution, including any unresolved choice.

The reader needs access to the affected work, its recipient and the person or arrangement able to decide its purpose. An adequate existing goal can be used directly. If the remaining issue is an operating target and comparison, OPS.21 already supplies that method; use its result here only when a wider goal relationship still needs construction.

### GOALS.1:2 - Problem

“Improve service,” “finish ten cases,” and “become capable of handling difficult cases” can each be useful. They demand different actions and evidence. Treating them as interchangeable can make people deliver volume when quality matters, promise an unknown research result, or substitute an automated output for wanted human mastery. The first task is to determine what should change or remain adequate and how that statement will be used.

### GOALS.1:3 - Forces

A goal needs enough stability to orient action and enough revisability to respond to a changed need. A demanding aspiration can reveal a better method while an unsupported promise imposes costs on those who rely on it. Immediate delivery, inquiry, learning and maintenance can cooperate or compete. Clear meaning should reduce reconstruction without creating a form larger than the working question.

### GOALS.1:4 - Solution

#### GOALS.1:4.1 - Recover the desired condition and its grounds

Ask who needs what result, in which situation and by what useful horizon. Examine a representative case. Separate the condition valued by the recipient from a proposed means: a customer needs a usable repaired device; a new scheduling tool is one possible way to improve the repair service. State conditions that must remain protected, such as safe operation, eligibility, workload limits or an existing promise. Use the conditions that actually govern this work.

Explain why this aim matters now. A known unmet need, a selected strategy, an established obligation, a useful opportunity or an explicit personal purpose can supply that ground. When participants disagree, expose the differing result or value and obtain the decision through the governing practice. A clearer sentence can make the disagreement discussable; it cannot decide whose preference governs.

Choose the goal's scope from the receiving result. An individual contribution can be framed separately when it has a usable recipient and completion basis. When success depends on several jointly necessary contributions, identify the whole result and the relevant relations. Decompose far enough to obtain or assign those contributions without assuming that every organizational level needs a miniature copy of the same number.

#### GOALS.1:4.2 - Distinguish the uses of the statement

Explain which of the following meanings the current statement carries. Several may coexist with different values.

| Meaning | What it says | Immediate consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desired outcome or maintained condition | What should become true or remain adequate for the recipient. | Construct its achievement or continuation test in GOALS.2. |
| Ambition | A worthwhile outcome beyond or different from the present expectation. | Expose the gap and search for a feasible way, provision or narrower inquiry. |
| Forecast | What is expected under stated present assumptions. | Keep the basis and uncertainty available; update them when evidence changes. |
| Commitment | What another participant may rely on under an effective agreement or obligation. | Obtain the relevant authority, acceptance and means; change it through its actual terms. |
| Inquiry goal | The answer or discriminating knowledge needed for a decision. | Specify a question, adequate method and useful possible conclusions. |
| Learning goal | A capability that a named person or intended audience should acquire for later work. | Identify the performance, allowed support and evidence; obtain the learning arrangement. |

An aspiration may be deliberately uncertain. A committed investigation can reliably promise a qualified answer or a truthful inconclusive result under its agreed scope, while the desired discovery remains uncertain. A learner can undertake practice without promising that a fixed number of hours will establish mastery. These arrangements become usable when their receiving parties understand the distinction.

In an operating example, ambition 12, forecast 8 and a supported promise of 10 need not be forced into one number. The ambition locates a possible improvement, the forecast informs response and the promise governs reliance. If the means currently support only 8, the supposed promise of 10 needs further provision or renegotiation; writing it on a goal sheet does not supply the difference. Use OPS.13 and OPS.21 for the actual operating construction.

#### GOALS.1:4.3 - Connect the goal to a possible next action

Reason backward from the desired condition to the contribution needed next, and forward from available means to what they can produce. If a suitable method, capable provider and needed conditions are already available, use them. If the way is missing, construct or obtain it through the subject practice and C.39. If a consequential uncertainty remains, use a warranted inquiry; STR.7 supplies the comparison between further evidence and sufficient present action.

The immediate result may be a task, a request for a missing contribution, a bounded inquiry, a learning opportunity or a decision to keep an adequate condition under observation. A goal can be meaningful before a complete plan is known. Keep the next reliance proportionate to what is available: preserve a worthwhile aspiration while withholding the unsupported delivery promise.

#### GOALS.1:4.4 - Make the interpretation usable and revisable

Agree the result, its kind, owner of the relevant decision, protected conditions and the next action with the participants who need them. Use the existing proposal, assignment or conversation if it carries these meanings. Pass achievement questions to GOALS.2 and joint feasibility to GOALS.3. State a material reason to return: changed recipient need, invalid means hypothesis, lost provision or altered participation.

Recognition consists in noticing that the same sentence is being used to mean different things. Assurance requires the actual ground, authority, conditions and evidence for any reliance the sentence creates. A statement may remain a proposal until those conditions are obtained.

### GOALS.1:5 - Archetypal Grounding

A research team is told to “raise yield to 95% this month.” Current knowledge does not establish a method that can achieve it. The useful decision is whether to fund a new process. The team separates the desired production yield from this month's inquiry: compare two feasible process variants under qualified measurements and return the conditions under which either merits further development. A well-supported finding that neither variant qualifies can complete the inquiry while leaving the production ambition unmet.

Now change the case: the process is established, materials and capacity are available, and the customer has an accepted delivery agreement. Renaming the production promise “learning” after a miss would change its meaning retrospectively. The team uses the promise's actual terms, diagnoses the failure and obtains any needed change. The distinction depends on the original work and reliance, not on whether the reported number is favorable.

### GOALS.1:6 - Bias-Annotation

The account favors explicit professional purposes that can be discussed with affected participants. Some values or conflicts resist a common measure. Keep those differences visible and use the legitimate decision or negotiation arrangement. A personal reason need be disclosed only to the extent the person chooses and the work legitimately requires.

### GOALS.1:7 - Conformance Checklist

- Can the recipient and desired or maintained condition be identified?
- Are the meanings of ambition, forecast, commitment, inquiry and learning clear where they change action?
- Does the next action have an adequate method and conditions, or is the precise missing contribution exposed?
- Can the relevant participant change the purpose or reliance through the actual decision arrangement?

### GOALS.1:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

**Treating a means as the goal:** return to the recipient result when a proposed installation, meeting or task can finish without supplying it. Retain the means when it helps obtain that result.

**Changing the kind after seeing the outcome:** preserve what was originally promised, then make a prospective revision through the relevant agreement. Honest uncertainty at the outset enables inquiry; retrospective relabelling conceals performance.

### GOALS.1:9 - Consequences

The next actor receives a meaningful task or an exact construction question. Uncertainty can be acted on without pretending it has disappeared. Clarifying a disputed purpose can reveal that no common undertaking is yet available; that is useful information for the decision owner.

### GOALS.1:10 - Architectural Rationale

The method starts with result and reliance. It draws on general control and existing operating goal construction, then supplies the cross-domain distinction needed when delivery, inquiry and development coexist. A numerical target with a relevant deadline can be sufficient once those meanings are settled; it is a poor substitute for settling them.

### GOALS.1:11 - SoTA-Echoing

Doerr's committed/aspirational distinction and stretch cases supply different expectations and responses within OKR. This method retains that distinction and develops inquiry, learning and maintained conditions using STR, HCD and OPS.21. It rejects a universal stretch percentage because the received reliance and available means differ. A stronger domain-specific goal construction should be used when it supplies the same needed distinctions more directly.

### GOALS.1:12 - Relations

GOALS.2 receives the interpreted goal and constructs its achievement basis. GOALS.3 receives the stated reliance and resource needs. GOALS.4 revisits the interpretation when its grounds change. OPS.21 remains the full supplier for an ordinary operating aim; STR.7 supplies warranted inquiry and HCD supplies the human development construction.
