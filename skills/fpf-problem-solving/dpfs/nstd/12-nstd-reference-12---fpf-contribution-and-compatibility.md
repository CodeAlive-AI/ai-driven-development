## NSTD.Reference:12 - FPF contribution and compatibility

For the general methods used here, see [FPF Core](../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md). Use `A.6.3.NAR` for the relation between source and narrative, `C.2.8` for what a particular reader can recover, and `A.19.ECS` and `C.16` for the evaluation in `NSTD.6`. The support maps identify the other methods used for particular claims.

When a Core definition changes, reconsider the narrative uses that relied on the changed provision. Check whether they still work with the new edition. `E.4.PFR` explains the dependency and `G.11` the question of currentness.
