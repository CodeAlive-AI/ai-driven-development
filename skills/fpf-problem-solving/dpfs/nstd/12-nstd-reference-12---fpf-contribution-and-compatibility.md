## NSTD.Reference:12 - FPF contribution and compatibility

The definitions and general methods used by this edition are available in [FPF Core](../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md). In particular, `A.6.3.NAR` supplies the source-to-narrative relation and its ordinary and claim-specific uses; `C.2.8` supplies reader-qualified structural recovery; `A.19.ECS` and `C.16` supply the evaluation distinctions used in `NSTD.6`. The support maps and the relations below identify the additional content used for each relevant claim.

NSTD depends on those definitions for the uses stated here. A relevant change calls for reconsidering the affected narrative instruction or evaluation; it does not require restarting unrelated uses. Compatibility across a changed Core edition is a separate question about that use. `E.4.PFR` explains this dependency distinction and `G.11` the currentness question.
