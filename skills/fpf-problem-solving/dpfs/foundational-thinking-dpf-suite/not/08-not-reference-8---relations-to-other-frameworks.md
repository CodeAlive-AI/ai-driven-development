## NOT.Reference:8 - Relations to other frameworks

[Mathematical Thinking (MATH)](../math/_index.md) supplies objects, compositions, arguments and interpretations used when the represented construction is mathematical. [Computational Thinking (CMP)](../cmp/_index.md) supplies effective procedures, including interpreters and translations. Use NOT to design the expression conventions for those methods. When the work requires a computed consequence, it also needs an effective procedure for obtaining it.

[Mathematical Modeling (MMP)](../mmp/_index.md), [Physical Thinking (PHY)](../phy/_index.md) and FPF C.29 connect a subject with mathematical constructions and interpreted observations. B.5.MPC coordinates mathematical, physical and computational contributions when they are jointly needed. NOT.5–.6 help express those correspondences while keeping the related objects and claims distinguishable.

In FPF Core, C.37.MP relates the claims needed when different uses of a working method require complementary descriptions. Explanation Design (EXD) develops explanations; Human Capability Development (HCD), in the Human Capability Engineering Suite, develops and assesses human capabilities. They remain external contributions, used when the work needs that result. A notation repair can help any of them, while an operation missing from the method or from the reader's preparation needs the corresponding method or learning contribution.

The [Suite Reference (Foundational)](../suite-reference/_index.md) locates the publications and their shared architecture. Use the cited body's operative rules when its question arises. If an available edition changes those rules or no longer supplies the result your use needs, revisit that dependency even if the PatternID is unchanged.
