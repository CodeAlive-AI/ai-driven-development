The Suite also helps when the available concepts or methods cannot express a worthwhile question or construct an answer. A useful result may be a new mathematical object, a subject model or explanatory account, an algorithm, or a notation whose operations make further questions and Methods possible. A subject difficulty can lead to a new formal construction; a new construction can reveal a subject question that was previously unavailable. Research designs, observations and subject knowledge supply their own grounds, while the five contributions here construct and connect the means of inquiry. These results can enlarge both the Suite's own repertoire and knowledge used elsewhere in the Library. The same research programme may draw on several frameworks and contribute to several of them.

These practices contribute to the Library’s [canon for a shared human–AI culture of thinking and acting](../../../library/reference/02-library-reference-1---one-connected-language-several-kinds-o.md#libraryreference13---a-canon-for-shared-humanai-culture). People and AI agents can share methods for constructing and criticizing accounts while making different contributions to the same inquiry. A warranted result can expand what they can investigate and how they work together. The mathematical, physical and computational grounds of that result still come from the corresponding practice.

If you have a mathematical question already, start with [Mathematical Thinking (MATH)](../math/_index.md). If you need to turn a subject question into a mathematical one, start with [Mathematical Modeling (MMP)](../mmp/_index.md). For a physical account, consequence or observation, start with [Physical Thinking (PHY)](../phy/_index.md). For algorithm construction, interpretation or analysis, start with [Computational Thinking (CMP)](../cmp/_index.md). For expressions whose rules, interpretation or changes need repair, start with [Notational Engineering (NOT)](../not/_index.md). When the difficulty lies between contributions, use the question index below.
