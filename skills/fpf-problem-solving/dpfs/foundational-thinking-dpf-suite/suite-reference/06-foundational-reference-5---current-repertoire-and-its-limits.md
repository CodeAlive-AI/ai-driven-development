## Foundational.Reference:5 - Current repertoire and its limits

The Suite publishes **71 pattern bodies in five DPFs**: twenty in MATH, seventeen in MMP, ten in PHY, sixteen in CMP and eight in NOT. These methods form a selected repertoire.

| DPF | Published repertoire |
| --- | --- |
| [Mathematical Thinking (MATH)](../math/_index.md) - 20 patterns | Formation, operations and interpretations; proofs, witnesses, extraction and countermodels; bounds and convergent approximations; invariants, symmetry and variation; changed axioms and conjecture development. |
| [Mathematical Modeling (MMP)](../mmp/_index.md) - 17 patterns | Admissible formulations, structured unknown relations, information-dependent and continuing choices, probabilistic recording, inverse recovery, statistical inference, model criticism, intervention effects, counterfactual comparisons, observation design, reduced evolution, surrogates and coupled models; computable continuation when physical constraints change; comparison of interacting operations and construction of a way of working through mathematical transformation. |
| [Physical Thinking (PHY)](../phy/_index.md) - 10 patterns | Physical similarity and analogues; limits from permitted transformations; restrictions on unknown laws; effective descriptions by scales and couplings; evolution from balances and response laws; motion from variational principles; macroscopic behavior and fluctuations from weighted microscopic alternatives; measuring interactions; and tests separating rival accounts. |
| [Computational Thinking (CMP)](../cmp/_index.md) - 16 patterns | Reduction, recursive construction, sharing, search, relaxation, local updates and learning; approximation, sampling, representation and lower bounds; interpretation, abstraction and interacting composition; construction of computational units and operations for a required whole. |
| [Notational Engineering (NOT)](../not/_index.md) - 8 patterns | Expression requirements, formation and binding, interpretation, transformation, translation with loss recovery, complementary representations, redesign around difficult operations, and temporal or embodied notation. |

[MMP.21](../mmp/27-mmp-21---compare-interacting-operations-through-mathematical.md) and [MMP.22](../mmp/28-mmp-22---construct-a-way-of-working-through-mathematical-tra.md) are included in the seventeen MMP bodies: the first compares interacting operations mathematically, and the second constructs a changed way of working through a mathematical transformation. General methodological development remains supplied by Core. [FPF C.39.RQ](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/103-c-39-rq---construct-situated-method-requirements.md) constructs situated requirements; [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) constructs and compares performance and provision arrangements; [FPF B.1.5.CM](../../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/09-b-1-5-cm---construct-and-qualify-a-composite-method.md) qualifies the proposed composition; and [FPF A.3.1.CC](../../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/25-a-3-1-cc---check-the-coherence-of-a-method-claim.md) checks the relevant claims for coherence. A mathematical model contributes to these questions without replacing their practical conditions or decisions.

A new difficulty, source contribution, medium or working condition can require further methods or a specialized profile. Use a suitable source or existing specialist method when it already supplies the needed operation. When the current language cannot provide a promised result, the missing operation or connection remains an open need. It can require a specialist contribution, development of the method or a narrower answer.
