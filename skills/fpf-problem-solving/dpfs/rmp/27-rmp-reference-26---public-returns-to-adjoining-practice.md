## RMP.Reference:26 - Public returns to adjoining practice

Use a companion publication when the question belongs to an adjoining practice. Apply its method or use a qualified existing result for the case. Finding a reference identifies where the needed contribution is explained.

To read an FPF pattern, use the complete specification in a reader that can search for its full heading. If you need a local copy, [download the complete FPF-Spec.md](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/raw/refs/heads/main/FPF-Spec.md). The table gives the relevant headings and the result to bring back.

| Needed contribution | Where to continue and what to bring back |
| --- | --- |
| Develop a missing research strategy. | Use RMP.2:4.4 to work back from the required answer. The seal case shows how to repair a particular failed distinction. In the downloaded FPF specification, find `## C.39 - Find and Develop a Way to Obtain a Result` for the general recovery, construction and adaptation Method. Bring back an explained research evidence relation or the precise missing operation, with its conditions and useful stop. |
| Recover an answer from existing sources. | Start with RMP.1:4.3 in this publication. In the downloaded FPF specification, find `## F.1 - Find and Select Sources for a Current Question`, `## F.0.1 - Source-Local Meaning Recovery`, and `## A.10 - Evidence Graph Referring: Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph` for their contributing methods. Bring back the answer, sources, applicability and remaining uncertainty relevant to the research question. |
| Specify or examine the measurement account used by the inquiry. | In the downloaded FPF specification, find `## C.16 - Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM‑CHR)`. Bring back the measurand, applicable method and model, qualification, value and uncertainty needed for the research use, together with the competent domain or laboratory result. |
| Identify or improve a reusable research Method, or assess its fit. | Use [FPF C.39.MF](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/103-c-39-mf---choose-and-reopen-a-method-development-focus.md) to establish which Method is under consideration, [FPF A.3.1.WT](../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/24-a-3-1-wt---design-and-interpret-a-method-work-trial.md) for a trial in representative work when needed, and [FPF A.3.1.FT](../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/26-a-3-1-ft---qualify-method-fit-and-transfer.md) for fit or transfer. Bring the research question, its criticism requirement and any observed difficulty. With [RMP.9](39-rmp-9-decide-whether-and-how-to-improve-research-methods-and.md), assess suitability or decide whether a trial would add evidence the research needs. |
| Engineer a system or arrangement for obtaining a needed result. | Use [Systems Engineering](../engineering-dpf-suite/syse/_index.md) for an apparatus, laboratory or organization. Use [SYSE.24](../engineering-dpf-suite/syse/32-syse-24---choose-how-the-project-will-obtain-a-needed-engine.md) when how to obtain the result needs assessment or choice, and [SYSE.1](../engineering-dpf-suite/syse/20-syse-1---choose-and-reopen-the-project-system-of-interest.md) when the system of interest for a product undertaking needs clarification. Give the engineering work the relevant research requirements. Return the engineered contribution and its conditions to the inquiry. The research claim, including an independently useful knowledge finding, requires its own evidence. |
| Continue routine operating observation or response. | Use [Operations Management (OPS)](../management-dpf-suite/ops/_index.md) with the variables, response conditions and operating authority that apply. If a question about mechanism, validity or transfer remains unresolved and research is warranted, return that question to RMP.1. |

Use qualified sources for field-specific instruments, admissible observations, authenticity and safety. Apply the rights, consent and decision authority governing the case. The companion publications explain reusable methods. An actual measurement, permission or professional finding requires its own basis.
