## RMP.Reference:10 - Connect corpus synthesis and exact revision

Use [RMP.7](37-rmp-7---synthesize-a-research-evidence-corpus.md) when the question concerns what a body of evidence establishes. An adequate existing synthesis may answer it directly. Otherwise trace reports to their underlying studies or sources, including dependence, exclusions and missing contributions. Choose a synthesis appropriate to the question. It may pool estimates, compare without aggregation, develop an interpretation, reconcile historical accounts or relate findings of different kinds.

Two reports from one study can add detail about that study. Establish its dependence on other studies from their participants, data, comparisons and other relevant connections. A theme may describe a situated finding without giving its frequency in a population. When a common magnitude cannot be estimated, report what the corpus does establish and why the stronger estimate remains open.

Use [RMP.8](38-rmp-8---use-research-results-to-retain-or-revise-an-episteme.md) to establish whether an analysis or synthesis supports retaining or revising a wider claim, model or use. Match the subject and conditions on both sides and reconcile claims that rely on the result. Dating the factory decision leaves its later implementation open. Evidence for a laboratory wear relation can remain sound even when the transfer premise of a field prediction fails.

Give the answer, its basis, uncertainty and the limits on reliance. Where a correction affects a known use, reach the responsible party through a permitted route or apply it directly if your authority covers that use. A short linked explanation may suffice. If a further research opportunity arises, assess what it could establish and what obtaining it would cost.

For a result that includes a reusable Method, use [RMP.8's publication branch](38-rmp-8---use-research-results-to-retain-or-revise-an-episteme.md#rmp8441---make-a-research-method-contribution-recoverable-across-publication-forms) to identify the knowledge conveyed in an article, pattern or framework. Reconstructing a derivation, rerunning a calculation, obtaining new measurements and transferring the Method need different inputs and criteria for agreement. The [calibration case](38-rmp-8---use-research-results-to-retain-or-revise-an-episteme.md#rmp856---one-calibration-contribution-an-article-and-a-pattern) shows which steps and conditions must survive in a shortened account.
