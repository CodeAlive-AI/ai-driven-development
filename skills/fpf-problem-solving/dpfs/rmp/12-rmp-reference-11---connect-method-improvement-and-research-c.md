## RMP.Reference:11 - Connect Method improvement and research consequences

Use [RMP.9](39-rmp-9-decide-whether-and-how-to-improve-research-methods-and.md) when the question concerns a research Method or its practice. Locate the difficulty in the method, its description or implementation, a particular execution, the performer’s capability or an affected claim. Retain an adequate method. For a determinate defect, a correction and the relevant verification can finish the task.

For an open choice, compare attainable alternatives for the same research purpose and the criticism the research must withstand. A new comparison is worth obtaining when its result could change the warranted answer and the full cost is justified. Otherwise retain the supported finding and restrict any unsupported use. Declining a comparison settles whether to undertake it. Method qualification requires evidence about the method.

A corrected analysis can be returned through RMP.5 and RMP.8 while a wider Method question remains open. If a Method change affects an operational choice, credibility claim or synthesis, trace the dependency and retain other supported results. To treat new work as research, apply the RMP.1 membership conditions. Use RMP.9 when there is a Method question to answer.

Core distinguishes Method identity, trial, fit, worth and variant. A.3.1.MI addresses introduction in practice. [RMP.9's source account](39-rmp-9-decide-whether-and-how-to-improve-research-methods-and.md#rmp911---sota-echoing) connects these contributions to research. With C.11.MV, the present grounds may support a choice or a restricted set of candidates. Further inquiry depends on what can be obtained and whether its contribution justifies its cost. The research account can carry the choice and its grounds.
