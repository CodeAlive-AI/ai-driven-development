## RMP.Application:6 - Restore a service while investigating an unresolved mechanism

This constructed case distinguishes an immediate service task from a research question about its mechanism.

Suppose response times rise after a cache is enabled. An applicable operating procedure and current observations support restoring the previous configuration for today’s service. The authorized operator can act under that procedure. The general explanation of the delay may remain open.

A separate inquiry asks when contention for the cache’s shared lock can worsen response time even as remote requests decline. Suppose the sources leave this relation unsettled for the implementation and concurrency range. A scoped, criticizable explanation could change the model and later design guidance. This inquiry remains useful with today’s operating decision settled.

Use [the distinction between model, explanation, research and advice (PSDA.Reference:2.1)](../psda/03-psda-reference-2---how-the-constructions-connect.md#psdareference21---do-we-need-a-model-an-explanation-research-or-advice) to identify each needed contribution. Suppose one simulation reproduces the delay through lock contention and another through the mix of requests. The aggregate delay alone does not distinguish the explanations. Other observations may already do so. [PSDA.3](../psda/20-psda-3---construct-complementary-situation-and-option-models.md) identifies what each model assumes and supports. [RMP.2](32-rmp-2-choose-a-criticism-bearing-research-design-or-stop.md) helps choose observations that could distinguish their mechanism claims.

One candidate design observes lock waiting and request classes during a replay that preserves the relevant concurrency. Check measurement, access and the correspondence between replay and target workload. Serializing the requests would change the interaction being investigated. Choose a design that can discriminate where the answer is important enough to warrant the work.

For a chosen investigation, use [RMP.3](33-rmp-3---operationalize-the-research-design-without-replacing.md) for consequential operational choices, the qualified domain Method to carry it out, and [RMP.4](34-rmp-4---preserve-a-criticism-ready-research-trace.md) for the actual trace. Use [RMP.5](35-rmp-5---analyze-and-criticize-the-research-claim-under-its-m.md) to interpret the material, with [RMP.6](36-rmp-6---choose-a-credibility-test-that-bears-on-the-research.md) for a further credibility question when needed. An adequate existing result can supply a contribution directly. If no informative observation is attainable at worthwhile cost, the allocation question can finish with no new study and the mechanism unresolved. Today’s supported service restoration remains available.

Suppose later evidence supports the locking explanation within the tested concurrency range. Give model and design users that range and its grounds. For a new configuration, the service owner needs evidence of fit and the required deployment authority. [EXD.4](../exd/12-exd-4---co-construct-and-repair-an-explanation-in-dialogue.md) helps explain the relation between understanding a mechanism and qualifying a particular design for use. A workload outside the supported range requires another applicability judgement. Retain the result for conditions it covers.
