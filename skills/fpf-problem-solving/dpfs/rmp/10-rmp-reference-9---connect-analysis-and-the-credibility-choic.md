## RMP.Reference:9 - Connect analysis and the credibility choice

Use [RMP.5](35-rmp-5---analyze-and-criticize-the-research-claim-under-its-m.md) to follow the connection from a consequential piece of evidence through the method to the proposed claim. Use an adequate existing analysis when available. Identify important planned and later choices, contrary evidence and uncertainty. State the claim the material supports and what a stronger claim would still require. A limited interpretation or finding that the answer remains open may be sufficient.

Use [RMP.6](36-rmp-6---choose-a-credibility-test-that-bears-on-the-research.md) for a question about the credibility of an identified claim. Start with the claim, its basis, its use and the specific threat. Reproduction, replication, robustness, validation for a use, corroboration and transfer examine different relations. Compare what an attainable result could establish with the costs of access, preparation, performance, interpretation, protection, shared resources and displaced work.

A test can be chosen, specified, performed and interpreted at different times. Lack of access is evidence about feasibility. An inconclusive result leaves the tested proposition open. A decision to finish without a new test retains its uncertainty. If a feasible comparison later becomes worthwhile, revisit that choice. For a disputed requirement, distinguish the value of the function it protects from the authority to amend the rule.
