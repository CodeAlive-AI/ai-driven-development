## IS.Reference:10 - What framework quality does and does not establish

[FPF E.21](../../sections/11-part-e---the-fpf-constitution-and-authoring-guides/61-e-21---fpf-pattern-quality-evaluation-characteristicspace.md) supplies judgement of a pattern's contribution, while [FPF E.4.DPF.DA](../../sections/11-part-e---the-fpf-constitution-and-authoring-guides/10-e-4-dpf-da---evaluate-a-dpf-or-lpf-package-for-its-declared.md) concerns this connected framework against its declared practice. [FPF F.19](../../sections/12-part-f---the-unification-suite-concept-sets-sensecells-and-system-role/24-f-19---ontology-first-plain-technical-rewriting.md) addresses wording that prevents recovery of an action, participant or condition. A good local result, a readable explanation and a justified source use answer different questions; each result supports only its own scope. These framework-quality judgements explain what the publication warrants; they are not compulsory evaluation steps in every local facilitation engagement.

For a local support strategy, the actual target, help and receiving result are evaluated through [FPF A.3.1.MI](../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/28-a-3-1-mi---introduce-a-method-into-practice-and-decide-what.md) and the appropriate professional practice. A framework-quality judgement does not establish the effectiveness of that local strategy.
