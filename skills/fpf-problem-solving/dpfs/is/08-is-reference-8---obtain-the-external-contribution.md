## IS.Reference:8 - Obtain the external contribution

Implementation-support work uses subject Methods, organizational arrangements and learning programmes supplied by external practices. Obtain and use those contributions under the conditions of the receiving work.

The links locate the supplying publications; for a reproducible use, identify the edition or revision actually read. Their PatternIDs locate the relied-on bodies. Read the relevant Method and its conditions when you must perform it; use a sufficient existing result directly when its applicability and actual availability are already established. If a linked body or required expertise cannot be obtained, preserve the dependent limit instead of assuming it has been supplied.

One person or AI may supply several such contributions, but their joint demand and actual qualification still matter.
