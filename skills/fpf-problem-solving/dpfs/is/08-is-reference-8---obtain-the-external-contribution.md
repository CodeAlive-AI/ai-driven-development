## IS.Reference:8 - Obtain the external contribution

Implementation-support work uses subject Methods, organizational arrangements and learning programmes from other practices. Use or obtain the contributions the receiving work requires, under the conditions that make them applicable.

The links and PatternIDs locate the relevant publications and sections. For a reproducible use of a particular text, identify the edition or revision consulted. Establish that the performer has the knowledge, access and conditions needed for the operation. Consult an applicable description or demonstration where needed. Use an existing result directly when it fits the receiving task. If the necessary contribution remains unavailable, report which conclusion or action depends on it.
