## IS.Reference:2 - Make useful practice obtainable in real work

Use the general helping Methods in this framework when people or systems are expected to use a practice, but a description, invitation, tool or training event has not made the intended work obtainable. A practice may be new to its recipients, a familiar way that has become difficult to continue, or a proposed way whose local fitness remains under examination. The support practitioner helps obtain the contributions that make its use possible and worthwhile, examines what actually happens, and changes or ends the help accordingly.

Start with one receiving use. Ask the performer to show what they are trying to obtain, the occasion in which they need it and the first contribution they cannot obtain. A missing permission may need an authorized decision; a difficult judgement may need an expert; a missing personal ability may need practice and feedback; an unsuitable method may need revision or replacement. The first useful result can be one repaired connection.

Within implementation facilitation, providing and revising help changes a condition for establishing, restoring, adapting or continuing a recipient's practice. It does not cover every professional service merely because that service supplies a useful result. If an adequate service already makes the wanted result obtainable, use it directly. A helper can arrange qualified performance as part of a support strategy without requiring the recipient to acquire that specialty; arranging the support and performing the target operation remain different contributions. The field also differs from designing every part of the organization or choosing the recipient's whole development strategy.

The practitioner needs to understand the proposed support operations, recover the receiving work with its participants, and obtain qualified subject judgement where the decision depends on it. They need not personally master every supported profession. A source, expert or capable AI can supply a contribution, but its availability, fitness and permission to act remain conditions of the proposed arrangement.
