## IS.Edition:2 - Product-series rule

The **Implementation Facilitation Principles Framework** product series covers editions for those who help recipients make evidence-informed changes usable or develop the capacity to conduct later changes. Admission to the series requires an effective constitution and an admission decision. This edition belongs to the continuing IS series.

Select editions that connect agreement about the help, diagnosis of the difficulty, provision and adaptation of help, interpretation of later use through the Core introduction Method, and continuation or ending of support. They must also cover how knowledge from other parties can affect a change, how facilitation fits its participants and context, and how recipients can learn to facilitate later changes. For admission, establish that the complete edition can be used independently and that its source limits and required suppliers have been qualified. Distinguish the recipient’s practice, the help and its provision. Preserve direct use of adequate services, support across independent participants and A.3.1.MI’s use by an independent implementer. Use the responsible practices for new subject knowledge, development of the whole, organization design and human learning.

Later editions may refine, add, split or redistribute Methods while preserving the support practice, independent bounded use and the limits of each source and supplier. To admit an edition, establish its relation to the source edition (`EpistemeEditionRelation`), its conformance to these rules and the effective admission decision. Apply the same rule to forks, translations and other derivatives. Changing the governed practice or these identity conditions requires another series. A change of title, publication home, Suite membership or maintenance arrangement alone preserves that identity.

When a necessary use is lost, source grounds fail or a required contribution no longer reaches its recipient, review the affected qualification and decide whether to repair the offering, correct the admission claim or retire it. During the life of the series an admitted edition remains a historical member, including after it is superseded, withdrawn or becomes unavailable. Explicit retirement ends the series and current membership, while its history remains. A later active series requires a new constitution. Establish any ongoing maintenance, responsible party, future edition or continued availability from evidence for that claim.
