## IS.Reference:5 - One performer, several practices; several performers, one result

A local leader, internal specialist, external provider, independent practitioner or qualified AI can perform implementation-support work. Several parties can contribute under their own organizational arrangements and decision authority.

One residency coordinator, for example, can develop a method during a development project and later organize programmes and cohorts as ordinary provision. Project membership, method of work, capability and organizational assignment are different relations.

The target operation still needs its actual subject expertise. The same facilitator can help two professions investigate a difficulty without being qualified to decide both professions' technical questions. Conversely, a sufficiently capable AI can supply a previously scarce contribution. Permissions, physical access, reliable evidence and the intended holder's own performance remain separate questions.

Use C.32.MWA.PP when materially different configurations of performance, Method co-use and provision must be compared. Its selected configuration and conditions feed the support strategy; proposed relations still need realization. A.15.8.MS supplies method-use support, and C.32.MWA distinguishes the interacting structures where that changes the comparison. Obtain OCE and Operations results for organization, allocation and continuing service. Use their sufficient results directly, without a second comparison or introduction.
