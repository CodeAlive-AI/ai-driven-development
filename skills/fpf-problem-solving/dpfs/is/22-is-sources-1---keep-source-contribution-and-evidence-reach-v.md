## IS.Sources:1 - Keep source contribution and evidence reach visible

The [Implementation Support Practitioner Competencies](https://implementationpractice.org/resources/implementation-support-practitioner-competencies/) resource page gives access to the [Practice Guide to Supporting Implementation](https://cippro.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ISP-Practice-Guide-v1-10.27.22.pdf), dated December 2020. Its account of helping informs IS.1–3 and IS.5. When using these contributions outside human services, assess their relevance to the intended practice and conditions.

A.3.1.MI:11 identifies sources on implementation, adaptation, process evaluation and causal comparison. The Methods that draw on them state the questions they contribute and the limits of transfer. To assess a local strategy, use grounds for its feasibility in that organization and, when claiming an effect, for the causal relationship under the conditions in question.

Harbor Design, the translator and the independent repairers are constructed cases. Use their assumptions to work through the operations and comparisons. For a report about real practice, identify the work and evidence that support the requested claim under its conditions. For example, a record may establish that help was supplied. Establish a local outcome or a causal effect from evidence relevant to that further claim.
