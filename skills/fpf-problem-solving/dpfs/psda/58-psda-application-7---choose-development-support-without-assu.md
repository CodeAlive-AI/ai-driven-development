## PSDA.Application:7 - Choose development support without assuming an office

The following example supplies the staffing, permissions and time available for the comparison.

Four project teams repeatedly revise commitments after discovering an unchecked premise in the estimates. The director proposes a development office. The six-week undertaking has two aims, improving the estimation method and helping the leads apply and develop it while delivery continues. The teams need to form, test and revise the premises behind their estimates.

Using [PSDA.1](18-psda-1---generate-plural-problem-formulations.md) and [PSDA.2](19-psda-2---set-and-reopen-the-problem-boundary.md), the adviser distinguishes missing technical knowledge, too little opportunity to challenge a commitment, and difficulty turning a weak signal into a useful inquiry. Each points to a different possible need. Records or a participant’s correction can establish what occurred. Attributing the repeated failure to one mechanism needs evidence for that causal claim.

With [FPF C.40.JD:4.5](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/121-c-40-jd---develop-a-way-its-holder-and-its-provision-togethe.md#c40jd45---synthesize-the-organization-from-the-work-and-conditions), identify the work the teams need to retain locally and the specialist help, common resources and wider decisions they require. Use [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) and [OCE.8](../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md) to compare proposed ways to perform and supply the whole result, testing their feasibility. Here the evidence supports the managers’ coaching competence, and each has one protected hour per week for local work. The common subject question still requires specialist help.

Compare two arrangements for six weeks. Distributed support uses the existing managers and a shared specialist. The proposed office initially uses the same specialist and local practice and adds two weekly coordination hours. No additional specialist capacity has been secured. The table gives the recurring specialist work. In the distributed proposal the specialist can prepare a single result and make it available to every lead before that lead’s own protected hour. The leads can use it separately. The office proposes the same activities plus coordination.

| Specialist contribution | Managers with distributed help | Proposed office using the same specialist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing commitments | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Specialist inquiry yielding one reusable result | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Four distinct case reviews, half an hour each | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Additional office coordination | 0 hours | 2 hours |
| Total against six available hours | 6 hours | 8 hours |

The four teams need the same technical distinction and may share the specialist’s result. Each lead has time in their protected hour to use it and take part in their half-hour case review. Assume the specialist can schedule the four reviews in those windows after preparing the result and alongside the two hours already committed. This supplies a joint arrangement that fits six specialist-hours. One report can serve all four leads because the brief permits each to use it at a different time.

If the work instead requires every lead to attend a two-hour inquiry together, the same specialist total leaves the timetable open until attendance is checked. With only one hour authorized per lead, that session cannot be delivered as proposed. Even two hours per lead would require a common window. Reopen the proposal, the time available or the form of support that the client will accept.

Under the commissioned use of the reusable result and the stated timetable, the adviser recommends distributed support for the six weeks. The proposed office needs eight specialist-hours against six available. Concentrated demand, a reusable service, another qualified provider or a different coordination cost could change the comparison. An external provider whose access and availability are unknown remains conditional. With [PSDA.9](26-psda-9---test-robustness-and-sensitivity-of-decision-alterna.md), test consequential changes when needed. Use [PSDA.14](30-psda-14---prepare-and-return-a-decision-support-recommendati.md) to return the recommendation to the director for the separate choice.

If confidentiality later requires a separately produced two-hour inquiry for each of the four teams, those inquiries alone require eight specialist-hours. With two hours for case reviews and two already committed, this version needs twelve against six. It cannot meet the changed commission with this provider and allocation. Examine permitted alternatives such as another qualified provider or a changed service. The technical distinction already established remains available wherever its disclosure and use are permitted.

When the chosen arrangement is authorized for use, assess its performance from the work carried out. In a contrasting case, examine how a lead identifies a consequential premise, obtains the missing subject answer and forms a justified commitment. This may support a conclusion about that lead’s contribution under the observed conditions. Evaluate the revised method and any stronger capability claim on their respective grounds. Use [PSDA.15](31-psda-15---arrange-decision-follow-up-or-reassess-advice-afte.md) and [FPF C.40.RQ](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/119-c-40-rq---requalify-changed-opportunities.md) to revisit advice or opportunities that depended on a changed premise.
