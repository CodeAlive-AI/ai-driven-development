## PSDA.Application:7 - Choose development support without assuming an office

This is a constructed illustration; its working conditions are stipulated.

Four project teams repeatedly revise commitments after discovering that estimates depended on an unchecked premise. The director proposes a development office. The receiving question is broader: how can the teams improve how they form, test and revise those premises while current delivery continues? The six-week undertaking is meant to improve the estimation Method and the leads' ability to use and further develop it.

An adviser first distinguishes three explanations: missing technical knowledge, insufficient opportunity to question a commitment, and difficulty constructing a useful inquiry from a weak signal. They imply different contributions. Records and participants' corrected accounts can identify which situations occurred. Establishing which mechanism causes the recurring failure may require a further qualified inquiry; the adviser does not claim that a concern map has established it. Use [PSDA.1](18-psda-1---generate-plural-problem-formulations.md) and [PSDA.2](19-psda-2---set-and-reopen-the-problem-boundary.md) to preserve these differences.

[FPF C.40.JD:4.5](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/118-c-40-jd---develop-a-way-its-holder-and-its-provision-togethe.md#c40jd45---synthesize-the-organization-from-the-work-and-conditions) supplies the organizational construction: retain the needed local work, then obtain the professional help, common means and wider decisions. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) compares whole performance and provision arrangements; [OCE.8](../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md) develops the organizational choice of human, AI and provider contributions. In this case, existing evidence supports the managers' relevant coaching competence, and each has one protected hour per week for the local work. Specialist help remains necessary for the shared subject question. These are premises of the comparison.

Compare two proposed arrangements for the present horizon. Distributed support uses those managers and a shared specialist. A dedicated office would initially use the same specialist and local practice, adding two weekly hours of coordination; no other specialist has been obtained. The relevant recurring specialist demands are:

| Specialist contribution | Managers with distributed help | Proposed office using the same specialist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing commitments | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| One genuinely shared inquiry session | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Four distinct case reviews, half an hour each | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Additional office coordination | 0 hours | 2 hours |
| Total against six available hours | 6 hours | 8 hours |

The shared session is common only because the teams need the same distinction and may use the material together. Their local cases remain separate. Protected team time, permitted material, manager authority and the ability to receive the specialist's result are stipulated as available. The table tests one capacity constraint. In this case, the other premises and the six-hour total make the distributed arrangement feasible.

Under those conditions, the adviser recommends distributed support for the six-week undertaking. The proposed office does not fit the present specialist capacity. Concentrated demand, a genuinely reusable service, another qualified provider or changed coordination costs could alter the comparison. An outside provider whose access and availability are unresolved remains a conditional alternative rather than being silently ranked last. [PSDA.9](26-psda-9---test-robustness-and-sensitivity-of-decision-alterna.md) tests those consequential changes; [PSDA.14](30-psda-14---prepare-and-return-a-decision-support-recommendati.md) carries the bounded recommendation to the director's separate choice.

Suppose confidentiality later prevents the common session. Four separate two-hour sessions would require eight hours, plus two hours of case reviews and two existing hours: twelve in total. The distributed arrangement now fails the same six-hour constraint. Reopen its provision or scope, not the already supported technical distinction.

When a feasible arrangement has been chosen and authorized, the teams still have to perform the work. Observe whether a lead can recover a consequential premise, obtain the missing subject answer and form a qualified commitment in an actual contrasting case. That supplies bounded evidence about the intended change. Decide separately what that evidence supports about the revised Method and the leads' capability, and what a next undertaking would need. [PSDA.15](31-psda-15---arrange-decision-follow-up-or-reassess-advice-afte.md) and [FPF C.40.RQ](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/116-c-40-rq---requalify-changed-opportunities.md) govern the affected advice and opportunity returns from changed grounds.
