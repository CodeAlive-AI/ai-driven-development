## PSDA.Reference:15 - Development-direction advising within a wider undertaking

Within a wider development undertaking, this framework's development-direction advising profile supplies bounded problem formulations, alternatives, decision models and recommendations; it does not own the whole development undertaking. [FPF C.40.OD](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/117-c-40-od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development.md) combines the developed Library contributions to problematisation, Method construction, transformation-flow description, capability development and cultural continuation. B.5.PI, C.39, C.40.CD/CU, E.18/E.18.NET, C.32.MWA, E.23.CDI and C.36/C.36.RP retain their precise subjects and conditions. A.12 retains the internal acting/changed-parts explanation where its premises hold. HCD, OCE, the Core Method constructions and the actual target practice retain the operations that make a subject-specific transition attainable. C.40.OD:11 compares these connections with direct use of C.32.MWA.PP, C.40.CU and the subject Method. Use that simpler combination when it supplies the needed result at lower cost. The section also identifies the CIPD and MIT CISR organizational sources and explains how their contributions were adapted. The synthesis helps connect the contributions and revise the construction when conditions change. In particular, the same-agent explanation in C.40.JD:4.2 connects available capability to a development operation and its receiving use; it neither replaces Reflexive Split nor infers acquisition from the description of a change.

STR.16 uses STR.8/11/14 for contribution and commitment construction, RMP.1/2/8 for independent epistemic purposes and attainable inquiry, MA.1–3/8 for scarce demand and time-related obligations, and OCE.8/9/11 for obtaining and realizing support while current work continues. STR.16:11 describes its comparison with the OECD mission-portfolio source and the limits on transfer. An independent scientific inquiry keeps its own purpose and acceptance conditions alongside a commercial release.

The 2024 role-community essays cited by C.40.OD describe overlapping organizational and community relations, participant journeys and competing formal and informal influence. These conceptual sources invite the adviser to ask how participants' own purposes, burdens and authority affect a proposed provision.

Examine these constructions through their intermediate results, combined conditions and changed cases. Their worked examples stipulate those conditions. Use evidence from the relevant setting for claims about organizational effectiveness or a provider's available resources. Reopen a connection when a change in the subject, supply or intended use invalidates it. A direct method can suffice for its question.
