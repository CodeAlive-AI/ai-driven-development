## PSDA.Advising:6 - Keep a qualified live candidate set

If the useful future contribution itself is unsettled, use an adequate available opportunity or develop the missing contribution with the [opportunity-construction Method (FPF C.40.OCN)](../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/111-c-40-ocn---construct-a-bounded-opportunity.md). It searches neighboring uses, distinguishes a niche, a proposed provision promise and a worthwhile problem, constructs directions and supports, and exposes dependencies and reachability.

If the candidate directions are not sufficiently specified, use [PSDA.5](22-psda-5---generate-decision-alternatives.md) to make the advice-relevant alternatives explicit. Keep each direction's status explicit: a feasible direction under stated conditions, a conditional opportunity, a candidate probe or a fragment retained for combination. Keep ruled-out configurations and named gaps visible as such, not as eligible alternatives. Do not let an attractive but conditional opportunity enter a decision-ready set as if its dependencies had obtained.

Retain materially different ways to develop, obtain support, allocate effort, stage a change or continue the present arrangement when they bear on the question. Distinguish different means of supplying the same result from different worthwhile problems.

[OCE.8](../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md) can supply the organizational same-result arrangement comparison after the required result, receiving use, horizon and acceptance premise are stable enough. It returns a changed or unjustified result premise to its owner; it does not supply this profile's whole opportunity search.

Use C.18 for a question about generation, an archive or a frontier under declared operators, descriptors and a retention or comparison rule. Use C.19 for a live pool governed by its current policy. To recommend a probe, identify what it could distinguish, its feasible exposure and protected conditions. To carry it out, establish the applicable authorization and operating conditions. Return a supported direction or limit when a probe is unavailable or would add too little.
