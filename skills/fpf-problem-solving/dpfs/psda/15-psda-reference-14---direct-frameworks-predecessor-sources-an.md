## PSDA.Reference:14 - Direct frameworks, predecessor sources, and honest omissions

This framework supplies formulation, model, value, uncertainty and comparison constructions through [PSDA.1–9](_index.md#pattern-index), and professional engagement, participation, facilitation, recommendation and follow-up through [PSDA.11–15](27-psda-11---bound-the-decision-support-engagement-and-authorit.md). [Human Capability Development (HCD)](../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/_index.md) supplies personal demand, programme and evidence contributions. [OCE.8](../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md) supplies whole-arrangement construction and comparison when that same-result question is current. Select the contribution on which the claim depends.

The earlier opportunity-construction description, [advising profile](37-development-direction-advising.md) and [source-responsibility account](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources43---predecessor-source-account-access) remain distinct sources, with their public expressions and access limits identified below. Their construction moves are carried by C.40.OI, C.40.NU, C.40.PC, C.40.DC, C.40.JF, C.40.OP, their engagement conditions by PSDA.19, and their affected-opportunity return by C.40.RQ. Use [PSDA.14](30-psda-14---prepare-and-return-a-decision-support-recommendati.md) for the qualified recommendation and [PSDA.15](31-psda-15---arrange-decision-follow-up-or-reassess-advice-afte.md) for follow-up or reassessment.

The [Foundations programme](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources42---foundations-programme-source-access) and [comparison](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources42---foundations-programme-source-access) illustrate a programme design considered before a particular person's development question. A learner-oriented narrative can arrange explanations and practice for learning; a compact pattern body helps a practitioner select a move for a working difficulty. For a particular person's capability, programme fit or learning result, use the relevant direct practices and observations.

For a question about career design, motivation and learning science, real-options or specialist investment valuation, professional certification, legal or safety compliance, or implementation, use the applicable practice. If the inquiry needs a contribution that existing evidence cannot supply, seek it from a suitable source or explain how its absence limits the answer.
