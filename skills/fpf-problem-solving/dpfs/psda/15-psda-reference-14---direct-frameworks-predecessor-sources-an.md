## PSDA.Reference:14 - Direct frameworks, predecessor sources, and honest omissions

This framework supplies formulation, model, value, uncertainty and comparison constructions through [PSDA.1–9](_index.md#pattern-index), and professional engagement, participation, facilitation, recommendation and follow-up through [PSDA.11–15](27-psda-11---bound-the-decision-support-engagement-and-authorit.md). [Human Capability Development (HCD)](../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/_index.md) supplies personal demand, programme and evidence contributions. [OCE.8](../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md) supplies whole-arrangement construction and comparison when that same-result question is current. Their use is claim-specific.

The earlier opportunity-construction description, [advising profile](37-development-direction-advising.md) and [source-responsibility account](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources43---predecessor-source-account-access) remain distinct sources, with their public expressions and access limits identified below. Their construction moves are carried by C.40.OI, C.40.NU, C.40.PC, C.40.DC, C.40.JF, C.40.OP, their actual engagement conditions by PSDA.19, and their affected-opportunity return by C.40.RQ. Use [PSDA.14](30-psda-14---prepare-and-return-a-decision-support-recommendati.md) for the qualified recommendation and [PSDA.15](31-psda-15---arrange-decision-follow-up-or-reassess-advice-afte.md) for follow-up or reassessment.

The [Foundations programme](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources42---foundations-programme-source-access) and [comparison](63-psda-sources-4---source-access-for-the-development-applicati.md#psdasources42---foundations-programme-source-access) illustrate a programme design considered before a particular person's development question. A learner-oriented narrative can arrange explanations and practice for learning; a compact pattern body helps a practitioner select a move for a working difficulty. For a particular person's capability, programme fit or learning result, use the relevant direct practices and observations.

Broader career-design exercises, motivation and learning-science accounts, real-options valuation, specialist investment valuation, complete professional certification, legal/safety compliance and domain implementation are not reproduced here. Their omission is not permission to bypass them when a receiving claim needs them. Return that exact question to its actual current owner.
