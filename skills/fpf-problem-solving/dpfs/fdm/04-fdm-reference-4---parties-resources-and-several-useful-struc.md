## FDM.Reference:4 - Parties, resources and several useful structures

A founder-group account, legal-entity account and portfolio-risk account can all be useful. They answer different questions. A common founder can matter to correlated exposures, governance or expected support; the relation alone does not identify another entity as the debtor or make its funds available.

Give the criterion and use for each grouping. To sum balances, check the parties, dates, currencies, restrictions and relations the aggregate depends on. One organizational tree may suffice for a report and omit a relation another question needs. Use [SIE.5 through SIE.6][SIE] to resolve identity or composition where necessary.

Suppose X and Y have 100 between them in the same currency. Both balances are nonnegative, available by the due time, and there are no other claims, transfers or sources. Can X pay 60? Balances of 20 and 80 or of 80 and 20 both fit the total and give opposite answers. If Y can hold at most 40, X has at least 60 and can pay. If a different report puts the total available to both at no more than 55, X cannot pay 60. Neither decision needs an exact split. A reporting elimination of an intragroup receivable and payable affects that report. For a change to the debt itself, establish the effective agreement or event.

A financial service can depend on several kinds of relation. An agreement can contain a guarantee clause. The creditor holds the right it grants, and the guarantor may perform by paying the creditor. The creditor may then spend the cash received on equipment. A service model can connect those actions, parties and positions without treating the creditor or the acquired equipment as parts of the agreement. To choose how far to follow each relation, change the use of the same payment: its receipt can answer a collection question, while a question about discharge also needs the application of the receipt under the terms.

For a common funding facility, establish when the authorized allocator can identify the account to fund. A payment service may act under an existing mandate. If release requires a named officer’s approval, include the time needed to obtain it. A facility of 100 can meet a single request for 60 from either X or Y if the authorized allocator can learn the payee and direct the funds there in time. If funds must be reserved by account in advance and cannot move later, a guaranteed 60 at each possible account would require 120. If both may call simultaneously, one timely message still leaves the facility 20 short. A scenario forecast can use stated assumptions. For a service-probability target, identify the relevant population and probability of timely service, including both demand and the ability to respond. A supported lower bound can establish the target. Service in every allowed outcome meets any probability threshold up to one.
