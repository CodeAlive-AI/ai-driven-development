## MDPE.Application:3 - Compose movement from a physical relation or a scientific image

Use [MDPE.29](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md) to turn a physical relation or a scientific image into movement material, vary it, compose transitions and judge the resulting whole. The two linked scores give different uses of a scientific source.

### MDPE.Application:3.1 - Make the source useful to a composition

Decide whether the source constrains physical realization, suggests an artistic operation, or serves both in different parts. [MDPE.29:4](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe294---solution) shows how to compose the phrase, develop variations, join them and revise the whole for the chosen relation.

### MDPE.Application:3.2 - Case A — Keep an apex in the music without demanding a longer flight

[MDPE.29:5.1](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2951---case-a--keep-an-apex-in-the-music-without-demanding-a-longer-flight) compares the flight required by a model of an unsupported performer with the contour a performer can trace with a hand while remaining supported. It derives the physical demands and gives a twenty-four-second score. Choose by whether the brief requires bodily flight or the visible contour of an apex.

### MDPE.Application:3.3 - Case B — Use relativity to generate a spatial duet, not to explain the dancers' bodies

[MDPE.29:5.2](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2952---case-b--use-relativity-to-generate-a-spatial-duet-not-to-explain-the-dancers-bodies) takes compression along one spatial axis as an artistic rule. It develops two phrases, their transition and a thirty-two-second duet. The score changes the prescribed paths according to the chosen rule. The physical principle that prompted the rule has its own domain of application.

### MDPE.Application:3.4 - Adapt the construction without losing its distinction

When a physical assumption changes or a phrase no longer joins its neighbour, use [MDPE.29:4.3](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2943---adapt-the-construction-without-losing-its-distinction) to revise what depends on it. PHY can supply the physical account, SOM the bodily contrast, RHY the timing, and HCD a task for developing a human performer’s capability when that is needed.
