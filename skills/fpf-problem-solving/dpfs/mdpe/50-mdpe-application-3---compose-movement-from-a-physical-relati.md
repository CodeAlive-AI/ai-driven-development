## MDPE.Application:3 - Compose movement from a physical relation or a scientific image

Use [MDPE.29](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md) to turn a source idea into movement material, investigate variations and compose the whole. The following questions lead to its complete construction and worked scores.

### MDPE.Application:3.1 - Make the source useful to a composition

Choose whether the source constrains physical realization or inspires an artistic operation. [MDPE.29:4](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe294---solution) develops the phrase, consequential changes, joins, whole composition and revision of the selected relation.

### MDPE.Application:3.2 - Case A — Keep an apex in the music without demanding a longer flight

[MDPE.29:5.1](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2951---case-a--keep-an-apex-in-the-music-without-demanding-a-longer-flight) compares actual flight with a supported hand contour, derives their different physical demands and constructs the full twenty-four-second score. The selected alternative depends on whether flight itself or the visible contour is required.

### MDPE.Application:3.3 - Case B — Use relativity to generate a spatial duet, not to explain the dancers' bodies

[MDPE.29:5.2](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2952---case-b--use-relativity-to-generate-a-spatial-duet-not-to-explain-the-dancers-bodies) develops a one-dimension compression into two phrases, an explicit transition and a thirty-two-second duet. The artistic operation and the source's physical meaning remain distinguishable.

### MDPE.Application:3.4 - Adapt the construction without losing its distinction

[MDPE.29:4.3](24-mdpe-29---compose-movement-from-a-physical-relation-or-scien.md#mdpe2943---adapt-the-construction-without-losing-its-distinction) returns changed physical premises and phrase joins to their construction. PHY, SOM, RHY and HCD contribute when the actual composing or performing difficulty needs their different results.
