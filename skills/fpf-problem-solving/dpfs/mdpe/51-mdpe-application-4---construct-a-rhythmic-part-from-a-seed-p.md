## MDPE.Application:4 - Construct a rhythmic part from a seed, phase and action rule

Use [MDPE.30](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md) to place a cyclic seed against an accompaniment, assign actions and sounds, and establish what the resulting part supports. A change in the entry or phrase length may call for a new phase, allocation or splice.

### MDPE.Application:4.1 - Generate the small seed and choose its phase

[MDPE.30:5.1](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3051---generate-the-small-seed-and-choose-its-phase) derives onsets 0, 3 and 6 in eight pulses. Rotating them forward by two gives 0, 2 and 5. Both onset sets include the required pulse 0. The rotated part also leaves pulse 6 free for another instrument’s cue. [Section 4.1](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3041---recover-the-seed-and-place-it-in-the-receiving-music) gives the placement operation and the inputs it requires.

### MDPE.Application:4.2 - Turn the onsets into actual hand and sound parts

[MDPE.30:5.2](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3052---turn-the-onsets-into-actual-hand-and-sound-parts) prescribes alternating hands on audible attacks across the boundary. Three attacks per eight pulses switch the starting hand at the next cycle. The two-cycle timbral exchange can be compared with a single-sound part on the same onsets.

### MDPE.Application:4.3 - Change the receiving condition without confusing the two action rules

[MDPE.30:5.3](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3053---change-the-receiving-condition-without-confusing-the-two-action-rules) changes the allocation when the brief requires the low sound from an actual right-hand stroke on pulse 0 of every eight-pulse cycle. [Section 4.2](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3042---construct-the-action-and-sound-allocation-across-the-boundary) distinguishes alternating on audible attacks from alternating on every pulse, including silent motions.

### MDPE.Application:4.4 - Extend the phrase while preserving a chosen action relation

[MDPE.30:5.4](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3054---extend-the-phrase-while-preserving-a-chosen-action-relation) inserts a copy of the four-pulse transition into the eight-pulse part. The resulting twelve-pulse phrase keeps the two three-pulse gaps between right- and left-hand attack blocks. To assess its use, check it against the receiving accompaniment and the performer’s available actions.

### MDPE.Application:4.5 - Return the useful part and its limits

[MDPE.30:4.4](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3044---establish-what-the-part-supports-in-the-receiving-music) can return the constructed score, an assessment of whether it fits the receiving music, or a result from a performance under stated conditions. Use applicable evidence of performance, or undertake a feasible receiving trial when the remaining question warrants it. Application 5.3 distinguishes the structure of a method, what someone can recover from its description and what a performer has learned to do.
