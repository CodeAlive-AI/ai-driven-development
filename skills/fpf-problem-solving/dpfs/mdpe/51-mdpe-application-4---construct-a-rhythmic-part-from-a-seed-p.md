## MDPE.Application:4 - Construct a rhythmic part from a seed, phase and action rule

Use [MDPE.30](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md) when cyclic material still needs a place in the accompaniment, an action rule and a sound allocation. Its complete method explains what changes when the receiving entry or span changes.

### MDPE.Application:4.1 - Generate the small seed and choose its phase

[MDPE.30:5.1](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3051---generate-the-small-seed-and-choose-its-phase) fully derives the three-onset, eight-pulse seed, then changes its phase to keep a fixed entry and another instrument's cue clear. [Section 4.1](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3041---recover-the-seed-and-place-it-in-the-receiving-music) gives the general placement operation and its input boundary.

### MDPE.Application:4.2 - Turn the onsets into actual hand and sound parts

[MDPE.30:5.2](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3052---turn-the-onsets-into-actual-hand-and-sound-parts) continues alternating audible attacks through the cycle boundary and compares the resulting two-cycle timbral exchange with a simpler allocation.

### MDPE.Application:4.3 - Change the receiving condition without confusing the two action rules

[MDPE.30:5.3](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3053---change-the-receiving-condition-without-confusing-the-two-action-rules) changes the allocation when the right-hand sound must return at every eight-pulse beginning. [Section 4.2](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3042---construct-the-action-and-sound-allocation-across-the-boundary) explains attack-wise and pulse-wise alternation beyond this seed.

### MDPE.Application:4.4 - Extend the phrase while preserving a chosen action relation

[MDPE.30:5.4](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3054---extend-the-phrase-while-preserving-a-chosen-action-relation) derives a twelve-pulse smooth-toggle part by splicing the transition. It then returns to the receiving accompaniment to decide whether the new part can be used.

### MDPE.Application:4.5 - Return the useful part and its limits

[MDPE.30:4.4](25-mdpe-30---construct-a-rhythmic-part-from-a-seed-phase-and-ac.md#mdpe3044---try-the-receiving-part-and-retain-the-usable-result) returns the constructed part or supported receiving use and selects a receiving trial when needed. Application 5.3 develops the related distinction between a method's structure, understanding its description and acquiring performing capability.
