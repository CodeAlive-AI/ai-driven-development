## MDPE.Reference:8 - What this publication foregrounds and leaves outside

This publication foregrounds five connected problem families: selecting the engineering subject and
style-development architecture; creating performance possibilities and preparing the performing whole;
performing and making decision-relevant differences observable and selectable; transferring and keeping a
reproducible practice; and developing the supporting environment, resolving practice conflicts, performing a
selected local change, preserving alternatives, testing trajectories, and choosing the next bounded Work.

The [Cross-Pattern Application](47-cross-pattern-application.md) contains worked teaching examples, including
movement explorations, a responsive duet and complete fixed scores. They show how to connect selected
methods under stated conditions; specifying a score does not establish successful performance or learning.

This publication does not provide a complete repertoire, curriculum, catalogue of performance techniques,
event manual, software implementation, artistic canon or domain history. Use the applicable domain methods,
sources and authorities for those results and for legal or safety decisions. The pattern bodies state where
such results enter and what they can support. Systems Engineering, Human Capability Development, Organization Change Engineering, Operations Management and Embodied Rhythmics remain separate domain frameworks. Generic Method development is supplied by FPF Core, including C.39. [External result use](16-mdpe-reference-15---external-result-use.md) explains the particular contributions and their conditions.
