## SOM.Reference:7 - Companion publications and dependencies

Use the links to consult the supplying publications and the PatternIDs to find their relevant sections.

| Companion | Contribution used here | When it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FPF Core](../../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md) — A.6.P.RI, B.5.EA, B.1.5.EW | Keeping an account tied to its observer, expressing an experience, and recovering simultaneous Method participation | Comparing inside and outside accounts and preserving the encompassing activity |
| [Human Capability Development (HCD)](../hcd/_index.md) — HCD.6, HCD.6.1, HCD.9, HCD.10, HCD.11, HCD.12 | Constructing representative practice, making distinctions learnable, providing feedback, varying and progressing practice, and interpreting performance evidence | Learning design and assessment; HCD.12 is used when the question concerns transfer to unfamiliar work |
| [Embodied Rhythmics (RHY)](../../rhy/_index.md); [RHY.12](../../rhy/25-rhy-12---develop-rhythmic-capability-through-representative.md) for learning | The rhythmic relations used by the receiving activity, with representative rhythmic practice when needed | When timing is rhythmic, as in the salsa example; ordinary bodily regulation need not require this framework |

SOM also needs the receiving practice's technique, criteria and physical limits. Use the relevant dance, music or tool-use practice for those contributions. The writing and dance examples illustrate somatic questions under their stated conditions.

If a companion changes an operation on which the work relies, reconsider the combinations that use it. Establish whether a teacher or lesson can supply suitable instruction for the movement in question.
