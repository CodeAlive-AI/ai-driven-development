# Human Capability Development Principles Framework

**DocID:** `HCD` within **FPF Library**.


HCD is a pattern language for deriving human capability demand from later Work, designing and sustaining development arrangements, judging human contribution, transfer and retention, and evaluating instructional material under stated audience, task, support and time conditions.

> **Author:** Anatoly Levenchuk with AI-assisted development and review.

# Human Capability Development Principles Framework Readme

## Practical entries

Human Capability Development connects what a person needs to contribute in later work with a feasible way to develop that contribution and evidence of what the person can now do. Tools, AI and providers can support the work; their contribution must remain visible when interpreting the person's performance. Instructional material can also be evaluated and repaired without making a claim about a particular person's development.

The selected examples below show how results from several methods work together. Bring a question, a decision you face or a difficulty you have noticed. Compare the result an example supplies with the work you came to do. Which decisions are yours and which did the author make already? Take a useful contribution back to your question. Use a sufficient answer directly. For a remaining question, use the Table of Contents' working questions to find the contribution you need. For a question outside human capability development, follow [Framework Boundary and Refresh](_index.md#human-capability-development-principles-framework-architectural-reference) to the named domain owner. Use an adequate existing result where it fits; return only to the question left open by a missing input or changed condition.

You can ask an assisting agent to explain or apply these methods in the language of your work, using the distinctions that matter to your question.

### HCD-PRODUCT - Build a learning offer before the learners are known

**Situation:** An author has a future audience and intended work, but topics, materials, practice and promised support do not yet form an attainable product.

**Question:** What complete learning route can this audience use, and which subject, material or supply contribution is missing?

**First useful result or blocker:** A design for the intended task family, the consequence of a missing connection, or a comparison of whole designs when the recipient needs to choose. Include the entry conditions, contributions and burdens that can change that answer.

**Start with:** [HCD.28 - Construct and Revise a Whole Learning Product for a Future Audience](45-hcd-28---construct-and-revise-a-whole-learning-product-for-a.md). Its solution explains how to join the contributions; the links below locate the suppliers.

**Stop or return:** Stop with the construction needed for the design or offer decision. Use an applicable subject method. If a required criterion has yet to be established, return that question to the practice responsible for it. For a material defect, use HCD.19. Use HCD.7 to arrange available help or HCD.8 to obtain or build a missing contribution. For a named learner, use a suitable activity directly when its entry conditions and support fit the work. If the person needs a programme designed or compared, take the relevant options and requirements to HCD.2.

Future diagnosticians may calculate an indicator correctly while choosing the wrong model. HCD.28 first checks whether the subject selection criterion is available, implicit in expert action or unresolved. With an adequate subject basis, HCD.5 and HCD.6 construct the learning mechanism and task; HCD.7 establishes available assistance, with HCD.8 used for a contribution that must be obtained or built. HCD.20 compares individual and group options, and HCD.22 constructs the selected shared task. An individual arrangement remains an option when it provides the needed practice. The [constructed whole-product application](50-hcd-application-5---app-hcd-04---constructed-whole-learning.md) shows how the complete load can change the design.

### HCD-GROUP - Develop personal contributions and coordination in shared work

**Situation:** A group produces a usable result. A teacher or manager needs to decide what each person can contribute next time, how people learn to use one another’s contributions, or whether the proposed arrangement can provide suitable practice.

**Question:** What will these people need to do together? Which decisions and dependencies must each person learn to handle, with what help available at the time?

**First useful result or blocker:** An assessment of the arrangement, a comparison of alternatives, a shared task ready to use, or a condition that prevents the requested work. For a personal assignment, identify the decisions the learner made with the available support. To design practice for coordination, specify how one role’s contribution can reach another while its holder can still act on it. To assess a past interaction, trace what the participants actually used. A common result may also be possible when the roles act independently.

**Start with:** [HCD.20 - Compose and Compare Arrangements for Individual and Group Learning](37-hcd-20---compose-and-compare-arrangements-for-individual-and.md) when the configuration is unsettled; enter [HCD.22 - Design Shared Learning Tasks with Necessary Individual Contributions](39-hcd-22---design-shared-learning-tasks-with-necessary-individ.md) directly when the target and arrangement constraints already exist.

Use [HCD.22:4.2.1](39-hcd-22---design-shared-learning-tasks-with-necessary-individ.md#hcd22421---learn-joint-work-while-preserving-different-roles) when learning to respond to another participant is part of the later work.

**Stop or return:** Finish when the comparison, feasibility finding or task design answers the request. Use HCD.7 or HCD.8 for a support condition that still needs work and HCD.28 for an affected connection in the learning product. Ground claims about personal performance, the common result, team capability and the material’s contribution in the relevant observations.

For a cohort of 24, group size depends on the interaction the task requires. Two three-minute contributions each plus six minutes of reconciliation give an initial bound of four people in thirty minutes, excluding separate help. The worked construction adds six minutes of qualified help per group and staggers the starts, allowing two mentors to make all six visits before the dependent personal actions. HCD.20 shows the participant and mentor timeline and the changed burden. At a shared instrument, meaningful observation and operator turns produce different constraints; the discussion formula does not determine that group size.

To establish or change actual membership and contact, use [HCD.21](38-hcd-21---form-and-change-learning-work-groups.md). To develop understanding through reasons, use [HCD.23](40-hcd-23---learn-through-explanation-and-comparison-of-solutio.md); for evaluative judgement and revision, use [HCD.24](41-hcd-24---learn-evaluation-and-revision-through-peer-review.md); for a relation that must reach another project, use [HCD.25](42-hcd-25---learn-transfer-through-comparison-of-projects.md). A group whose work has drifted can use [HCD.26](43-hcd-26---regulate-shared-learning.md), while incompatible preparation, material transfers or return windows lead to [HCD.27](44-hcd-27---coordinate-personal-learning-routes-with-a-shared-r.md). Return what participants could actually contribute or use, and the limits they encountered, to the learning arrangement that depends on them.

### HCD-FEASIBLE - Make a development proposal fit the work and available support

**Situation:** A development proposal contains useful topics and exercises, but its complete tasks, learner effort and provider contribution have not been shown to fit together.

**Question:** What can be offered within the available time and support while preserving the intended contribution to later work?

**First useful result or blocker:** What the proposed arrangement can supply, a constraint that rules it out, or a comparison of the feasible alternatives when the recipient needs to choose.

**Start with:** [HCD.2 - Compose and Compare Human Capability-Development Programmes for Later Work](20-hcd-2---compose-and-compare-human-capability-development-pro.md) for one known person with compatible work, target and starting evidence. For an author with a future audience, start directly with [HCD.28](45-hcd-28---construct-and-revise-a-whole-learning-product-for-a.md); its product variants can later supply the personal comparison.

**Stop or return:** Stop when the requested feasibility finding, design or comparison is complete. For missing support, identify or consult a provider that can supply it. A change to the intended outcome needs the responsible party’s decision. If the cause concerns working conditions, seek the contribution that can address it. Programme comparison does not authorize launch.

1. **Establish whose contribution the proposal concerns.** For one person, [HCD.1 - Derive Human Capability Demand from Representative Later Work](17-hcd-1-derive-human-capability-demand-from-representative-lat.md) supplies the required contribution and its basis. [HCD.3 - Diagnose Whether a Capability, Misconception, or Behaviour Limits Work](18-hcd-3-diagnose-whether-a-capability-misconception-or-behavio.md) distinguishes a human development target from a tool, access, assignment or other limitation. Where simultaneous work competes for development effort, [HCD.4 - Architect a Balanced Human Capability Profile Across Simultaneous Work](19-hcd-4-architect-a-balanced-human-capability-profile-across-s.md) supplies the priorities and contributions to protect. Use those results in HCD.2 to assess the proposed route, construct a feasible route, or compare alternatives when a choice between them is at issue. For a future audience whose individual learners are not yet known, it returns the design question and available fragments to HCD.28. A product variant returns to HCD.2 only with the named person’s compatible starting evidence, work, support and participation conditions.
2. **Test what the route would make attainable.** One established feasible route can answer a request for one. For a comparison, examine the complete tasks the alternatives would support on the same basis. In the constructed [programme-design application](51-hcd-application-6---application-return-to-a-programme-and-in.md), three compositions each use 340 learner hours over 34 weeks. A change from ten to six hours per week leaves 204 hours: each original proposal exceeds the allowance by 136 hours. HCD.2 asks what work, practice and evidence would be removed or replaced. Check whether the complete tasks required for the promised outcomes still fit the reduced allocation.
3. **Construct the missing development contribution.** Use the task material already available to judge whether a proposed route supplies the promised work. When the decision calls for comparing routes, fill the gap that prevents comparison under the same intended output and audience assumptions. [HCD.5 - Select Human Capability-Development Methods](21-hcd-5---select-human-capability-development-methods.md) selects how to develop the target action from the starting evidence, or proposes conditional choices from stated entry assumptions for a future audience; [HCD.6 - Design Representative Practice Tasks](22-hcd-6---design-representative-practice-tasks.md) turns that choice into a whole task, criterion, permitted help and relevant variation. If the learner cannot yet notice a distinction required by the task, [HCD.6.1 - Help a Learner Discern a Needed Distinction in Practice](23-hcd-6-1---help-a-learner-discern-a-needed-distinction-in-pra.md) helps design an accessible contrast and use the learner’s response to guide the next attempt. Return the noticed distinction to the whole task; recognizing it does not establish the ability to perform that task. These results let HCD.2 compare what the options actually provide.
4. **Test the support needed at the time of use.** [HCD.7 - Arrange Providers, Access, Tools, and AI Support for Human Capability Development](24-hcd-7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-hu.md) uses those tasks to establish actual feedback, assessment, access and capacity. The example's 240 teaching hours divided among twelve participants is a planning allocation, not twenty hours of individual contact each. Doubling participants with unchanged individual checking demand doubles that checking work. [HCD.8 - Obtain or Build Missing Human Capability-Development Support with Bounded Recursion](25-hcd-8---obtain-or-build-missing-human-capability-development.md) enters only for a missing contribution that must be obtained or built. Return its cost and operating limits to the comparison.

A useful substitution may emerge from comparing the fragments. Establish whether the complete programme fits the 204 hours before offering it on those terms. Changing the intended outcome is another possible decision, which must be explicit before comparing a narrower offer.

### HCD-EVIDENCE - Decide what a successful attempt permits you to rely on

**Situation:** A learner reaches a correct answer after help, or succeeds in familiar work, and someone needs to decide what this permits them to rely on in later work.

**Question:** Which contribution is supported, under what conditions, and does the development arrangement need to change?

**First useful result or blocker:** A bounded performance conclusion and, when needed, a targeted change to the task or assistance; an unresolved transfer, delay or support question remains separate.

**Start with:** [HCD.11 - Assess Human Performance in Representative Work](28-hcd-11-assess-human-performance-in-representative-work.md), using the attempt and its actual conditions. If practice is being conducted, [HCD.9 - Perform Deliberate Practice with Feedback](26-hcd-9---perform-deliberate-practice-with-feedback.md) preserves the first attempt, supplied help, feedback, learner correction or supported retention. An observed repair can establish how the learner used feedback. Repeat the affected requirement when the practice question or the agreed method calls for it.

**Stop or return:** Stop when the available evidence is sufficient for the intended reliance or local revision. Seek a further observation when it can settle or materially improve a consequential claim and its value warrants the cost.

1. **Separate the successful product from the person's contribution.** In the constructed [APP-HCD-02](48-hcd-application-3---app-hcd-02---constructed-performance-tra.md), a teacher initially helps Learner-L17 reject a release proposal supported by the wrong firmware evidence. That correction is assisted. Two later fresh attempts show L17 detecting the mismatch before a teacher or AI cue, supporting a narrower independent configuration-checking conclusion.
2. **For a transfer question, examine the change relevant to the next use.** [HCD.12 - Test Transfer to Unfamiliar Work or Learning from a New Source](29-hcd-12-test-transfer-to-unfamiliar-work-or-learning-from-a-n.md) uses unfamiliar work when transfer matters. The release case allows the compatible changes to share batches of at most four. It requires two hours per change plus one hour per batch. Ten changes need `20 + 3 = 23` hours and fit a 24-hour allowance. At 22 hours, ten no longer fit; nine need `18 + 3 = 21`. L17's corrected nine-change answer after feedback supports helped resource adaptation while preserving the earlier configuration result.

### HCD-CONTINUATION - Keep a useful arrangement while its conditions change

**Situation:** Development already has useful results, but later work, support or a claim about a development method changes; a successful work episode may also raise a question about what to retain or learn next.
