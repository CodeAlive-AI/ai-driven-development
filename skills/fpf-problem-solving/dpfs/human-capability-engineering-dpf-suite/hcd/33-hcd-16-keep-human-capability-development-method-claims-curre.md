## HCD.16 — Keep Human Capability-Development Method Claims Current

> **Type:** Human Capability Development practitioner method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

### HCD.16:1 - Problem Frame

**Use this when** one exact Human Capability Development Method or status-preserved candidate and one human-development claim face evidence that can change their disposition for a named repertoire use. The trigger may be a changed learner population, later Work, provider, tool or AI condition, source claim, transfer or retention result, safety boundary, or serious alternative.

Begin by naming the exact Method or candidate, its current status, the claim that can change, and the bounded use for which the repertoire decision matters. The first useful result is a current HCD repertoire slice stating the strongest supported use and its limits. Retaining that use, narrowing a claim, leaving a comparison unresolved, or stopping a use at a real protection limit can finish the present question. When a change or inquiry is justified, assign `adopt`, `revise`, `branch`, `retire`, or a selected `further probe`; insufficient evidence alone does not select a study.

A practitioner can reconsider a Method from relevant evidence while preserving its identity and candidate status, the provider conditions, the reach of the evidence and target-domain authority.

Use Method Engineering for general questions of Method identity, construction and maintenance (§4.2). Do not open HCD.16 merely to update a bibliography, rebuild a publication, choose one local option, or declare an already-grounded selected set.

### HCD.16:2 - Problem

Human-development Methods are often named at the wrong grain. “Coaching,” “deliberate practice,” “apprenticeship,” “retrieval,” or “AI tutoring” can hide different operations, learner conditions, supports, providers, tasks, and evidence claims. A new source, tool version, or local success is then used either to replace the whole repertoire or to preserve it unchanged.

Both reactions lose decision value. A source may contradict only one mechanism claim, one population branch, or one assisted-performance inference. A local failure may concern provider capability, access, assessment, or target-domain correctness rather than reusable Method semantics. Conversely, a reassuring average can conceal a critical error, failed transfer, weak retention, or dependence on support.

HCD.16 keeps the evidence question human-specific while returning Method identity and generic repertoire maintenance to Method Engineering. It changes only the exact HCD claim and bounded repertoire use that the evidence can defeat.

### HCD.16:3 - Forces

| Force | Working tension |
| --- | --- |
| Currentness and stability | Respond to material evidence without treating every new publication or tool version as a new Method. |
| Method identity and HCD use | Preserve generic Method semantics and status while deciding whether a human-development use remains warranted. |
| Local evidence and transfer | Use direct observations where they fit without generalizing across learners, Work families, providers, supports, or horizons. |
| Improvement and critical error | Reward useful performance while preventing aggregate gains from hiding unacceptable errors or protected losses. |
| Assistance and human contribution | Use tools, AI, coaching and scaffolding where appropriate while separating equipped performance from later unassisted or changed-support performance. |
| Decision and uncertainty | State the strongest supported use or a qualified unresolved comparison; select a new probe only for an obtainable contribution worth its full burden. |
| Repertoire and selection | Keep a current field-specific slice without pretending that it performs a local choice, universal retirement, or Method admission. |

### HCD.16:4 - Solution

#### HCD.16:4.1 - Fix the repertoire use and exact claim

Name the practitioner or steward, decision owner and authority, bounded repertoire use, target human capability, later-Work family, learner or practitioner population, material conditions, evidence or qualification window, and protected consequences.

Identify one exact HCD Method or candidate and one claim whose disposition can change. A useful claim names the intended action or contribution, population, task or Work, practice and feedback conditions, provider and support, measure, horizon, and inference. “The Method works” is too coarse.

If the repertoire use, subject, status, or claim cannot be recovered, identify the missing value and what is needed to recover it. Do not manufacture an HCD disposition from a school name, source citation, carrier, prompt, tool, or local activity label.

#### HCD.16:4.2 - Recover Method and candidate status from Method Engineering

Recover the Method or candidate status and the reusable semantics needed to identify the present claim. Obtain construction, admission, description, variant lineage, provenance, generic trial, fit, worth, introduction or currentness results from their direct Method-Engineering owners only when this bounded HCD judgement consumes them. Keep the candidate status until the proposed Method is independently admitted.

A changed file, source, prompt, support configuration, tool, provider, or local Work deviation does not create a Method variant. When the evidence suggests changed reusable semantics, return the exact changed operation, dependency, entry or stop rule to A.3.1.MV. When it concerns description, support, introduction, or performed Work, return that question to the owner of the corresponding description, support, introduction, or Work result.

HCD.16 consumes these results to know what it is evaluating. It neither admits a Method nor retires one universally.

#### HCD.16:4.3 - Bind human-development evidence to the correct conditions

For the exact claim, recover evidence about the target action, learner starting state, representative later Work, practice and feedback, distribution and delay, unfamiliar transfer, retention, assessment, provider capability, support and AI conditions, critical errors, burden, and protected consequences at the strength the decision needs.

Keep these evidence states separate:

- assisted practice performance;
- unassisted performance after practice;
- performance under changed or withdrawn support;
- same-job performance after prior assisted exposure;
- unfamiliar same-family transfer;
- transfer to other Work;
- delayed retention under stated intervening practice; and
- provider, cohort, service, organization, or product outcomes.

Evidence for one state may motivate a probe in another; it does not fill it. A provider's capability, a deployed tool's productivity effect, or a learner's corrected answer is not the human capability claim by substitution.

#### HCD.16:4.4 - Compare the current claim with serious alternatives

State the smallest contradiction, missing evidence, or changed condition. Compare at least the current claim, the serious alternative that would change the disposition, and the consequences of retaining, narrowing, branching, or withdrawing the claim for this use.

Ask whether the apparent problem lies in:

| Candidate locus | Decision-changing question |
| --- | --- |
| Source-to-claim row | Does the source actually support this population, task, measure, horizon, transfer, support, provider, or causal inference? |
| Method-use condition | Does the already admitted Method still fit this learner, later Work, provider, support, risk, and evidence need? |
| Population or condition branch | Does evidence defeat only one subgroup, configuration, support posture, or qualification window? |
| Assessment | Did the task, scoring, cue, tool, or missing criterion fail to expose the intended contribution? |
| Provider or access | Was the Method absent, unavailable, or enacted by an unqualified provider rather than defeated? |
| Reusable Method semantics | Did an operation, dependency, entry, stop, or other reusable claim change, requiring an A.3.1.MV return? |

Reopen only the locus that consumes the changed basis. A newer publication date, renamed school, improved interface, or changed carrier does not reopen an unaffected HCD claim.

#### HCD.16:4.5 - Settle the current result before selecting further inquiry

First state what the available evidence supports for this repertoire use and which comparison remains unresolved. Retain supported practice, narrow an unsupported claim, or stop a use whose real protection condition is not met. None requires designing a study merely to finish the current account.

Select `further probe` only when a new observation is obtainable in the relevant window and could change the disposition enough to warrant its whole burden. Include designing and describing the inquiry, learner and provider time, preparation, practice, feedback, comparison, delay, specialist access and displaced development or protective Work. A plausible discriminating question does not itself supply people, authority, time or access. Use C.11.DUA when that evidence-demand judgement needs appraisal.

For a selected probe, specify the person's first attempt, allowed help, representative change, source and tool conditions, provider contribution, delay or support contrast when material, critical-error condition, protected contributions, complete burden, stopping rule and decision-changing observations. Keep the target-domain criteria and safety boundary with their owners. An unavailable or unwarranted probe leaves the strongest supported current result and the dependent uncertainty.

A generic Method trial, Method admission, variant decision, or organization rollout remains with Core's Method-development family or another direct owner. HCD.16 selects only the human-development inquiry needed for this bounded repertoire use. Its selection or design is not an obtained observation.

#### HCD.16:4.6 - Return the supported slice and any selected change

State the supported current use, narrowed claim, unresolved comparison or real protection stop for the exact HCD repertoire use. For a justified change or selected inquiry, use the following dispositions:

| Disposition | Meaning here | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `adopt` | Include an already admitted Method for the named HCD use under the supported conditions. | Does not admit the Method, select it for one person, authorize Work, or establish effect. |
| `revise` | Change the exact HCD claim, evidence condition, population scope, support condition, or use statement while preserving Method status. | A semantic Method change returns to A.3.1.MV; a text edit alone is not the result. |
| `branch` | Keep distinct, evidence-bearing use conditions or populations whose dispositions differ. | Does not create a Method variant or inherit admission for a candidate. |
| `retire` | Stop offering the Method or claim for this bounded HCD use. | Does not erase the Method, source history, candidate account, or other supported uses. |
| `further probe` | Select one feasible, worthwhile observation for the named decision while preserving status and uncertainty. | Selection is not the obtained result. The probe has full burden, access, protection, stop and response conditions; an unavailable later observation withholds only its dependent claim. |

Keep the actual grounds, serious alternatives, uncertainty and change conditions needed by the receiving use in the same repertoire slice. An unresolved comparison is a truthful result when no change or new inquiry is warranted; preserve any independently supported use. Add a probe description only for a selected inquiry. When different claims need different outcomes, keep them separate rather than averaging them into one Method score.

#### HCD.16:4.7 - Return the current repertoire slice and route stronger results

Retain the positions needed by the current repertoire slice and its receiving use:

| Position | Content for the receiving use |
| --- | --- |
| Use and authority | Practitioner or receiver, bounded HCD repertoire use, decision owner, authority, population, later Work, qualification window, and stop. |
| Subjects and status | Exact admitted Methods and separately status-preserved candidates; current reusable semantics, descriptions and lineage supplied by ME. |
| Human-development claims | Target contribution, learner starting state, practice and feedback, representative Work, transfer, retention, provider, support, safety, tool and AI conditions. |
| Evidence and currentness | Direct observations and sources with population, task, comparator, measure, horizon, support, provider, causal, and currentness limits. |
| Alternatives and result | Serious alternatives, supported current use and limits, unresolved comparison or protection stop; any selected `adopt`, `revise`, `branch`, `retire`, or `further probe`, with scope and protected consequences. |
| Gaps and reopening | Decision-relevant missing values and change conditions; needed return owner and retirement meaning. A selected probe also states its full burden, access, stopping rule and response. |


When several exact already grounded members must be declared for one selector-facing use, pass the members and basis to G.5. When a shipped maintained set, source pack, publication, or other named currentness object needs targeted refresh, use G.11. HCD.16 does not recreate either result for an ordinary claim-sized decision.

### HCD.16:5 - Archetypal Grounding

#### HCD.16:5.1 - A constructed E27 repertoire decision

This replay is constructed. It stipulates the Method and candidate statuses needed to demonstrate the HCD decision; it supplies no real ReleaseWork-R9 occurrence, provider Work, learner observation, Method admission, or effect.

For the bounded 2026-Q4 development use, stipulate one admitted `Trace-and-Specialist-Return Method-E27` for developing source/configuration tracing and appropriate specialist return. Its learner attempts a representative review task, traces the claims to the supplied sources/configuration and returns a question to a qualified specialist when the case crosses the stated boundary. A provider gives criterion-bearing feedback; the learner corrects or justifies the response and retries the affected contribution on a changed case. These practice and feedback operations constitute the stipulated development Method; the review operation is the target being practised. Preserve it as admitted only inside the example. Also stipulate two status-preserved candidate accounts: `Cue-Led-Correction Candidate-E27` and `Counter-Hypothesis-First Candidate-E27`. Their names and derivation do not admit Methods or establish variants.

The current evidence question comes from unfamiliar AI-error cases. Assisted correction was accurate after a cue, but no current observation establishes that E27 independently generates and tests a counter-hypothesis before accepting plausible wrong AI output. Trace and specialist-return contributions remain supported in the stipulated earlier cases. The evidence therefore changes only the independent-challenge claim.

For the present repertoire decision, stipulate that a valid comparison would require three learner-hours and two provider-hours in total, including design, task preparation, attempts, feedback and comparison. Only half an hour of provider time remains before the decision; extending it would displace already committed development Work. No adequate new comparison is obtainable in that window. The supported trace and helped-practice uses can remain while the independent-challenge comparison stays unresolved.

The current repertoire slice is:

| Exact subject or claim | Constructed disposition | Boundary and next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Trace-and-Specialist-Return Method-E27` for guided trace-and-return practice | `adopt` for the named development use under the stipulated conditions. | Preserves only the already admitted Method and supported claims; supplies no universal selection or ReleaseWork-R9 result. |
| “Accurate assisted practice demonstrates independent learning” | `retire` for this use. | Assisted correction remains useful practice evidence, but it cannot support the independent-challenge claim. |
| `Cue-Led-Correction Candidate-E27` | `branch` as a candidate account for helped correction episodes only. | Keep candidate status; do not use its helped result as evidence for independent unfamiliar performance. |
| `Counter-Hypothesis-First Candidate-E27` | Comparison unresolved; preserve the candidate account without selecting a new probe. | No independent-challenge, superiority, Method-admission, fit or worth claim follows. The narrower supported repertoire slice is complete for this window. |

Now consider a later decision about offering the independent-challenge use. The three learner-hours and two provider-hours, current criteria, specialist access, participation and authorization are available within the protected window. Distinguishing candidate-supported independent challenge from helped correction can change that offer enough to justify the full burden. Select `further probe` for this changed condition; the earlier limited result did not require it.

The selected probe uses two matched unfamiliar release-review cases with current stipulated criteria. In each, capture the first trace, configuration hypothesis, challenge, action or refusal, critical error, and specialist return before help. One case then permits the ordinary cue-led correction; the other permits the candidate counter-hypothesis prompt only after the first attempt. A later fresh case under normal permitted support and, only if the decision needs it, a separately resourced delayed or perturbed-support observation can distinguish local independent contribution from helped correction. Stop on missing criteria, unsafe use, absent specialist access, unacceptable burden, or damage to traceability.

Suppose both initial attempts need help, E27 corrects each, and on the fresh case E27 generates and tests the relevant counter-hypothesis before a coach or AI cue supplies it, while preserving traceability and the specialist return. The result now supports that observed contribution under the fresh-case conditions after both practice episodes. It does not identify which practice produced it or rank the two candidates: order, carryover between episodes and case differences remain possible explanations. Keep both candidate statuses and the earlier helped results.

This limited result can finish the observation question. If the repertoire choice depends on the comparative effect of the two practices, return that question to the A.3.1.WT trial owner for a qualified comparison design. Appraise its full design, participant, provider and displaced-work burden before selecting it; the present allocation does not automatically cover a different inquiry. Without an obtainable, worthwhile comparison, retain the supported use and leave the ranking unresolved.

Possible results remain bounded:


| Observation | Repertoire response |
| --- | --- |
| Independent challenge appears on fresh unfamiliar cases after both practice episodes while trace and specialist return remain protected | Preserve that observed use without ranking the candidates. Reconsider `adopt` only for an independently admitted Method at the supported use. Use `branch` to distinguish evidence-bearing use conditions whose dispositions differ, including within candidate accounts; preserve candidate status. Branching does not admit a Method or create a variant; return admission, variant, fit and worth questions to ME. |
| Correct challenge appears only after the counter-hypothesis prompt | Preserve helped-practice value and narrow the claim; independent contribution remains unproved. Select another probe only if its obtainable contribution now warrants its burden. |
| Traceability or specialist return worsens | Reject or revise the candidate use regardless of aggregate accuracy. |
| The task, source, configuration, or critical-error criterion is defective | Repair or return that evidence input; do not retire the human-development Method from an invalid observation. |

#### HCD.16:5.2 - Unlike evidence states do not select an engineering Method

AI-supported customer service can show deployed equipped productivity and a bounded same-job outage signal. Consulting tasks can show that assistance helps inside one task frontier and hurts outside it. School mathematics can show a difference between assisted practice and later unassisted performance. These are decision-relevant evidence shapes, but their populations, Work, tools, measures, horizons, and providers do not establish an adult release-engineering Method. Use those observations at their supported source scope and narrow the unsupported engineering claim now. An E27-specific independent-learning, transfer or superiority claim still needs compatible evidence; its absence does not commission an engineering study. Keep the current limited use or unresolved comparison, and select a new comparison only for its obtainable, worthwhile contribution.

#### HCD.16:5.3 - A source contradiction can reopen one claim without opening treatment

An emerging human counterconditioning review can reopen one source-to-claim row about an extinction-learning mechanism. A publication-bias critique can reopen a pooled claim about choice-architecture effects. HCD.16 may return `revise`, `retire`, or `further probe` for an exact HCD use, but it prescribes neither clinical treatment nor public-policy nudging. The specialist domain retains the intervention and outcome decision.

### HCD.16:6 - Bias-Annotation

Novelty and prestige can make a new source, tool, model, or school seem repertoire-changing before the affected claim is named. Familiarity creates the opposite bias: a long-used Method is protected from contrary transfer, retention, provider, or critical-error evidence.

Status laundering is especially dangerous. A well-documented candidate, successful local deviation, or newly versioned description can look like an admitted Method. Recover status and reusable semantics from Core's Method-development family before comparing HCD evidence, and keep a favourable HCD disposition bounded to its use.

### HCD.16:7 - Conformance Checklist

- The bounded repertoire use, decision owner and authority, population, later Work, qualification window, and protected consequences are explicit.
- One exact admitted Method or status-preserved candidate and one decision-changing HCD claim are named.
- Core's Method-development patterns supply the status, semantics or other result needed by this bounded judgement; unrelated Method evaluations are not prerequisites.
- Assisted, unassisted, changed-support, same-job, unfamiliar-transfer, other-Work, delayed, provider, and organization evidence states remain distinct where material.
- Source population, task, comparator, measure, horizon, support, provider, causal, and currentness limits travel with the claim.
- Serious alternatives and the smallest contradiction or missing evidence are compared without a global Method score.
- The result states the strongest supported use and limits, any unresolved comparison or real protection stop, and any selected change. `further probe` requires an obtainable, worthwhile contribution with full burden, access, protection, stop and response conditions.
- Candidate status is preserved; adoption and retirement do not change universal Method status.
- G.5, G.11, Core's Method-development family, clinical, target-domain, safety, provider, and authority questions are returned only when their own entry conditions obtain.

### HCD.16:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

| Anti-pattern | Better move |
| --- | --- |
| “A newer paper replaces the Method.” | Name the exact source-to-claim row and reopen only the use that consumes the changed claim. |
| “The tool changed, so this is a new Method variant.” | Compare reusable semantics and return description, support, tool, or local-Work changes to their direct owner. |
| “The candidate worked locally, so adopt it.” | Preserve candidate status and return Method admission, fit and worth to Core's Method-development family; HCD decides only its bounded evidence use. |
| “Assisted scores improved, so learning occurred.” | Preserve assisted-performance evidence and withhold the unsupported human-learning claim. Obtain the particular independent, changed-support, transfer or retention evidence only for a selected stronger use when the inquiry is feasible and worthwhile. |
| “One subgroup failed, so retire the Method everywhere.” | Branch or retire only the defeated population, condition, or use and preserve supported uses. |
| “Refresh the whole publication after every source change.” | Reopen the affected claim; use G.11 only for a maintained currentness object under its own conditions. |

### HCD.16:9 - Consequences

The HCD repertoire becomes more explicit and easier to repair. Practitioners can identify which Method use remains warranted, which claim changed, which branch needs direct evidence, and which candidate must stay a candidate. Source or tool changes no longer force a global rebuild.

The cost is disciplined evidence binding. Some decisions finish with a limited current use or unresolved comparison; a selected probe consumes real learner and provider resources. A useful local Method can be retired from one use while remaining available elsewhere. Method Engineering, providers, target-domain owners and evidence owners supply only the separate results needed by the particular HCD use.

### HCD.16:10 - Rationale

The remaining HCD question is not whether a Method exists in general. It is whether an exact human-development claim remains warranted for a bounded population, later Work, provider and support arrangement, evidence window, and receiving use.

Separating this question from Method admission and generic repertoire maintenance preserves reusable semantics and lineage while letting direct human-development evidence change practice. Claim-sized dispositions avoid both fashion-driven replacement and loyalty-driven stagnation.

### HCD.16:11 - SoTA-Echoing

**Working question:** how should a human-capability-development repertoire change when sources, tools, learner conditions, or direct evidence disagree?

**Selected line — adopt and adapt.** The best-known defensible line for this bounded question is claim-sized updating that preserves population, task, comparator, measure, horizon, support, provider, and Method status. Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond's [deployed customer-support study](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044) separates assisted productivity from a noisy same-job outage signal; Dell'Acqua and colleagues' [task-frontier experiment](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838) supplies a bounded independent-error challenge; and Bastani and colleagues' [school-mathematics trial](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) separates assisted practice from later unassisted performance. HCD.16 adapts these unlike evidence states in §§4.3–4.6 and §5.2 rather than pooling them into one AI-learning verdict. None establishes E27's capability, a universal tutor, other-Work transfer, or long retention.

The serious default is repertoire change by newest source, popular school, average score, or tool release. It is cheap, but it loses claim scope and makes a source identity or visible proxy decide what only receiving-use evidence can decide. HCD.16 rejects that default and accepts the extra work of binding evidence because the result is better on critical-error safety, transfer truth, status preservation, and local repair while allowing a supported current use, narrowed claim or unresolved comparison to finish without an unwarranted inquiry.

Bauer and colleagues' [review of counterconditioning and reward-related extinction directions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlm.2024.107994) and the Mertens/Maier [choice-architecture synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2107346118) and [publication-bias critique](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2200300119) serve as failure and contradiction evidence: a credible current line may be emerging, heterogeneous, or sensitive to bias without supporting a universal HCD or specialist intervention. This changes the source-row and `further probe` rules in §§4.4–4.6 and the domain return in §5.3.

A.3.1.MV supplies generic Method semantics and lineage; A.3.1.MI supplies introduction, observations and later-use returns. Use those results when the bounded HCD judgement depends on them. Reopen the comparison when stronger direct evidence changes an affected population, Work, learner state, provider, tool, support, measure, horizon, causal limit, or source role, or when an improved Method-repertoire practice returns the same status distinctions and local repair at lower decision effort.
