## HCD.Reference:10 - PatternID and reader order

HCD.* is the Human Capability Development PatternID namespace. Numbers are stable addresses, not steps. The [Table of Contents](_index.md#table-of-contents) gives the reading order. Demand is a useful cold start when the required human contribution is unknown; equivalent qualified input permits direct entry to any Method under its own conditions. Reader order is not programme order, Work order, package dependency or a mandatory lifecycle.
