## HCD.Reference:1 - Start from the contribution that later Work needs

Use HCD when a person, manager or development practitioner needs to establish what a person contributes to later Work or decide how to develop or sustain that contribution within available time, access and support. The question may arise before a programme, during a difficult practice attempt, after a successful assessment, or years later when the Work changes. A learning-product author can also use the framework while the future audience is still an assumption.

The recurring difficulty is connecting a development activity to the contribution it should change. A curriculum may cover every topic while leaving whole tasks out of reach. A good assisted answer may conceal the action the person still cannot supply. A once useful arrangement may become unsuitable after a tool, task or provider changes. Identify the contribution required and what available evidence establishes. If a development change is needed, compare candidate ways to make it and their feasibility. For a selected change, use or arrange the support it needs and assess what the subsequent observations establish.

Begin with the question or decision at hand. Identify whose contribution matters, the later Work and its conditions, what is already known, and the finding or decision still needed. Then enter the relevant body through the [Practical entries](_index.md#practical-entries). The first useful result can be small: a demand hypothesis, two comparable task fragments, a corrected practice attempt, one qualified performance claim, or an exact missing contribution to obtain. Use what is already known about a person when it settles whether an activity fits. Use HCD.2 if choosing or designing a personal programme is the task. An author developing a product for a future audience can proceed directly to [HCD.28](45-hcd-28---construct-and-revise-a-whole-learning-product-for-a.md).

The main choices concern complete-task attainability, preservation of useful existing contributions, and the effort displaced by development. More practice, realism, independence, assessment or provider preparation can improve one condition while making the whole arrangement too burdensome or changing the task being judged. Select these contributions for the live question and compare their combined demands. The [programme and instrument application](51-hcd-application-6---application-return-to-a-programme-and-in.md) makes that whole-arrangement constraint concrete.
