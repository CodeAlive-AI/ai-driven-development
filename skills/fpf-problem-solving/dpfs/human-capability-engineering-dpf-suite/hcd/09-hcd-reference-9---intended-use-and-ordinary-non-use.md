## HCD.Reference:9 - Intended use and ordinary non-use

Use this edition to identify the human contribution needed in later Work, construct or choose a feasible way to develop it, and interpret observations for a question about performance, transfer, retention or a development decision. It also supports learning-product and instructional-material work, individual and shared learning arrangements, and the continuation or revision of HCD Methods and cultural practices. The [Practical entries](_index.md#practical-entries) and [Available patterns](11-hcd-reference-11---available-patterns.md) distinguish these working questions and their results.

Use domain findings for specialist correctness and safety, clinical services for treatment, and authorized decision makers for release or employment decisions. In HCD, use the evidence specific to a claim that a provider operated, a person performed or developed, a practice continued or an intervention had an effect. Use a curriculum, provider arrangement or assessment that fits the question. Select or construct the missing contribution when needed.
