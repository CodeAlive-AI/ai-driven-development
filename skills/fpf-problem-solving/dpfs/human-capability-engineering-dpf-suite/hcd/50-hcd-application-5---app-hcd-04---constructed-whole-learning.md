## HCD.Application:5 - APP-HCD-04 - Constructed whole learning product and shared task

An author plans a product for future technical diagnosticians who can calculate energy per unit volume but may mistake an increased indicator for proof of a cause. The aim is to compare rival explanations, select an observation that distinguishes them and state a supported conclusion. [HCD.28:4–HCD.28:5](45-hcd-28---construct-and-revise-a-whole-learning-product-for-a.md) connects the subject basis, preparation, task, support, feedback and personal observation needed for this offer.

Start with what the available basis lets the learner do. A qualified demonstration and supported practice may make an implicit expert choice learnable. Recover the subject criterion when it is still missing and the intended teaching or use needs it. For a material that hides an available distinction, use HCD.19. For help that cannot arrive in time, use HCD.7 to assess usable support or HCD.8 to obtain or build the missing contribution when that is the task. State any remaining limit. Where the offer includes devising an inquiry, give each learner a receiving decision, raw case information and ordinary sources while the alternatives and the choice of observation are still open. Examine how the learner constructed the comparison. HCD.28:5.1 gives a workshop example and shows what belongs in the instructor’s notes.

For 24 entrants, HCD.28 compares a supported individual route with four-person groups for the same outcome and final assistance conditions. The individual core takes 90 minutes. The group core adds six active minutes per learner for qualified help and preserves both personal contributions and reconciliation. In the [HCD.20](37-hcd-20---compose-and-compare-arrangements-for-individual-and.md) timetable, pairs of groups start at 10:50, 11:00 and 11:10, two mentors are booked from 11:00 to 11:30 and the personal responses can finish before noon. Six 20-minute preparation branches add two learner-hours. [HCD.22](39-hcd-22---design-shared-learning-tasks-with-necessary-individ.md) works through the measurements, conditional models, choice of a distinguishing observation, common conclusion and subsequent personal use. The two-day plan reserves 288 minutes for individual assessment, feedback and follow-up checks. With coordination and recovery, the individual and four-person routes reserve 17.2 and 16.6 provider-hours respectively. At full use of learner recovery, active allowances are 39.5 and 42.65 learner-hours. HCD.20 also compares pairs and their peak demand. A group answer does not establish every person’s judgement. After the entry check and worked contrast, each learner uses the ten-minute first judgement to investigate the rise in workshop electricity use. The group’s accounts are still withheld. For a question about A's own metered consumption, its two comparable readings already give an answer. To choose which control to inspect, the learner can propose two possible starts of the same equipment and select a record that distinguishes the one which actually ran. The prepared archive returns the records each learner requests. The instructor can assess the diagnostic choices from the learner’s request and recommendation. The pump cards support group comparison, and the final personal pump task asks the learner to interpret an existing measurement and decide whether to stop. Judge each on the choice it actually leaves open.

For a paid distributed offer, establish compatible meeting and response windows, count work outside meetings and provide a usable continuation after a missed contribution or qualify the offer by that limit. If the offer covers everyone admitted, test permitted combinations of requests against the shared provider’s windows, including requests the organizer cannot distinguish when booking. If qualified help cannot reach learners in the proposed window, examine feasible staggering, individual support, another provider or a narrower promise. Payment and a cohort assignment provide different evidence from usable participation and instruction.

An internal team can use employer-provided time and equipment without buying a course. Around one instrument, participants might predict, measure, challenge and take turns operating it. Establish applicable access, confidentiality, training data and personal actions, reusing the current arrangement when adequate. Capacity depends on exclusive turns and concurrent work. When the team orders a report for its own use, a specialist may supply the whole report. When the team must reach its own decision, it needs the relevant findings in time, authority to act and an opportunity to exercise that authority. To learn to reach such decisions together, members need practice using one another’s contributions.

For a future audience learning a familiar calculation that needs no exchange, a sufficient individual route is a complete product. If a named learner later needs a programme assessment or comparison, use HCD.2 with that person’s supported starting condition, later work and access.
