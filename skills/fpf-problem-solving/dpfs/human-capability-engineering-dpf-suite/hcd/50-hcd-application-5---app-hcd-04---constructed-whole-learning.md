## HCD.Application:5 - APP-HCD-04 - Constructed whole learning product and shared task

An author plans a product for future technical diagnosticians who can calculate energy per unit volume but may mistake an increased indicator for proof of one cause. Their target is to compare rival explanations, select a discriminating observation and state a bounded conclusion. [HCD.28:4–HCD.28:5](45-hcd-28---construct-and-revise-a-whole-learning-product-for-a.md) develops the whole route from qualified subject content to preparation, task, actual help, correction and personal observation.

The subject repair comes first when necessary. Reconstruct an implicit expert choice when the unresolved operation or criterion prevents the intended teaching or use. Qualified demonstration and supported practice may otherwise supply the needed learning action. An unavailable qualified method needs subject development. If the method is available and the material hides the distinguishing condition, HCD.19 diagnoses that contribution. If the feedback cannot arrive, HCD.7 and HCD.8 address supply. Repairing one does not silently repair the others.

For 24 entrants, HCD.28 compares a supported individual variant with a shared-reasoning variant under the same intended result and assistance conditions. The individual route retains a ninety-minute core; the four-person route adds six minutes per learner for qualified help without removing either personal contribution or reconciliation. HCD.20 gives the joint timeline: group starts at 10:50, 11:00 and 11:10, with two mentors booked 11:00–11:30 and personal responses complete before noon. A twenty-minute preparation branch for six entrants adds two hours and returns them to the same whole task. [HCD.20](37-hcd-20---compose-and-compare-arrangements-for-individual-and.md) checks sizes and peak help; [HCD.22](39-hcd-22---design-shared-learning-tasks-with-necessary-individ.md) shows the actual measurements, conditional models, observation choice, common conclusion and later personal return. The completed two-day comparison retains 288 minutes of personal checking and correction checking, plus coordination and bounded recovery. Its one-cohort allowances are 17.2 provider-hours for the individual option and 16.6 for four-person groups; full-reserve learner allowances are 39.5 and 42.65 hours. HCD.20 also develops the pair option and its peak demand. These different coordinates prevent a claim of unconditional economy. The common answer supplies no automatic result for every member.

A paid distributed-adult offer must find compatible meeting and response windows, count reading and correction outside meetings, and provide a usable route after a missed contribution. Payment and an assigned cohort do not establish those conditions. If the proposed simultaneous window cannot supply qualified help, the offer needs a viable staggered or individual variant, another provider, or a changed promise.

An internal team can instead use employer-provided time and equipment without purchasing a course. Its useful group actions may be prediction, measurement, challenge and operator rotation around the instrument. The access and confidentiality conditions, permitted training data and personal actions still require construction; a formula for sequential discussion does not size this task. If only the team's delivered report is required and learning is not promised, division of labour or external competent help may be the appropriate work arrangement.

For an unknown audience whose target is a familiar calculation with no necessary exchange, a sufficient individual route remains a complete product. When a specific learner arrives, HCD.2 compares its useful finite contribution from that person's supported baseline. The product account does not assign the whole offer to everyone matching an audience label.

These are constructed contrasts. They establish neither actual participation, operating support, personal learning, team capability nor causal effectiveness. The appropriate observation and decision remain with their direct methods and owners.
