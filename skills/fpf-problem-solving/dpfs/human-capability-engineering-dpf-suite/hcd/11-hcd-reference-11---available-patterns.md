## HCD.Reference:11 - Available patterns

| PatternID | Task | Result and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HCD.1 | Derive human capability demand from representative later Work. | Demand account, prospective hypothesis, outside-HCD return or discriminated evidence blocker. |
| HCD.3 | Diagnose whether a capability, misconception or behaviour limits Work. | Qualified intervention target or non-training return. |
| HCD.4 | Architect a balanced profile across simultaneous Work. | Profile comparison, recommendation, unresolved question, bounded choice or exact blocker. |
| HCD.2 | Compose and compare capability-development programmes for later Work. | Same-holder programme comparison, exact missing input, or learning-product design return when the person is not yet known; no programme choice. |
| HCD.5 | Select a capability-development Method. | Bounded Method selection or shortlist, non-training return, or exact missing input; no effectiveness claim. |
| HCD.6 | Design representative practice, including preparation for a later explanation. | Criterion-bearing task set with whole action, support, critical errors, correction, meaningful variation and resource fit; §4.2.2 and §5.7 compare constructing a solution with explaining a worked solution. |
| HCD.6.1 | Make a needed distinction discernible in practice. | Accessible contrast, action using the distinction and a learner response that guides correction, variation or whole-task use. |
| HCD.7 | Arrange providers, access, tools and AI support. | Enabled contribution arrangement with competence, access, capacity, checking and fallback, or the exact gap. |
| HCD.8 | Obtain or build missing capability-development support with bounded recursion. | Obtain/build decision, bounded provider or environment preparation, representative operating test, or first unsupported dependency. |
| HCD.9 | Perform deliberate practice with feedback. | Focused-practice episode preserving first attempt, help, feedback and response; targeted retry after correction, or supported retention with a reason to continue or stop. |
| HCD.10 | Choose whether and how to vary, space, interleave or progress practice. | Condition-sensitive next episodes with timing, retrieval, sources, support, challenge, observation and response rule kept distinct. |
| HCD.11 | Assess performance in representative Work. | Observed contribution and bounded capability inference, or a distinct evidence or assessment gap. |
| HCD.12 | Test transfer to unfamiliar Work or learning from a new source. | Transferred contribution, adaptation need and tested limits, with new-source learning separate. |
| HCD.13 | Test retention and dependence on tools or other support. | Condition-qualified retention, independent-core and support-dependence findings or unresolved comparison. |
| HCD.14 | Revise the development arrangement from evidence. | Specific revision hypothesis, test, cost, protected conditions and conditional update. |
| HCD.15 | Sustain continuing Human Capability Development, including a worthwhile lesson from successful Work. | Qualified development question or return to Work; where needed, a holder-specific continuing arrangement with triggers, contributions, evidence, burden, authority, re-entry and stops, or an exact blocker. |
| HCD.16 | Keep HCD Method claims current. | Exact Method/candidate, supported bounded use and limits, narrowed claim, unresolved comparison or real protection stop; a justified change or obtainable worthwhile inquiry may select `adopt`, `revise`, `branch`, `retire` or `further probe`. |
| HCD.17 | Deliberately continue and change HCD culture. | Truthful enacted/candidate account and bounded `continue`, `revise`, `branch`, `replace`, `stop` or `unknown`; current grounds can suffice, and a new intervention is conditional on its useful attainable contribution. |
| HCD.18 | Select or construct a characterization and evaluation specification for instructional material. | Task-derived denominator, selected properties and scales, evidence and missingness, contrasts, result meanings and continuation; or a compatible specification to use directly. |
| HCD.19 | Evaluate the material for the promised use with the evidence required by a compatible specification. | A finding about what the material supplies and any limiting defect, criterion or evidence gap. A complete evaluation returns every selected property with its value or missing evidence. A bounded diagnosis identifies its scope. Give the assistance, repair reach and affected-use test where relevant. |
| HCD.20 | Compose and compare individual and group learning arrangements. | Required personal actions and interaction, task-group and provider sizes, help, total and peak burdens, and any remaining limit. A sufficient individual option or a qualified comparison may complete the task. |
| HCD.21 | Form and change learning work groups. | A feasible composition and the means to establish contact, evidence of contact or work already completed, or an identified missing condition. Include a feasible alternative or recovery when needed and available. |
| HCD.22 | Design shared learning tasks with necessary individual contributions. | A task that gives each learner the required operation, a usable contribution from others and the appropriate access, feedback or recovery. An assessment can establish what the existing task supports or which condition is missing. |
| HCD.23 | Learn through explanation and comparison of solutions. | A design for comparing explanations and using their grounds, an explained comparison, evidence of a learner’s application under the help used, or the limit of the available evidence. |
| HCD.24 | Learn evaluation and revision through peer review. | A supported product judgement, evidence of the reviewer’s own evaluation or of the author’s reasoned use of feedback, or the conditions for the intended practice. Identify a material unresolved issue and obtain qualified help when needed. |
| HCD.25 | Learn transfer through comparison of projects. | A qualified correspondence or its limit, a design for using it, or evidence of the learner’s own application under the stated conditions. Distinguish new learning from transfer of an earlier capability. |
| HCD.26 | Regulate shared learning. | A supported account of the shared attempt, a warranted continuation or process change, an observation of uptake, or a specific request or limit. |
| HCD.27 | Coordinate personal learning routes with a shared rhythm. | A qualified finding about transfers and windows, a feasible route or re-entry, or the particular condition that limits it. |
| HCD.28 | Construct and revise a whole learning product for a future audience. | A connected design, an assessment or comparison of routes, or a stated subject, learning or supply gap. Include the relevant entry conditions, burdens and peak windows. |

The human-development and cultural cases are constructed illustrations. Claims about performed work, provider operation or changes in a person or population require evidence from the relevant setting. HCD.19 separately reports a bounded prepared-agent material walkthrough under its stated conditions.
