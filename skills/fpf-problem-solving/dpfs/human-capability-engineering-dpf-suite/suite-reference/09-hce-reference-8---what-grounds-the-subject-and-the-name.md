## HCE.Reference:8 - What grounds the subject and the name?

The two constituent frameworks carry their full explanations, cases and source comparisons. [HCD's source account (HCD.Sources:1)](../hcd/57-hcd-sources-1---source-use-and-currentness.md) distinguishes human-learning evidence, specialist criteria and the broader programme construction. [SOM's rationale (SOM.Reference:6)](../som/06-som-reference-6---rationale-source-use-and-alternatives.md) explains the relation between bodily modeling and learning, its comparison with direct instruction, and the limits of its teaching claims. Their sources can support mechanisms without establishing every proposed combination's effectiveness.

For a historical comparison, the [October HCD](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/7282291e766505e36c869f3ceaac9f64deec9b24/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/HUMAN-CAPABILITY-DEVELOPMENT-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) and [September SOM](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/7282291e766505e36c869f3ceaac9f64deec9b24/DPFs/Human%20Capability%20Engineering%20DPF%20Suite/SOMATIC-MOVEMENT-MODELING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md) editions identify the constituent Methods used in the first Suite account.

Different arrangements can produce the same finished movement. In a manual precision task, participants who had practised together resumed the task with a familiar partner at a high level of performance. Pairing them with unfamiliar partners required adaptation. When arranging later work, establish whether the partner can change and what the evidence covers. The result of this study bears on its particular manual task. [Heidersberger and colleagues, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-03341-9)

The distinction between the human whose capability changes and the person or AI providing help has empirical consequences. In [Bastani and colleagues’ 2025 mathematics trial](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122), both AI tutors improved assisted practice. On the subsequent unassisted exam, the standard chatbot group performed worse than the control group; safeguards largely removed that loss without establishing an improvement over the control. HCD.2, HCD.10 and HCD.12 therefore keep supported performance, independent performance and transfer separately assessable. A useful service result can be enough for a work task, while a capability-development claim needs evidence about the learner.

An alternative to treating AI merely as an answer provider is a deliberately designed tutor. [Kestin and colleagues’ 2025 randomized study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6) found higher immediate post-test performance with a structured AI tutor than with the compared active-learning classes in two college-physics lessons. The arrangement included expert-prepared material and controlled progression. This is evidence for that instructional alternative, not for long retention, workplace transfer or every AI tutor.

Together the studies favor selecting and testing the teaching arrangement over choosing or rejecting it by the provider’s label. They do not rank SOM’s bodily mechanisms or establish the effectiveness of every HCD–SOM combination. Those questions retain their own sources and tests.

The distinction also matters in physical collaboration. One experiment found that physically coupled tracking practice improved later individual performance relative to solo practice. A later study using a similar tracking task found no additional individual benefit under its tested conditions. Use a joint performance to answer the actual order for joint work. To establish a change in capability over a period, compare what that person could do before and afterwards under comparable conditions, including the help available. If the recipient also needs to know whether the intervention produced the change, examine what would distinguish its effect from other practice. [Ganesh and colleagues, 2014](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep03824) · [Beckers and colleagues, 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-76706-x)

Related fields help locate this assembly. [IEEE ICICLE's Learning Engineering account](https://sagroups.ieee.org/icicle/learning-engineering-process/) connects learning sciences, human-centered engineering design and data-informed decisions to support learners. This informs the learning branch; the bodily content and its conditions still need their own explanation. [ISMETA's professional account](https://ismeta.org/about-the-profession) describes somatic movement education and its professional boundaries. It distinguishes that practice from clinical diagnosis and treatment.

The name Human Capability Engineering identifies the bounded synthesis explained here. Human denotes the person whose capability is to change, Capability limits the intended subject, and Engineering names the connection between knowledge, deliberate construction and qualified use. The title does not claim that all these Methods belong to one established profession.

Two nearby names would suggest a different principal subject. [Human factors and ergonomics](https://iea.cc/about/what-is-ergonomics/) concerns interactions between humans and other elements of a system and the design of those interactions. [Human Development in the lifespan sense](https://human.cornell.edu/degrees/human-development) studies broader biological, psychological, social and cultural change. Those bodies of knowledge can supply relevant constraints, but neither name identifies the deliberate capability construction and present coverage of this Suite as clearly.
