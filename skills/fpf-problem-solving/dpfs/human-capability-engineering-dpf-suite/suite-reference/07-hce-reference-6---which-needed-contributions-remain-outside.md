## HCE.Reference:6 - Which needed contributions remain outside this edition?

A human target does not make every means of changing a person available in HCD or SOM. Begin with the result required in the person’s work. An existing explanation, performance or technical arrangement may suffice. Where something is missing, the publications below give methods for obtaining or assessing it.

| Needed result | What the publication offers and what the work requires |
| --- | --- |
| A usable explanation | [Explanation Design (EXD)](../../exd/_index.md) helps an author construct or select an explanation for this recipient and helps a reviewer judge whether it serves the intended use. To report what a reader understood, use their own explanation or application to establish what reasoning they recovered with the help available to them. Its instructional branch can contribute to learning; a successful explanation is not by itself evidence of capability change. |
| Rhythmic relations or their learning | [Embodied Rhythmics (RHY)](../../rhy/_index.md) gives ways to represent and reason about rhythmic relations. A score may already establish the prescribed timing. Establish what actually sounded from evidence of the performance. RHY.12 develops human rhythmic capability and RHY.13 examines transfer, retention and support; the full field also serves technical and mixed performers. |
| Music or dance technique, performance or cultural continuation | [Music and Dance Practice Engineering (MDPE)](../../mdpe/_index.md) gives methods for composing, performing and sustaining a practice. For a proposed performance, use the commission or the participants’ artistic choice to determine what the performers must be able to do. To establish what another group actually took up, examine its work and its connection to the source. Wider recognition requires evidence from the audience or community concerned. |
| Organizational capability or its continuing direction and provision | [Management (Management DPF Suite)](../../management-dpf-suite/_index.md) addresses the organized undertaking. Developing one participant may contribute, but does not establish the organization's capability, commitments or authority. |
| A robotic or technical capability | [Engineering (Engineering DPF Suite)](../../engineering-dpf-suite/_index.md) offers methods for designing, realizing and assessing a technical system. Establish which arrangement is actually available before relying on its capability. A missing robot-learning or controller Method still requires its specialist source. |
| A physiological, psychological or clinical change outside the described learning and somatic Methods | Obtain the competent specialist contribution required by the target. [HCD's clinical unlike case (HCD.Application:9)](../hcd/54-hcd-application-9---clinical-unlike-case---return-diagnosis.md) returns diagnosis and treatment to their proper practice. This edition does not supply those interventions merely because they may affect capability. |

The distinction between target and task also matters within human work. Personal management can regulate current priorities and activity without pursuing capability change. Pedagogy, adult education and coaching can provide different learning, advising or regulating contributions. A profession, service label or learner's age does not alone select the mechanism or establish the required expertise. Use the result and conditions to identify the contribution; keep the unprovided result visible.

Further work may establish additional human-capability fields and their connections, or show that a needed result is better obtained externally. This edition's learning and somatic coverage does not define the boundary of the larger human subject.
