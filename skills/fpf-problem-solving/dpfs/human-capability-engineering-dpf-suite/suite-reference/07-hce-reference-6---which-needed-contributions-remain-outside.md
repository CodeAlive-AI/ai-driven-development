## HCE.Reference:6 - Which needed contributions remain outside this edition?

A human target does not make every means of changing a person available in HCD or SOM. Their full Methods determine what this edition can supply.

| Needed result | Available return and coverage boundary |
| --- | --- |
| A usable explanation | [Explanation Design (EXD)](../../exd/_index.md) supplies an explanation the recipient can recover and use. Its instructional branch can contribute to learning; a successful explanation is not by itself evidence of capability change. |
| Rhythmic relations or their learning | [Embodied Rhythmics (RHY)](../../rhy/_index.md) supplies the rhythm-specific account. RHY.12 develops human rhythmic capability and RHY.13 examines transfer, retention and support; the full field also serves technical and mixed performers. |
| Music or dance technique, performance or cultural continuation | [Music and Dance Practice Engineering (MDPE)](../../mdpe/_index.md) supplies its artistic and cultural constructions. It determines the practice result that a human contribution must serve, while wider recognition and reproduction remain separately examined. |
| Organizational capability or its continuing direction and provision | [Management (Management DPF Suite)](../../management-dpf-suite/_index.md) addresses the organized undertaking. Developing one participant may contribute, but does not establish the organization's capability, commitments or authority. |
| A robotic or technical capability | [Engineering (Engineering DPF Suite)](../../engineering-dpf-suite/_index.md) supplies the applicable system construction. A missing robot-learning or controller Method still requires its specialist source. |
| A physiological, psychological or clinical change outside the described learning and somatic Methods | Obtain the competent specialist contribution required by the actual target. [HCD's clinical unlike case (HCD.Application:9)](../hcd/54-hcd-application-9---clinical-unlike-case---return-diagnosis.md) returns diagnosis and treatment to their proper practice. This edition does not supply those interventions merely because they may affect capability. |

The distinction between target and task also matters within human work. Personal management can regulate current priorities and activity without pursuing capability change. Pedagogy, adult education and coaching can provide different learning, advising or regulating contributions. A profession, service label or learner's age does not alone select the mechanism or establish the required expertise. Use the actual result and conditions to identify the contribution; keep the unprovided result visible.

Further work may establish additional human-capability fields and their connections, or show that a needed result is better obtained externally. This edition's learning and somatic coverage does not define the boundary of the larger human subject.
