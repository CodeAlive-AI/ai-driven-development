## HCE.Reference:7 - What preserves the whole when its Methods change?

The discipline retains its identity while theories, teaching techniques, instruments and service arrangements change, provided knowledge of human change remains connected to deliberate capability construction, actual human performance and warranted reconsideration. The engineering target is still the person's capability; the person's participation, permitted interventions and applicable professional conditions constrain how it can be pursued.

A replacement contribution must preserve the relations on which its receiving use relies. A different movement-teaching Method can replace a SOM contribution for a programme when it supplies the required bodily action, retains the relevant whole-task conditions and supports the needed judgement. If the programme only needs the movement to succeed with a cue, a partner can supply the cue. When the aim is for learners to acquire the ability to choose and time a cue for different partners, arrange for them to make that choice under the relevant conditions and judge its effect. Prior work can already establish their ability to do this and can settle whether they need this instruction. To claim they gained the ability, establish what changed. To credit the replacement teaching Method, establish its contribution to that change. The same visible movement can count towards either goal. What the learner and partner each did determines what it establishes. The words used to describe sensations can differ. HCD.5 and HCD.14 supply the corresponding Method choice and evidence-based revision.

Changes run in both directions. A new observation can provide grounds for revising the bodily explanation or programme; a changed task can invalidate the former test; a changed helper can alter what the person must supply. Compatible results remain usable while the affected assumption is reconsidered.

This specialization can contribute to a larger engineering arrangement joining human, organizational and technical capabilities. Each holder's means of change and evidence remain identifiable. A successful service can require all three while still leaving a separate question about what one human can do. The broader system's purpose and acceptance therefore do not replace the human result specified here.

If an additional field supplies a human mechanism and consequential connections that the current pair lacks, the assembly can expand. If a proposed addition only shares an audience or supports an occasional use, an external return may be sufficient. The same comparison can justify keeping a contribution outside, changing a boundary or using a better independent source.
