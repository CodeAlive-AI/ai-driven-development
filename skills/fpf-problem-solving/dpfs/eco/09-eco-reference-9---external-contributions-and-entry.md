## ECO.Reference:9 - External contributions and entry

This DPF relies on the general meanings and selected methods in [FPF Core](../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md), including B.1.5 for constituent and encompassing methods, C.11 for proportionate inquiry and C.36 for cultural continuation. The dependence is from ECO to Core.

Use the Management Suite for the cited financial, accounting, operating, asset, organizational or corporate work. Explanation Design (EXD) addresses explanation. Human Capability Development (HCD), in the Human Capability Engineering Suite, addresses human learning. Use existing qualified contributions directly. Obtain or develop a missing result when the economic question depends on it.

Use the [Foundational Thinking DPF Suite](../foundational-thinking-dpf-suite/_index.md) to construct or interpret a mathematical, physical or computational model when that contribution is needed.

When a supplying Method changes, reconsider the economic conclusion that depends on the changed operation or condition. Use the still-applicable results directly. The Table of Contents and the connected examples in the Readme provide entry points. Stop when the requested result is supported. For the starting question of an economic calculation, see [the earlier ECO.1:4.1](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/1e57ccda08ebbb9d49b7b2190bc2740b72075a13/DPFs/ECONOMIC-REASONING-AND-COORDINATION-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#eco141---recover-the-decision-and-the-consequential-numbers) and [the current ECO.1:4.1](11-eco-1---recover-the-basis-for-an-economic-calculation.md#eco141---identify-the-economic-question-and-the-quantities-that-matter).
