## MNT.Reference:2 - Organizing the Methods around different results

The sixteen patterns are grouped into four publication Parts:

- Part A identifies the maintained use, chooses policy and interprets failure and condition through [MNT.1](12-mnt-1---identify-the-maintained-system-use-and-permission-bo.md)–MNT.4.
- Part B makes a present response feasible and properly bounded through [MNT.5](16-mnt-5---prepare-maintenance-service-capability-spares-tools.md)–MNT.8.
- Part C distinguishes performed intervention, restored functioning and return to use through [MNT.9](20-mnt-9---perform-and-record-the-maintenance-intervention.md)–MNT.11.
- Part D maintains information, programme learning, Method choice, simultaneous coordination and cultural continuation through [MNT.12](23-mnt-12---maintain-maintenance-information-and-configuration.md)–MNT.16.

These are navigation groups. They do not assert four stages of every job or one composite Method containing all sixteen contributions. Enter a Method where the question starts, using current adequate evidence or a qualified equivalent result. An unused sibling contributes no prerequisite.

A typical condition-to-intervention route starts with an interpreted condition from [MNT.4](15-mnt-4---monitor-and-interpret-system-condition-for-maintenan.md) and a choice from MNT.6. Readiness from [MNT.5](16-mnt-5---prepare-maintenance-service-capability-spares-tools.md) and operating coordination from [MNT.7](18-mnt-7---coordinate-maintenance-intervention-and-continuing-o.md) qualify feasible performance. [MNT.8](19-mnt-8---isolate-make-safe-and-authorize-the-maintenance-inte.md) establishes the protection and permission for the selected act. [MNT.9](20-mnt-9---perform-and-record-the-maintenance-intervention.md) then supplies evidence of work and actual configuration; [MNT.10](21-mnt-10---verify-restored-functioning-after-maintenance.md) supplies the functioning result; [MNT.11](22-mnt-11---hand-back-and-authorize-resumed-use-after-maintenan.md) supplies hand-back and resumed-use permission. The route is conditional: it stops earlier when the requested result is already adequate.

Other questions use different connections. [MNT.12](23-mnt-12---maintain-maintenance-information-and-configuration.md)'s maintenance history supports [MNT.13](24-mnt-13---coordinate-the-maintenance-programme-and-fleet-lear.md) only where events and exposure are comparable. [MNT.14](25-mnt-14---compare-maintenance-methods-for-retention-or-change.md) compares a Method's practical worth; [MNT.16](27-mnt-16---deliberately-continue-and-change-maintenance-cultur.md) examines whether variants are transmitted and enacted in the practice population. [MNT.15](26-mnt-15---reconcile-simultaneous-maintenance-operation-and-su.md) handles common conditions that separate successful job decisions cannot establish.

**Constituent actions in ongoing work.** During a repair, a diagnostic observation may constitute part of locating the fault, and fault location may be part of restoring the equipment's required functioning. The relevant skills lie at several grains: handling the instrument, interpreting the reading under the operating conditions and coordinating the intervention with the maintained service. If the service must remain available through standby equipment, a diagnostic action that would disable that standby changes the whole arrangement. [MNT.15](26-mnt-15---reconcile-simultaneous-maintenance-operation-and-su.md) compares the affected commitments; B.1.5.EW helps expose the connection. Completed diagnosis leaves the repair, functioning and hand-back questions with their own Methods.
