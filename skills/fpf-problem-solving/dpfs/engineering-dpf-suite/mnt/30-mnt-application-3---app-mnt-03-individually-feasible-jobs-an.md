## MNT.Application:3 - APP-MNT-03 — Individually feasible jobs and one shared specialist

In the constructed packaging-plant case each job requires its own four hours of one qualified specialist within the same six-hour access window. The specialist needs eight hours for the two jobs. If the contracts instead allow one two-hour inspection to serve both, followed by two hours on each job, the specialist can finish both in six hours when the service and handover conditions permit that order. [MNT.15](26-mnt-15---reconcile-simultaneous-maintenance-operation-and-su.md) contains the whole-demand calculation and the alternatives that change it.

[MNT.5](16-mnt-5---prepare-maintenance-service-capability-spares-tools.md) establishes whether another provider is available and capable. [MNT.6](17-mnt-6---diagnose-condition-and-choose-a-maintenance-response.md) supplies the consequences of deferring either job. [MNT.7](18-mnt-7---coordinate-maintenance-intervention-and-continuing-o.md) relates the chosen arrangement to continuing operation. If the commission includes devising a change, determine whether a different allocation, timing or operating arrangement can meet the combined demands. If the change itself was commissioned, preserve the existing obligations until the governing terms or their holders authorize a revision. To report impossibility of the whole order, account for its permitted alternatives. If only the proposed window has been ruled out, return that narrower conclusion and the condition still open.

Preparation that can overlap production remains useful but does not resolve a shared-person overload. An unplanned extension also changes the capability and fatigue conditions. The team revises allocation or timing rather than treating greater attention as additional capacity.

When a shared fallback or isolation dependency joins the conflict, the comparison expands to those actual System and protection relations. A work-breakdown diagram's hierarchy does not establish them.
