## Engineering.Reference:5 - What should a small engineering team develop, obtain, or delegate?

A startup wants better engineering results and a stronger ability to compete. It is considering staff development, recruitment, providers, a method or platform change, and a different allocation of work among people, AI and robots.

**Name one needed result, then use [OCE.8](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/30-oce-8---compare-human-ai-robotic-and-provider-arrangements-f.md).** “Use more AI” and “train the team” are still broad possibilities. Turn each useful possibility into a whole arrangement that could obtain the result. Keep the intended use, horizon, acceptance criteria and protected conditions the same across candidates.

Use representative work to recover how the team currently operates: who contributes, what they can do, what they are assigned and permitted to do, how contributions meet, and where failures or support burdens occur. [FPF C.38](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/95-c-38---construct-comparable-ways-to-obtain-one-result.md) gives the general same-result comparison; OCE.8 supplies the organizational method.

| Possibility | What the comparison needs |
| --- | --- |
| Develop current members | The people and later work, current capability and transfer conditions, development time and opportunity cost. |
| Recruit or assign another performer | Demonstrated capability, eligibility, assignment, authority, resources and integration needs. |
| Obtain provider expertise | The provider's contribution and duties, acceptance evidence, data and knowledge custody, continued access, recovery and exit; the work left with the startup. |
| Change a method, interface or platform | Evidence of the limitation and the candidate's consequences, including burdens shifted to others; a representative trial when existing grounds are insufficient and its obtainable result warrants the effort. |
| Combine human, AI and robotic contributions | Capability, assignments, permissions, interfaces, oversight, recovery, security and safety limits, and observations of the actual arrangement. |

Several domain questions may contribute. [FPF C.39.MF](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/100-c-39-mf---choose-and-reopen-a-method-development-focus.md) and [FPF C.39.RQ](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/103-c-39-rq---construct-situated-method-requirements.md) clarify the method and required contribution; [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) compares method architectures. [SYSE.15](../syse/42-syse-15---choose-and-refresh-the-engineering-methods-needed.md) and [SYSE.24](../syse/32-syse-24---choose-how-the-project-will-obtain-a-needed-engine.md) help with the engineering repertoire and ways to obtain the result. [SYSE.12](../syse/35-syse-12---develop-or-retain-an-engineering-platform-for-prac.md) investigates a platform limitation, and [SYSE.20](../syse/43-syse-20---reconcile-overlapping-engineering-work-and-require.md) helps reconcile overlapping work.

For capability claims, [FPF A.2.2](../../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/10-a-2-2---system-capability-conditions-measures-and-fit.md) identifies whose capability is asserted and under what conditions. [FPF E.23.CDI](../../../sections/11-part-e---the-fpf-constitution-and-authoring-guides/64-e-23-cdi---developing-capability-for-a-named-work-family.md) addresses development for a particular family of work. [FPF A.15.8](../../../sections/07-cluster-a-v---constitutional-principles-of-the-kernel/22-a-15-8---work-performance-configuration-and-recovery-testing.md) helps when the performing configuration or recovery changes the answer; [FPF A.13](../../../sections/07-cluster-a-v---constitutional-principles-of-the-kernel/12-a-13---the-agential-role-agency-spectrum.md) and [FPF A.15.1](../../../sections/07-cluster-a-v---constitutional-principles-of-the-kernel/15-a-15-1---u-work-dated-performed-work-occurrence.md) distinguish the performer and actual work. Human demand, diagnosis and profile questions in this Reference can use the separate complete HCD publication.

Obtain a strategy, finance, legal, safety, maintenance or other specialist answer when it can change this comparison. State the particular question in the request. The organization makes its choice from the resulting comparison: select an arrangement, conduct a useful authorized probe, reject the candidates, or identify the evidence still needed.

Before dependent work begins, establish its required capability, assignment, permission and authority, and the evidence for protected conditions. Once the operating arrangement exists, OPS.5–OPS.7 can address its narrower admission, case-continuation and priority questions.
