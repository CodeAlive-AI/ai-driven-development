## Engineering.Reference:0 - Engineering, specialization and maintenance

Engineering connects knowledge of what can function with ways to construct, change and sustain functioning systems. The [constituent account (Engineering DPF Suite — Constituent DPFs and their different contributions)](../_index.md#constituent-dpfs-and-their-different-contributions) identifies the fields described by this Suite. Their knowledge constrains realizable effects; realization, qualification and later use can expose a mistaken premise or require a different Method. This continuing connection makes the whole useful beyond any one project.

Organization Change Engineering is a constituent of Management, the organizational specialization of engineering. Maintenance Engineering and Management is a constituent of this Engineering Suite: its technical and management Methods connect failure and condition knowledge to sustained functioning. The general engineering subject is wider than the direct contents of this edition. The [Management Architectural Reference (Management.Reference:3.1)](../../management-dpf-suite/suite-reference/04-management-reference-3---what-makes-these-fields-a-whole.md#managementreference31---organizational-construction-and-maintenance-use) explains organizational construction and the use of maintenance Methods in management work. Management's engineering contribution also concerns the arrangement of current Work, authority and resources; organizational redesign is not its only form of system change.

### Engineering.Reference:0.1 - Organizational construction and change

[Organization Change Engineering (OCE)](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/_index.md) concerns deliberate changes to organizational relations and the capability to obtain a needed contribution. Organization Development is broader: its questions also concern forms and levels of change beyond this construction. Strategy, human learning, continuing operation and product engineering retain the results distinguished in OCE.Reference:1 and OCE.Reference:8.

Knowledge of specialization, information, decisions, incentives and participation constrains which organization can support the intended contribution. [OCE.3](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/25-oce-3---generate-and-compare-organization-concepts.md) constructs and compares interacting choices; [OCE.4](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/26-oce-4---design-an-organization-s-contribution-architecture.md) develops contribution paths and specialization boundaries. Positions and assignments have their own conditions in OCE.5 and OCE.6. OCE.9 realizes a bounded organizational capability; OCE.13 and OCE.14 connect observed consequences to retaining or changing the relevant relations. These connections join organization knowledge to reusable construction and to evidence that can revise it.

In the engineering whole, the organizational construction constrains what a system can actually achieve. OCE.7 connects organization and product or service architectures without requiring them to have the same structure. OCE.11 connects a selected change to continuing service and its real burden. A new arrangement that depends on unavailable participants or unestablished authority cannot supply the claimed functioning merely because its technical components work.

In the management whole, those same organizational relations determine which contributions can be promised, authorized, supported and continued. Management therefore includes their deliberate construction and revision as well as the use of an adequate existing arrangement. A changed direction or service commitment can reopen the organization design; the consequences of that design can require a different commitment. This organizational subject makes OCE a constituent of the Management assembly. Engineering work uses its full constructions through that specialization.

[SEBoK's Enterprise Systems Engineering account](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Enterprise_Systems_Engineering) connects engineering principles to enterprise planning, design, improvement and operation, while distinguishing an enterprise from an organization. [Galbraith's organization-design practice](https://jaygalbraith.com/services/organization-design/) connects structures, processes, rewards and people practices to strategy and treats design as continuing work. These external accounts retain their own uses of enterprise. OCE supplies the Library's organizational distinctions and develops a bounded contribution within these related lines; it does not claim their complete subjects. The broader [AOM Organization Development and Change domain](https://www.aom.org/Divisions-and-Interest-Groups/Organization-Development-and-Change/) helps locate what remains outside.

A different organization model or realization Method can preserve this field when it still connects organizational knowledge, constructed relations, capability and observed consequences. Removing that connection would change the described subject. Changes in a particular assignment or operating condition instead reopen the affected result and its dependent uses; they need not invalidate the complete repertoire.

### Engineering.Reference:0.2 - Maintenance engineering and management

[Maintenance Engineering and Management (MNT)](../mnt/_index.md) connects knowledge of failure and condition to preserving or restoring required functioning. Its principal subject is Systems in use, especially physical and cyber-physical equipment under identifiable operating conditions. Technical maintenance and maintenance management supply different contributions within that field.

The connection is visible inside its Methods. [MNT.2](../mnt/13-mnt-2---select-and-reopen-the-maintenance-policy.md) constructs a policy from failure behaviour, task effects and the feasible response. A condition warning is useful only when detection, interpretation, support, access and intervention can fit its applicable interval. MNT.5 and MNT.7 supply the support and operating arrangement; MNT.8–MNT.11 distinguish protected intervention, performed work, functioning evidence and resumed-use permission. MNT.12 and MNT.13 connect configuration and failure history to changes in policy, support or programme. These are conditional connections: a sufficient policy recommendation does not require performing an intervention.

In Engineering, maintainability and response conditions constrain system design and realization. An inaccessible component, an unsupported replacement or an unrepresentative functioning test can defeat the claimed sustained effect. Results from use can reopen the failure account, task choice or system architecture. In a management use, the same maintenance field connects service commitments to policy, resources, access windows and a continuing programme. Failure history can change those commitments; a support shortage can change which technical response is feasible. Both connections depend on the bounded maintenance knowledge and practice. A manager with the requisite capability can perform the maintenance Method, or obtain its result from another performer; the technical conditions apply in either arrangement.

The [GFMAM Maintenance Framework, second edition](https://www.gfmam.org/sites/default/files/2021-02/GFMAM%20Maintenance%20Framework%20-%202nd%20Edition%20Final.pdf) distinguishes maintenance engineering and maintenance management in §2. Its §§6.4–6.9 connect task strategy, resources, execution, performance, information and programme management. That connected subject supports keeping the full maintenance field together.

Maintenance and asset management retain different questions. A maintenance policy for one pump selects how to preserve or restore required functioning. An asset-management comparison selects continued use, renewal, replacement, repurposing or withdrawal by value, costs, risks and service effects. Either can concern one asset or many; fleet scale does not decide the boundary. [MNT.Reference:1](../mnt/01-mnt-reference-1---the-working-problem-and-practical-gain.md) explains that distinction. A replacement can require both contributions.

New equipment, evidence or task technology can change the policy while preserving maintenance as a field. Its continuity depends on the connection between required functioning, supported technical reasoning, feasible action and feedback from use. A value-oriented decision about whether to retain the asset belongs to the adjacent asset-management question; it is not a broader maintenance policy.

### Engineering.Reference:0.3 - Specialization and use across Suite boundaries

This Engineering Suite edition directly includes Systems Engineering, Maintenance Engineering and Management, Knowledge-Corpus Access Engineering, Semantic Integration Engineering and Control Architecture Design. OCE has its complete home in Management, where organizational relations, authority and capability participate in the construction of supportable commitments. Each DPF retains its full Methods, cases, sources and limits. A reader reaches the same full contribution through a cross-Suite return when the work needs it.

The alternative of using a field without including it as a direct constituent retains access to its full Methods. An engineering practitioner can perform OCE's organizational construction through Management; a manager can perform MNT's policy or programme Method through Engineering. Neither use requires commissioning another performer. OCE's direct place in Management follows its organizational target and the continuing relations explained above. MNT's policy, provision and programme serve the distinct maintenance subject: preserving or restoring required functioning through failure and condition knowledge. Their usefulness to management does not require including the whole maintenance field there.

Splitting either repertoire into engineering and management lists would obscure connections inside its Methods, such as OCE's interaction of allocation and participation or MNT's relation between a technical warning and obtainable support. The complete languages preserve these connections at their respective homes. Finance can likewise supply a constraint to an engineering choice, and Operations can supply qualified capacity to maintenance, without their whole fields entering this edition's direct roster. A project uses the contributions it needs; the repertoire is not a compulsory project route.

Cross-Suite use requires keeping both sides of a changed dependency available. A revised organizational premise may affect realization and commitments; a revised maintenance premise may affect functioning and the service programme. Reconsider the affected connections when such a premise changes, while retaining independently adequate results. The question-led sections below show the particular receiving uses.

### Engineering.Reference:0.4 - Capability development and wider performing practices

[Human Capability Engineering](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/_index.md) is the specialized engineering discipline concerned with the deliberate formation, change, restoration and maintenance of a person's capabilities. Its first, incomplete Suite edition supplies the full HCD and SOM descriptions. The five direct DPFs of this Engineering edition connect to that specialized Suite; this publication arrangement leaves the human field's engineering contribution intact.

[Human Capability Development (HCD)](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/_index.md) describes deliberate change of a human holder's capability for intended later Work. Its knowledge of learning, practice, feedback and transfer constrains capability targets, programmes, learning products and development arrangements. Evidence of what the person can actually do can revise the target or arrangement and can change which human contribution a system design may rely on. This continuing connection of knowledge, construction and use supplies its engineering contribution. A course, a capability model and a person's realized capability remain different results; adequate existing capability, changed support or an external contribution can remove the need for development.

[Somatic Movement Modeling (SOM)](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/som/_index.md) specializes that human-capability contribution. Its subject is bodily modeling and learning within an already known movement practice. The teacher or practice supplies the technique and criteria; SOM connects bodily experience, regulated changes and observed action. Its contrasts, effort regulation, combined changes and adaptation to contact, support or load can change what the learner can enact. Feedback from that action can revise the learner's model and the teaching arrangement. The result is not a complete theory of movement or an account of every use of a human body.

HCD therefore does not include all human learning, and SOM does not include all embodied practices. Their engineering contribution follows from their constructive subjects and these connections, not from the physical bearer of a capability or a label in a DPF title. A particular engineering project can use sufficient capability evidence without performing development, just as a development task can finish with a sufficient design before a person has acquired the capability. The fields continue when particular learning theories or techniques change, provided knowledge, deliberate construction and evidence of the intended capability remain connected.

The human holder whose capability is to change, the person or AI supplying instruction, and the undertaking that will use the result occupy different positions. Changing the teaching provider can leave the human subject unchanged; developing a robot instead requires the applicable robot Methods. Pedagogy, andragogy and coaching can supply particular human-development contributions. Managing a team can instead change its collective Work, authority or resources. Those professional names do not establish identical Methods or separate disciplines; use the actual subject, intended change and knowledge needed for it.

[Music-and-Dance Practice Engineering (MDPE)](../../mdpe/_index.md) explains a wider practice: creating and performing material, learning, recognition, transmission, memory and cultural continuation. The engineering contributions inside that practice remain available. [Configure and Coordinate a Music-or-Dance Performing Whole (MDPE.3)](../../mdpe/27-mdpe-3---configure-and-coordinate-a-music-or-dance-performin.md) connects Systems Engineering results to performance-specific relations, simultaneous structures, timing, cues, material and correction. It constructs the smallest needed performing configuration and tests the uncertain coordination conditions. A score, stage plan or collection of individually capable performers does not establish the configured whole.

[Test a Support-Environment Change for a Music-or-Dance Practice (MDPE.22)](../../mdpe/40-mdpe-22---test-a-support-environment-change-for-a-music-or-d.md) recovers a surrounding relation that blocks an intended practice result, compares bounded changes, realizes the selected change through the relevant engineering or management Method and observes what happens in the practice. Environment-change Work, an obtaining support relation, a later practice observation and an attributed causal effect remain distinct. A failed or worsened trial can support a complete keep, revise or stop decision. This contribution does not make the entire practice a support system or reduce cultural continuation to system realization.

[Embodied Rhythmics (RHY)](../../rhy/_index.md) has rhythmic relations as its subject: attacks and rests, continuous movement, accents, timing, grouping, a common reference and coordination. Its Methods support recognizing, representing, altering, performing, teaching and observing those relations. Some uses develop a person's capability, and some constrain a performing configuration; the full field is not limited to either contribution. Its overlap with bodily learning therefore does not make it a specialization of SOM.

MDPE and RHY retain complete independent descriptions in the Library. Engineering can use the specific constructions and constraints just described while their other results keep their own conditions. The direct returns preserve these engineering contributions without making construction the complete subject of either field. A future change in the described fields or their continuing relations could change this composition; a new performance project or a different physical performer alone does not.

### Engineering.Reference:0.5 - The system-thinking long mantra across engineering contributions

A project can contain sound answers about a design, a production method, maintenance and information access yet still lack a workable whole. Each answer may rely on conditions that another answer removes. The **system-thinking long mantra** starts with intended receiving use and the wider functioning in which a system-of-interest would participate at its operating time. Those grounds come before inward reasoning about construction and backward reasoning about obtaining Methods, capable creators and their cooperation. Several projects can serve one product SoI. Work on an organization as a creator retains the product or products and their intended uses as its grounds, while fully engineering the organization and accepting its bounded contribution. A new contract or external consultant does not by itself change that SoI. An independently supplied tool or component can be its maker's SoI and a means or part in the receiving undertaking; retain the real use, decision and acceptance boundaries that justify the distinction. FPF A.1.STM:4.1–4.3.1 explains why those questions belong together and how partial answers can be revised. [SYSE.Reference:1.1](../syse/02-syse-reference-1---choose-a-useful-result-and-combine-its-ne.md#sysereference11---the-system-thinking-long-mantra-in-engineering) develops the connections inside Systems Engineering.

Across this Suite, the maintenance answer constrains more than a later service schedule. Access, detection, replacement and qualified return to functioning can change the construction that should be chosen. Conversely, a changed construction or operating condition can invalidate a failure model or intervention policy. MNT supplies the maintenance reasoning; the resulting conditions return to the engineering choice rather than remain an independent maintenance document.

Semantic integration similarly constrains what the system can do: compatible message formats do not establish that two participants use the same referent, unit or interpretation. SIE develops the meanings, mappings and validation needed by the receiving operation. A revised interpretation can change an interface or invalidate an earlier system-level conclusion. KCAE supplies access to the documentary knowledge needed for these decisions. Finding a source is insufficient when its conditions or edition cannot be recovered; a correct design argument cannot be used reliably if the evidence it relies on is unavailable or misunderstood. These contributions connect through the actual receiving operation, not merely because all four disciplines appear in the Suite.

The creators' cooperation can require organizational or human-capability contributions from the specialized Suites described above. Those results bring their own conditions: an acquired ability does not itself grant authority, and an organizational assignment does not establish technical capability. A changed division of work can alter the feasible design; a changed design can require different cooperation. The project account has to accommodate both directions.

The reminder gives a direction of justification, while a difficulty can draw attention to any part of the work. B.5.PI explains how a suitable mantra can reveal an unexamined question before a complaint. A.1.STM:4.0 connects that proactive use with following a noticed difficulty to the project result it affects. Once the systemic project question is current, recover or reuse the intended use and wider functioning before justifying the construction or provision choice. Then compare alternatives whose answers can be true together. The actual order of departments, documents and Work follows its own conditions; the supplying Methods and subject knowledge make the proposed combination workable.

### Engineering.Reference:0.6 - The elegance mantra and reducing repeated firefighting

When engineering or operating work repeatedly needs urgent correction, or a local efficiency gain leaves the receiving work no better off, use **FPF C.42, Reduce Avoidable Work While Preserving the Needed Result**, for the general method. Its **elegance mantra** connects the questions below. This section explains their engineering application. Here *elegance* concerns reducing avoidable work while preserving the needed result and its necessary conditions. Reducing repeated firefighting means changing what recreates a difficulty, alongside any immediate response needed to protect current work.

> Keep the needed result in view; remove unneeded work, prevent avoidable rework, distinguish waiting from processing, change the cause worth changing, and carry the change into the next performance.

These questions connect through the result and the effort needed to obtain it. Their order helps explain the reasoning; work can begin at a consequential question whose answer is already needed. Supported partial answers remain useful. An unknown cause can leave one proposed change undecided while another adequately supported response remains available.

**Establish what is needed in use.** A faster way of producing an unneeded result consumes resources without supplying the missing contribution. [FPF A.1.STM:4.1–4.3.1](../../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/07-a-1-stm---connect-project-reasoning.md) connects intended use to what must function and how it can be obtained. Removing work requires checking what would be lost, including protection, maintenance or needed knowledge. An experiment that rules out a plausible design can supply such knowledge; its negative result is not by itself avoidable rework. If the need changes, reconsider the work justified by it, with the people who can change the affected commitments.

**Prevent the particular rework.** A mismatch between the required configuration and the one actually made can force correction, repeated manufacture and renewed checking. [SYSE.13](../syse/38-syse-13---establish-configuration-identity-variants-and-effe.md) establishes the configuration identity and applicability needed by the decision. [OPS.18:5.5](../../management-dpf-suite/ops/52-ops-18---control-operating-quality-and-reliability.md#ops1855---an-unresolved-cause-need-not-delay-a-supported-correction) shows the connection: a customer approves proof P2, but the work station supplies P1. The available authorized correction retrieves the approved proof, checks its edition at the station and requires first-article acceptance. Catching P1 before printing protects the order without spoiled booklets. A small check can therefore reduce total work. Compare additional control with the remaining exposure and the work it would add. The full case distinguishes applying this protection from establishing which failure caused the mismatch and from observing recurrence over later orders.

**Separate waiting from processing.** Elapsed time includes both. Faster processing can leave the receiving result late when it still waits for a ready input, resource or decision. [OPS.8](../../management-dpf-suite/ops/36-ops-8---coordinate-queues-and-buffers.md) and [OPS.9](../../management-dpf-suite/ops/39-ops-9---diagnose-and-treat-the-current-operating-constraint.md) recover those mechanisms and compare changes against the accepted result. OPS.9:5.4 shows why the deadline for a first result and sustained output can favour different changes. Removing a required acceptance decision, or moving a queue outside the measured boundary, does not establish an improvement for the receiving work.

**Choose a change that acts on the loss.** Ending an unnecessary operation, preventing a configuration error, changing a handoff and improving processing are different possibilities. A technology change may address several causes at once; it need not wait for every other question to close. Compare the obtainable gain over the relevant period with construction, introduction, support and exception handling, including effort shifted to other participants. Keeping an adequate manual method can be the better choice, as the [handover case](04-engineering-reference-2---why-examine-a-handover-that-arrive.md) explains. [SYSE.39](../syse/60-syse-39---decide-whether-and-how-to-reduce-the-total-burden.md) develops this comparison for repetitive software-platform work. For a recurring operating method, [OPS.16](../../management-dpf-suite/ops/50-ops-16---decide-whether-and-how-to-change-an-operating-metho.md) connects the proposed change to a supported trial; changing responsibility or authority requires the corresponding organizational decision.

**Return the change to work and work to the explanation.** [FPF A.3.1.MI](../../../sections/05-part-a---kernel-architecture-cluster/28-a-3-1-mi---introduce-a-method-into-practice-and-decide-what.md) distinguishes the changed method, its introduction, actual performance and later use. Make the applicable instruction and support available, develop missing capability where needed, and examine what happens in the next performance. [OCE.10](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/32-oce-10---choose-a-response-to-participation-or-working-cultu.md) helps when access, workload or participation conditions explain a difficulty. A recurrence can reopen the configuration account; a new queue can reopen the capacity diagnosis; changed receiving needs can reopen which work is worth retaining. Preserve answers whose grounds still hold. Publishing a revised instruction alone establishes none of these performance results.

This connected account concerns avoidable effort for a needed contribution. Development can instead create new knowledge, purposes and capabilities worth pursuing. The [system-thinking account](02-engineering-reference-0---engineering-specialization-and-mai.md#engineeringreference05---the-system-thinking-long-mantra-across-engineering-contributions) connects changes to a product or its creator with the intended use that justifies them; the [method-development entry](03-engineering-reference-1---find-a-starting-pattern.md#engineeringreference17---improve-a-method-or-an-organization) leads to work on the way of obtaining it. Reducing present effort is one possible contribution to that continuation.

### Engineering.Reference:0.8 - Connect professional contributions to the required result

Use this account when a professional map or job title leaves unclear which work a result requires, how different specialties belong together, or what a new method or performer changes. The subject is the organization of professional contributions: their objects and results, the knowledge and methods they require, and their combination in actual work. Product management, organizational development and human capability development expose different parts of that subject. A familiar, sufficiently specified contribution can go directly to its full subject method.

Begin with what must become obtainable: a functioning system, a useful and supportable offer, an organization's continuing performance, a person's acquired capability, or knowledge on which another decision can rely. Work back to the required contributions and forward through their receiving uses. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/83-c-32-mwa-pp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-p.md) supplies the full method for comparing methods, performance and provision. [OCE.3:4.1.1.1](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/_index.md) explains when dependencies among decisions and contributions require organizational coordination. The professional account below makes the subject contributions and their relations recognizable before that arrangement is chosen.

The useful result is an explained professional arrangement: what must be supplied and why, which knowledge and methods warrant it, how the contributions fit, who can perform them with what support, and what a changed condition would reopen. Recognizing a contribution is an entry to this work. Qualification of its method, performer, authority and joint provision supplies the grounds for relying on the arrangement.

The examples help recover this common construction through different professional relations. The pump distinguishes the contribution from the target; Relay exposes whether separate contributions can actually be provided together; the human-development profile distinguishes a worthwhile capability direction, participation in learning and acquired capability. Use each case to identify the relation that selects the professional work, then return to the arrangement you are constructing. Understanding why a particular pump, service or programme works supplies a case-specific result. The broader result is being able to select and connect contributions when the objects, purposes or available means differ.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.1 - Two questions behind the professional map

Anatoly Levenchuk's Russian *Guide to Systems Engineering for Engineer-Managers* uses **archroles** for broad professional contributions to creating and developing systems. Its map crosses two questions. **What kind and scale of system are we working on?** The source distinguishes cyberphysical systems, organizations, clientele, human capability and an investor base. A component, a complete product and its providing organization also place work at different scales. **What contribution are we making to that target?** Developing its use concept, shaping its architecture, designing its functioning, realizing it and operating it have different immediate results.

The first question selects the subject knowledge. Designing a pump requires knowledge of the relevant physical behavior, materials and operating conditions. Designing the organization that maintains pumps requires knowledge of work dependencies, commitments, decisions and actual cooperation. Designing how a technician acquires diagnostic ability requires knowledge of diagnosis and of learning. Similar architectural reasoning can help all three while leaving these professional requirements distinct.

The second question differentiates work on the same target. A functional account of a pump explains what it does and how its functions interact. A constructive design proposes what carries that behavior. A manufacturing method explains how to obtain the proposed embodiment. Each constrains the others, but an answer about one leaves a different question about the others. The engineering line of the source map makes these contributions visible:

| Professional contribution | Result needed from it | Knowledge and connection that make the result usable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visionary or concept work | A reasoned account of valuable use and a system that could support it. | The receiving activity, alternatives and constraints connect the proposed benefit to a feasible system concept. |
| Architecture | Decisions about structures and characteristics that constrain several subsequent choices. | Knowledge of the system's interactions, alternatives and consequences establishes which constraints must be shared and which choices can remain local. |
| Functional design, the source's applied methodologist | A synthesis of the functions, dynamics and behavior of the system being engineered, whether product or creator. | Subject knowledge explains the transformations, interactions and conditions that a proposed embodiment must support. |
| Constructive design | Components and relations capable of carrying the selected functioning. | Knowledge of possible bearers connects function, architecture, realization, operation and maintenance. |
| Realization engineering | A way to produce, integrate, deploy or change the system. | The selected construction, available means and acceptance conditions must permit an actual working result. |
| Operation | The required functioning under actual operating conditions, with observations of what happens. | The use commitment, support and operating limits determine what can be sustained and which observations return to design. |
| Internal-platform engineering | Working means and access for the other contributors. | Their actual work determines the platform's required contribution; the platform has its own use, architecture, realization and operation. |

**Developer is a composite label in the source map.** Its band groups functional design, constructive design, realization and operation. The surrounding source also uses development for broader work before operation and for narrower team design. Recover the intended scope in each use. The label helps describe a combination; its presence does not add another independent contribution alongside those it contains.

The source's applied methodologist has a particularly consequential boundary. Adding automatic stopping at excessive pressure changes a pump's functional behavior: sensing, the stopping condition and the interruption of flow must fit together. Casting a casing instead of machining it, while retaining the required pump behavior, changes realization and its compatibility with the design. If the immediate engineering subject is the production line, the line's own transformations become the functional-design subject. The products and intended uses that the line must serve still ground the required capability and its acceptance. [SYSE.5](../syse/26-syse-5---develop-an-engineered-system-s-functional-organizat.md) develops this functioning-and-bearer construction. A researcher or practitioner developing another method keeps that method's actual subject; the local source term does not name every kind of methodology.

These contributions arise from different questions needed to obtain and sustain the target's functioning. Their unity comes from that shared result and their reciprocal constraints. A maintenance limitation can change the construction; an unavailable production method can change the architecture; an unaffordable operating arrangement can change the useful offer. [Engineering.Reference:0.5](02-engineering-reference-0---engineering-specialization-and-mai.md#engineeringreference05---the-system-thinking-long-mantra-across-engineering-contributions) develops those returns. Paragraph order can help teach the questions, while actual work begins with the unresolved contribution and returns wherever its grounds change.

This map is useful within its engineering subject. Research can seek a warranted explanation without undertaking to construct a system. Cultural or artistic practice can have purposes and grounds that the engineering map does not exhaust. Even within engineering, an established supplier result can make fresh performance of one contribution unnecessary. Treat the map as a way to recover work, with a declared subject and useful variation, rather than an exhaustive register of professions.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.2 - From contributions to roles and positions

A **professional contribution** is work needed by a receiving result. A **discipline** connects knowledge about a field with reusable ways of investigating, acting and revising that knowledge. A **professional repertoire** is the set of methods and judgements a practitioner or practice is prepared to draw on. A **profession** is a sustained field of professional practice with a recognizable repertoire and expectations of competent work; its social recognition, entry requirements and occupational arrangements depend on its setting. A **specialization** narrows a contribution by its subject, scale, method or working conditions. These distinctions explain different connections to actual work.

A profession can draw on several disciplines. An instructional designer needs learning methods and sufficient knowledge of what is to be learned; a product manager uses research, economic, engineering and operating contributions. A discipline can serve several professions: statistical inference can inform product inquiry, teaching research and reliability work. Using a financial result to choose a product does not make the whole financial discipline a constituent of product management. For a stronger claim that several knowledge and practice contributions form one discipline, [FPF C.20:4.1–4.5](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/39-c-20---composition-of-u-discipline.md) requires the actual field boundary, assembly, knowledge–practice connections and continuity conditions.

Knowledge makes professional methods selective. A theory of fluid flow constrains which pump design can work. Knowledge of learning constrains what a demonstration or exercise can establish about a person. Knowledge of financial claims and payment dates constrains what an attractive valuation can establish about funding. Practice can expose a false assumption, a missing relation or a useful new method. The professional field remains intelligible through that connection even as its tools and job titles change.

A role description expresses the contribution expected in a situation. A job or organizational position combines continuing responsibilities under actual organizational arrangements. An assignment connects a performer to specified work. These relations are many-to-many: one position can combine several contributions; one contribution can need several specialists; one specialist can serve several undertakings; one method can be used across several professions. [FPF E.10.ROLE](../../../sections/11-part-e---the-fpf-constitution-and-authoring-guides/31-e-10-role---recovering-what-role-means-in-the-current-claim.md) resolves a role statement when those meanings are being confused.

Allocation adds conditions that the repertoire alone cannot supply. The selected person, team, service or AI configuration needs the relevant capability, information, means, time and permission. A capable analyst may lack authority to commit expenditure. A manager with that authority may need the analyst's result. The two contributions must meet at an actual decision. One person can carry both where capability, authority and available time fit; two titles do not provide two independent time budgets.

[OCE.4](../../management-dpf-suite/oce/26-oce-4---design-an-organization-s-contribution-architecture.md) connects contribution paths, decisions, handovers and exceptions. OCE.5 determines whether and how continuing positions should carry that work; OCE.6 turns a selected design into an obtainable arrangement. C.32.MWA.PP:4.2 obtains the subject methods and usable joins; :4.5–4.8 compares whole alternatives and their joint burden. These methods allow an adequate existing arrangement, selective assistance, integrated performance, specialized performance and external provision to remain real alternatives.

Use the receiving result to decide which professional distinction matters now. A pump may need a changed behavior and a new production method. The organization may need a different maintenance commitment. Its technician may separately choose to learn the diagnosis. Those are connected results with different holders, operations and evidence. A common employer, project name or diagram does not merge their structures. Conversely, preserving their distinctions need not create separate departments.

The source's product-manager example combines visionary work with operational management of priorities and timing. It illustrates one professional combination. [Management.Reference:3.2–3.3](../../management-dpf-suite/suite-reference/_index.md) develops organizational and product repertoires at their own subjects. [HCE.Reference:4.1–4.3](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/suite-reference/_index.md) explains the different professional contributions to a person's learning, a programme and its provider. These profiles use the same distinction between contribution and allocation while retaining the knowledge each practice requires.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.3 - A product manager changes a maintenance service

Consider the fictional company Relay. Its service reports equipment condition and recommends components for a customer to inspect. The customer now requests automatic spare-parts ordering. Relay's PM must prepare a justified offer that the company can provide alongside its existing commitments. The organization has identified who may approve expenditure and amend the agreement; the PM's title alone does not carry those decisions.

The required result is a supportable service choice. [CUST.1](../../management-dpf-suite/cust/14-cust-1---manage-client-variation-and-develop-repeatable-offe.md) compares retaining recommendations, a bounded customer-confirmed option, Relay's own automatic service, partner provision, a narrower offer or refusal. Each option needs conditions for diagnosis, part compatibility, availability, delivery and support. A prototype ordering screen supplies only one contribution to that choice.

The professional map reveals how the answers must affect one another. Concept work identifies the useful change for the customer. [SYSE.2](../syse/23-syse-2---develop-linked-use-and-system-concepts.md) connects that use to a changed system concept: placing an order introduces an external action and its consequences. Functional design must connect the diagnosis, choice of part, stock information, authorization and fulfilment. [SYSE.5](../syse/26-syse-5---develop-an-engineered-system-s-functional-organizat.md) constructs and compares those arrangements; [SYSE.6](../syse/27-syse-6---decide-and-reopen-the-engineering-architecture.md) settles consequential constraints. Realization and operation then need compatible contributions, including the means of obtaining supplier data.

Management brings these engineering conditions back to the offer. A technically possible order can still exceed the service team's capacity or require a decision that no participant has authority to make. [Management.Reference:4.2](../../management-dpf-suite/suite-reference/05-management-reference-4---development-shared-support-and-effe.md#managementreference42---a-proposal-reaches-work-through-judgement-decisions-and-contributions) connects the proposal to judgement, effective decisions and available contributions. The PM can assemble and compare the proposal without personally performing every engineering operation or gaining every participant's authority.

For the initial choice, suppose the diagnostic evidence covers a specified class of faults, the supplier's live stock feed meets the required freshness condition, and Relay has provided the checking and support capacity for four orders per day alongside existing commitments. The customer retains approval of each purchase and accepts that condition for the proposed service. The PM therefore proposes customer-confirmed ordering for the qualified fault class, within that capacity and stock condition, for the authorized participants to approve. Unattended ordering for all reported faults would require diagnostic, purchasing and execution grounds that this arrangement has not established. The proposal now names a useful bounded service and what must remain true for it to work.

Assume for this case that a qualified AI configuration can analyze the supplied equipment information and prototype alternatives. The PM can use those results while a subject specialist examines diagnosis and part compatibility, and the authorized participants settle the service commitment. A more capable integrated configuration could carry more work if it meets the same conditions.

**Now the supplier withdraws live stock access before launch.** The agent can still give useful equipment recommendations, but the proposed ordering service can no longer support its former availability and delivery assumptions. Return the changed input to the dependent service choice. Keep analysis whose grounds still hold; reopen the ordering arrangement and the promise that relied on it.

Manual confirmation is a real alternative only with real provision. Suppose each supplier confirmation takes 30 minutes, the customer needs four per day, and the available operator has one hour per day after existing commitments. The arrangement supports at most two such confirmations under those assumptions. Assigning the already-occupied PM an additional operator title supplies no missing hour. Relay can obtain additional capacity, find a suitable supplier, negotiate a different service, or retain its recommendation offer. With no additional provision and no accepted change to the request, it retains the supported recommendation service and returns the ordering proposal for revision. If an alternative is selected, qualify its changed execution where needed rather than transferring the old stock-dependent result.

If a manager nevertheless requires the PM to retain the original delivery claim in the proposal, distinguish a request to make a new commitment from an existing obligation. Use [Engineering.Reference:21.3](23-engineering-reference-21---they-understand-or-agree-why-does.md#engineeringreference213---examine-a-disputed-basis-for-requiring-work) to recover the factual basis, any current duty, the grounds of the objection and who can make a revision effective. Lost access does not itself cancel an existing obligation; an obligation does not restore access. Carry the decision into the actual commitment and provision, including a decision to keep the supported existing service.

The map has helped locate the affected work: supplier access changed, so functional provision and the service promise changed; independent equipment analysis remained useful. This example concerns one offering change. Questions about product research, pricing, commercial risk or a wider lifecycle require their corresponding professional methods and knowledge; naming an entrepreneurial or PM contribution leaves those questions to be answered.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.4 - Whose capability must change?

The *Person Engineering* guide distinguishes the person deciding about their own development from someone helping another person develop. One participant can occupy both positions. The referential distinction is whose change is being considered and from whose position the work is undertaken. In helping, recover the recipient's intended contribution and their own decisions; in self-development, establish what you need to be able to do and obtain suitable help. The helper's successful performance and the recipient's acquired ability are different results.

Suppose Relay's analyst and an agent produce a suitable maintenance recommendation together. If the promised service includes that continuing support, the joint result may be sufficient. Now the customer asks for a particular technician to handle the relevant incidents on site when that assistance is unavailable. The promised performer and conditions have changed. The agent's answer can still help, but the technician must acquire and demonstrate the required contribution under those later conditions. Retain tools that will actually be available; independence from every aid is not an automatic learning objective.

[HCD.1](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/17-hcd-1-derive-human-capability-demand-from-representative-lat.md) derives that person's required contribution, and [HCD.2](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/20-hcd-2---compose-and-compare-human-capability-development-pro.md) compares feasible ways to obtain it. The subject method for diagnosis and the method for helping someone learn it supply different work. The source's teaching team can combine subject specialists, instructional designers, tutors and providers. Those designing learning need enough subject understanding to distinguish good performance and useful correction; they can work with subject specialists without personally performing every subject operation. These are contributions to secure, rather than a prescribed set of staff posts. [HCD.7](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/24-hcd-7---arrange-providers-access-tools-and-ai-support-for-hu.md) obtains the needed help. [HCD.9](../../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/26-hcd-9---perform-deliberate-practice-with-feedback.md) connects the person's attempt, feedback and response, with another attempt when the correction requires one.

An AI agent can also receive help, but the relevant change may be to instructions, retrieved knowledge, retained memory, tools or access. Model-parameter training is another mechanism with its own conditions. Locate the missing contribution, make the appropriate change and examine subsequent use; reading a text alone does not establish any particular persistent change. [FPF C.36.RP](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/129-c-36-rp---sustain-and-renew-shared-ways-of-working.md) connects shared ways of working with learning, help and provision; [C.36.MC](../../../sections/09-part-c---kernel-extension-specifications/130-c-36-mc---qualify-and-develop-a-method-culture.md) examines their continuing use. Changing a subject method, a teaching explanation, a human ability and an agent's configuration can affect one another without forming one compulsory learning sequence.

Return to the needed result after any such change. A new capability can make a different allocation possible; lost support can require a smaller promise or another provider; evidence from performance can call the professional account itself into question. Keep the contribution whose grounds remain adequate and obtain the next missing answer from its subject discipline.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.5 - Obtain the professional repertoire needed by this work

A professional map becomes useful when it leads to methods and qualified results. Start at the receiving action: what must somebody be able to decide, do or continue? Identify what it consumes, then recover the professional question that obtains that input. Continue backward only while a consequential contribution is unexplained. An available, adequate result can terminate a branch.

For a pump-maintenance offer, evidence about customer interruptions can justify a different service. That evidence does not determine the pump's behavior. The functional proposal needs physical and diagnostic knowledge; the proposed service needs feasible observation, action and support. If a technician is also to learn diagnosis, the correct diagnostic method supplies content for learning while HCD supplies the capability-development work. The same result can serve several receiving operations without turning all those operations into one profession.

Search for the required change or result, rather than beginning with a fashionable occupational name. Read a promising method's actual account: its subject, inputs, operations, assumptions and result. The [common Library instruction](../../../library/USING-FPF.md) explains that search and source return. If a method is available but the performer cannot apply it, obtain learning, demonstration or support. If nobody can supply the needed way, return that missing contribution to the relevant subject inquiry through C.39.

For each consequential join, establish what the receiving practitioner can use. A design specialist may need a range of loads and a permissible failure condition, rather than the researcher's interview notes. A finance specialist may need dated operating consequences, rather than an annual target. An instructional designer may need the criterion distinguishing a sound diagnosis from a plausible one, rather than a polished answer. Obtain the conversion or interpretation that makes the result usable. It may be supplied by one of those specialists or by their joint work.

These dependencies explain where specialization helps. Separating work can preserve deep expertise, independent challenge and reuse across several projects. Integration can reduce repeated translation, loss of context and waiting. The useful boundary follows the nature of the dependency: stable, interpretable results can permit relatively independent work; recurrent changes to shared assumptions can require closer cooperation or a different division of work. [SYSE.44](../syse/65-syse-44---divide-and-recombine-work-across-agents.md) compares divided and integrated performance and the work of recombination. OCE.3 and OCE.4 develop the organizational decision and authority relations.

Compare complete ways of obtaining the same result. For example, a specialist can return a bounded diagnostic conclusion; a qualified integrated performer can carry inquiry through recommendation; or a provider can supply an accepted service result. The alternatives differ in preparation, information access, response time, exception handling, independence and continuing provision. An external result is adequate only if the recipient can use it under the needed conditions. A low-priced tool requiring scarce expert reconstruction can impose more total work than a more complete service.

The choice also depends on which capabilities must remain with which participant. When a particular person's contribution matters, [A.2.2:2.1](../../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md) keeps capability relative to its conditions and support; [A.13:4.3](../../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md) helps specify the agency characteristic relevant to a boundary of independence. Decide separately who may initiate, choose means, change shared state or accept a commitment. A wide capability and a wide mandate can coincide, but neither establishes the other.

A usable arrangement must fit together in actual work. Obtain access, qualified contributors, windows of availability and effective commitments; preserve work already promised. Count the same person's concurrent demands together and shared preparation once where it is genuinely shared. Establish who receives an exception and can change the affected decision. C.32.MWA.PP:4.7 and :4.9 supply this construction and its realization. Stop when the present use has an explained obtainable arrangement or a precise unresolved contribution that prevents relying on it. The whole professional field need not be decomposed for a settled local question.

#### Engineering.Reference:0.8.6 - Reconsider the arrangement when its grounds change

Follow the changed condition through the relations it affects. The professional names can remain unchanged while the right allocation changes; a durable change in the knowledge and practice of a field can also justify revising the professional map itself.

| What changes | Professional question to reopen | What remains usable while its grounds hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The promised result or its holder | Which contributions and criteria now establish that result? A joint answer and a named person's acquired capability need different work. | Subject knowledge or partial results still relevant to the new use. |
| The subject method | Which operations, inputs and judgements changed, and what do their receiving uses now need? | Unaffected methods, evidence and professional contributions. |
| The performer | Can this person, team, provider or AI configuration supply the contribution under the required conditions, including a consequential exception? | The method and target criteria when neither changed. |
| Provision or authority | Is access, time, support or a required decision still obtainable; which promise depended on it? | Competence and technical results whose premises remain valid. |
| The scale or interdependence of work | Which previously separate demands now compete, and which shared contribution has become worthwhile? | Local results that remain compatible with the new whole. |
| The professional division itself | Does a sustained new problem, knowledge–practice connection or effective method require a different specialization or field boundary? | Contributions whose useful meaning survives the revision. |

AI can change the size of work that one configuration can perform, the need for intermediate handovers and the cost of trying alternatives. It can also perform inquiry or coordination where the necessary information, means and mandate are available. Compare the resulting whole arrangement. Preserve a human's own performance where that is part of the required result or an applicable condition; elsewhere qualify the most suitable available means without presuming a permanent human–AI division.

[SYSE.46](../syse/67-syse-46---test-an-agent-and-its-support-in-representative-wo.md) examines the actual configuration in representative work. Its result needs the task, model, tools, information, support, access and conditions of reliance. Different personas can share the same source error. Longer uninterrupted execution can still fail at receiving use. Conversely, an older failure does not establish the limits of a changed configuration.

The June 2026 [MirrorCode paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30182v1) supplies evidence of long-horizon reconstruction of specified software behavior using executable references and tests. Its gotree result was 2,000 of 2,001 tests for the reported run, not complete success. That task and its testing conditions support reconsidering some divisions of software work; they leave product usefulness, professional authority and a complete product-management result to their own evidence. The [current benchmark site](https://epoch.ai/MirrorCode) should be read with the exact study or configuration being compared. A claim that professions will disappear requires additional grounds about the broader work and its continuing organization.

The return can therefore retain the profession while changing its repertoire, retain the repertoire while changing performers, or alter the division among professions. Diagnose which relation changed. A new title is useful when it makes a recurring contribution clearer.
