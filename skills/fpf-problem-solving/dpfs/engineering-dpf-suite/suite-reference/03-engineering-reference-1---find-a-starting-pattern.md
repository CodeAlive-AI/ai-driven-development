## Engineering.Reference:1 - Find a starting pattern

Choose the row that matches the question you need to answer. Several patterns in one row are alternatives or complementary contributions; their own working conditions determine which you need.


### Engineering.Reference:1.1 - Begin before anyone asks for help

A team can keep delivering through recurring adjustments, or regard unusable outputs as ordinary, without requesting a review. [FPF B.5.PI - Initiate Inquiry from Ongoing Work](../../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/42-b-5-pi---initiate-inquiry-from-work.md) starts with an ordinary handover, demonstration or explanation of a consequential choice. Follow one result into its intended use and recover what was needed to obtain it. A comparison can yield a question, a retained early cue or a reasoned continuation without change; no anomaly label or PatternID is needed beforehand.

The connected move is to make a comparison possible, preserve what the familiar account misses, ask from the situation, obtain the missing contribution and return it to the original work. If a participant cannot express the distinction, [FPF B.5.EA](../../../sections/08-part-b---trans-disciplinary-reasoning-cluster/24-b-5-ea---articulate-a-distinction-from-experience.md) helps without demanding a diagnosis. [FPF A.15.11](../../../sections/07-cluster-a-v---constitutional-principles-of-the-kernel/26-a-15-11---make-methods-noticeable-in-work.md) arranges the occasion and reachable advice when those are missing. It can also adapt known checks into a usable execution checklist; passing them does not settle concerns outside their scope.

Use an existing subject answer directly when it suffices. If the way of working itself needs development, [C.39.MF and C.39.RQ](03-engineering-reference-1---find-a-starting-pattern.md#engineeringreference17---improve-a-method-or-an-organization) recover the method and its situational requirements. [Research Method Practice](03-engineering-reference-1---find-a-starting-pattern.md#engineeringreference127---qualify-a-research-question-or-choose-a-design) enters only if the unresolved question needs research; [human capability development](03-engineering-reference-1---find-a-starting-pattern.md#engineeringreference112---develop-a-practice-or-a-persons-capability) enters when a person needs to learn a contribution. A cue without a feasible response is not completed assistance. The [handover case](04-engineering-reference-2---why-examine-a-handover-that-arrive.md) shows the return to ordinary work.

### Engineering.Reference:1.30 - Retain or improve a research Method

Actual research work, criticism, a changed tool or new receiving conditions can raise a Method question. First distinguish the reusable Method from its description, implementation, available capability and the particular analysis or claim. | Your question | Open | What you can obtain |
| --- | --- | --- |

For example, an accepted sample-variance Method for n independent observations divides the squared-deviation sum by n−1.
