## Engineering.Reference:10 - Can we recommend a repair before it is ready to begin?

Yes, when the evidence supports the recommendation even though a condition for performing it is still missing. In the [constructed PS17 application (MNT — Cross-Pattern Applications)](../mnt/_index.md#cross-pattern-applications), the requested result is a bearing-replacement recommendation. [Diagnose Condition and Choose a Maintenance Response (MNT.6)](../mnt/17-mnt-6---diagnose-condition-and-choose-a-maintenance-response.md) compares the supported responses. [Prepare Maintenance Service Capability, Spares, Tools, and Authority (MNT.5)](../mnt/16-mnt-5---prepare-maintenance-service-capability-spares-tools.md) identifies the missing specialist return on the spare's applicability. The advice is complete; actual replacement cannot begin.

If intervention is then selected and the applicability return arrives, [Coordinate Maintenance Intervention and Continuing Operation (MNT.7)](../mnt/18-mnt-7---coordinate-maintenance-intervention-and-continuing-o.md) uses the operating-capacity result supplied in the case. [Qualify Capacity under Variability (OPS.10)](../../management-dpf-suite/ops/40-ops-10---qualify-operating-capacity-under-variability.md) explains how such an operating contribution is obtained when it is needed. The case's four-hour reduced-capacity allowance is distinct from the two-day lower-duty bridge that meets full present demand. The constructed whole-outage plan uses 225 of 240 minutes, including testing and hand-back. Changed demand or delay can defeat that fit.

The selected work proceeds only under the applicable protection and permission described in [Isolate, Make Safe, and Authorize the Maintenance Intervention (MNT.8)](../mnt/19-mnt-8---isolate-make-safe-and-authorize-the-maintenance-inte.md). [Perform and Record the Maintenance Intervention (MNT.9)](../mnt/20-mnt-9---perform-and-record-the-maintenance-intervention.md) records what was actually performed and the resulting configuration; [Verify Restored Functioning after Maintenance (MNT.10)](../mnt/21-mnt-10---verify-restored-functioning-after-maintenance.md) qualifies the required functioning; [Hand Back and Authorize Resumed Use after Maintenance, with Certification Where Required (MNT.11)](../mnt/22-mnt-11---hand-back-and-authorize-resumed-use-after-maintenan.md) handles control and resumed-use authority. These are separate claims, not a compulsory sequence for an advice request. Long-horizon recurrence remains unresolved. Stop at the adequate requested result, and reopen only the question whose evidence or operating conditions changed.
