## RHY.Reference:12 - Relations, source return and renewal

Use the following contributions when the rhythmic task requires them.

| Supplier | Contribution and receiving use |
| --- | --- |
| [FPF Core](../../sections/03-first-principles-framework-readme/_index.md) | Use A.22 to select a structure, C.16 and A.19.CPM for measurement and comparison, B.1.5.EW for simultaneous constituent and encompassing work, C.36.RP to make a way of working obtainable, and C.11.DUA to assess the value and cost of further inquiry. |
| [Notational Engineering (NOT)](../foundational-thinking-dpf-suite/not/_index.md) | NOT.1/.3/.5/.6/.8 supply general selection, interpretation, translation and complementary representations; RHY.5/.6 apply the needed contributions to rhythmic work. |
| [Mathematical Thinking (MATH)](../foundational-thinking-dpf-suite/math/_index.md) | When a formal comparison is needed, MATH.2 constructs equivalence classes that preserve the operations used by RHY.5; MATH.18 establishes which consequences transfer through an interpretation used by RHY.6. |
| [Human Capability Development (HCD)](../human-capability-engineering-dpf-suite/hcd/_index.md) | HCD.6/.9/.10 supply representative task, feedback and practice construction; HCD.12/.13 supply human capability probes. RHY.12/.13 retain the rhythmic task and its limits. |
| [Music and Dance Practice Engineering (MDPE)](../mdpe/_index.md) | Use MDPE.3 to choose or assess a performing configuration. A plan can specify required performers, instruments and conditions. Check availability when committing to perform, and use evidence of the occasion to establish who and what actually took part. MDPE.12 guides broader evidence use, and MDPE.17–.20 give receiving, source, continuation and forecast methods for the two cultural routes. |

Use the Readme for practical entries, this Reference for their connections and each Method for instructions, examples and source qualifications. Obtain instrumental or dance technique, curriculum design, population-research methods or specialist sensing from the relevant practice when the requested result needs them.

When a notation convention, arrangement, support, timing criterion, source finding or supplier’s contribution changes, reconsider the uses that depend on it. Keep unaffected conclusions. When readers repeatedly misunderstand an instruction, repair its explanation and the uses that rely on it. For a missing operation, check whether an existing method or combination supplies it. For the earlier and current addresses of RHY.7, RHY.7:4.1 and RHY.13:4.4, see [Edition and reuse](28-edition-and-reuse.md).
