## C.38 - Construct Comparable Ways to Obtain One Result

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable
> **Normativity:** Normative unless marked informative

### C.38:1 - Problem frame

**Plain name.** Turn labels such as *build*, *buy*, *reuse*, *provider*, *internal team*, *outsource*, or *AI* into comparable complete ways of getting the same result.

**Use this when.** An appraisal, construction or choice calls for comparable ways of obtaining one named result. The proposals may mix performers, Systems, Methods, commercial labels and enabling conditions. Establish what each whole way could deliver on the receiving terms before presenting the comparison or using it to choose.

**Reader.** The practitioner preparing decision-ready alternatives for one receiver. The pattern forms candidates; it neither chooses for the receiver nor realizes a retained way.

**First useful result.** A comparison establishes what the proposed ways can supply on the same receiving terms, which differences matter and which premises remain open. Two labels can describe the same way. A finding that one or none of the named ways meets the terms can finish their appraisal. An order to construct additional ways remains open until they are supplied or their impossibility within the permitted range is established. Where `C.11` applies, use the resulting `OptionSet` to derive a supported disposition. Its `ChoiceResult` may be advice to the chooser. To report an effective decision, establish the act or delegated procedure that gave it effect. Where realization is due, return the retained way to the direct realization Method. Identify the first unsupported branch if it prevents the work.

**What changes in practice.** Instead of comparing *buy* against *AI* or *internal* as if the labels were peer kinds, the practitioner asks how each whole way could make the same accepted result available. Duplicate labels merge, materially different ways under one label split, and omitted integration, evidence, support, capability, custody, resource, consequence, and exit burdens become visible before commitment.

**When to stop.** If the receiver can already obtain the ordered result on the agreed terms and no further choice or appraisal is due under the applicable commission, show how the existing provision meets those terms and omit the proposed new work. If one authorized way suffices and no comparison is due, use the direct domain Method. A domain comparison that already answers the decision can go straight to the chooser. For a choice, follow the governing decision method. Use `C.11` when it governs selection from the complete `OptionSet`.

**Use another pattern for these questions:**

- `A.15.9` for one missing or unqualified bounded result from another practice;
- `C.39` for a missing obtaining explanation;
- `C.40` for developing and examining material, including its coupled entry when problems and ways must change together;
- `C.18` for archive, front or possibility-space claims;
- `C.19` for live-pool exploration policy;
- `C.32` for architecture-candidate synthesis;
- `A.15.8` for one actual-Work or present-WorkPlan support configuration; and
- the direct domain Method for realization, production, provision, contracting, acceptance, or use.

### C.38:2 - Problem

Common option labels name unlike things. *Buy* may name a purchase relation, *provider* a continuing arrangement, *internal* a performer location, *reuse* an already available System, *AI* one possible Agent, and *build* many kinds of Work. Two labels can describe the same whole way, while two offers carrying the same label can differ in integration, custody, support, evidence, capability, and exit enough to reverse the choice.

The error is not merely missing detail. If candidate rows seek different results, use different acceptance bases, or silently switch situations and horizons, no amount of scoring restores comparability. If possible-future rows are written as facts, capability, authority, Work, provision, delivery, acceptance, and availability are asserted without their direct support.

The needed move is to fix one result question, construct complete-enough materially different ways on one parity basis, keep premise modality visible, and freeze the candidates for choice. It is smaller than a procurement or architecture programme and more complete than a make-or-buy label table.

### C.38:3 - Forces

| Force | Tension |
| --- | --- |
| Familiar labels versus comparable contents | Short labels help search, but they hide unlike relations and burdens. |
| Completeness versus proportionality | Every way must be complete enough for this decision without becoming a universal arrangement description. |
| Shared basis versus material difference | Rows need the same result and parity questions while preserving the differences that can change the choice. |
| Possible future versus current fact | A useful candidate describes what could be arranged without asserting that capability, authority, Work, provision, or acceptance already obtains. |
| Comparability versus forced total order | Some ways remain incomparable, gated, or tied; one scalar can erase protected conditions and decision-reversing uncertainty. |
| Common Method versus domain ownership | Result-first formation transfers across domains, while specialist criteria, architecture, contracting, realization, and acceptance remain local. |
| Candidate formation versus choice | A well-formed finite set is necessary for `C.11`, but constructing the set does not choose or borrow the chooser's authority. |

### C.38:4 - Solution

Start from the receiving commission. When the answer depends on what present arrangements can supply, establish what the receiver can obtain if they continue. For an appraisal confined to named proposals, follow each proposal on its stated terms. Check access, fitness for the intended use and availability over the required period where these affect the answer. An item in a supplier’s catalogue or a file in an archive establishes availability to this receiver only if the receiver can use it on those terms. Where something is missing, name it. That may leave only a permission to obtain or an adaptation to make. Use `C.42` if removing existing work would affect the provision on which the receiver relies.

Apply the same receiving conditions to each proposed way. Continuing the present arrangement may itself qualify, with its ongoing costs and dependencies. An appraisal may also use it to show what would happen without a change, even when it fails a condition for selection. Return the comparison to its commissioning recipient. If a choice is due, give those entitled to decide the alternatives and their grounds in a form their decision method can use.

#### C.38:4.1 - Follow the seven-step same-result sequence

1. **Fix the result of the commission.** Name the receiver, intended use, horizon and acceptance conditions. Identify who can commit to a way when that authority affects feasibility or a commitment is due. Separate unlike ends. If the commission requires both delivery now and the receiving team’s ability to do a later job under changed conditions, keep both in every way. State what will count as an acceptable delivery and, separately, what the team must decide or perform on the later job and what help it may use.
2. **Find what is already available and what is missing.** Use earlier work when its conditions still apply. Prior independent performance can establish the team’s capability if it covers the change in question. When the team must exercise independent judgement, establish which choices the brief, tools or helper already supply and examine the choices the team makes on its own. If the order calls for constructing a rule for a class of jobs, examine an earlier work product or give a task that leaves the consequential choices open. Check how the team justified the rule and where it applies. When the order calls for applying an approved rule, give the team that rule. A supplier’s accepted delivery establishes the delivery. Credit the team with decisions evidenced by its own work. Seek a missing or unqualified result from another practice through `A.15.9`.
3. **Recover the proposed ways and construct alternatives when needed.** Work back from the handover the receiver must be able to use. What must be produced, supplied or decided for it to occur? For a named proposal, follow its stated allocation, operations and sources forward through handovers, permissions and shared conditions to the result it could supply. If the recipient needs a different way, change an allocation, operation or source that matters and follow the altered arrangement through to the same receiving result. For each surviving way, describe the relevant proposed or available Agents, Work, Methods, Systems, values, provision or access relations, enabling branches, and conditions through which the same result could become available. Mark every decision-bearing premise as supported now, proposed, or unknown. Merge labels that resolve to the same decision-changing contents; split ways under one label when a content difference can change the choice. A way may combine delivery by a provider with work through which the team acquires and demonstrates the required capability. State how that capability will be checked and mark it as proposed until there is evidence. When the gap is an unexplained obtaining operation, use `C.39` to develop a candidate explanation or identify the unresolved contribution. A single supported way can close a request for a way to proceed. For a request to find another eligible way, distinguish a way constructed, a proof excluding every further way in the permitted range, and an open search. Comparing specified ways can finish with one or none eligible.
4. **Use one decision-changing parity basis.** Ask the same bounded questions of every way: result and availability condition; performers, Work, Methods, and means; production, transfer, provision, access, custody, or ownership; fit, interfaces, integration, and configuration; evidence, uncertainty, and assurance; support, maintenance, change, exit, and recovery; capability consequences; resources; and consequences for affected Systems. Keep questions that determine whether any proposal can deliver, including a prerequisite shared by every proposed way. Omit a group when its answers affect neither eligibility nor the comparison required. Keep a decision-reversing unknown as a visible gap.
5. **Restore comparability without forcing totality.** Keep the result, receiving use, acceptance conditions, situation, configuration boundary, evidence horizon, and burden categories shared. Use ordinary comparison when the ordering is direct. Use `A.19.CPM` and `A.19.SelectorMechanism` only when evidence gates, partial orders, incomparability, abstention, or set-valued retention matter. Do not hide protected conditions inside an unexplained score.
6. **Return the comparison for its intended use.** Deliver the appraisal when its grounds answer the commissioned question. Where `C.11` applies, provide a finite `OptionSet` of complete-enough whole ways and identify the outstanding conditions. The rule can support retaining a single way or a set. For the set, show which ways tie on the applied criterion and which remain incomparable under the stated order. Other dispositions include rejecting the current set, making another inquiry or taking a different route. Deliver a recommendation through the route authorized for this commission. To report an effective choice, establish either the authorized participant's adoption or the application of a rule empowered to decide this case.
7. **Name the continuation and reopen.** For a retained way, identify the first unsupported realization branch, if any, or the direct Method that can now proceed. Reopen only when a changed result identity, use, acceptance basis, candidate, specialist result, capability, interface, evidence item, burden, support condition, affected-System consequence, exit condition, or observation can change the comparison or choice.

#### C.38:4.2 - Use a proportional parity view

There is no universal arrangement schema. For the current decision, a small table or a few parallel paragraphs are enough if another reader can answer these questions for every way:

| Current decision question | What to make visible |
| --- | --- |
| Same result | What result becomes available, to whom, under which acceptance condition? |
| How it could happen | Which proposed or available Agents, Work, Methods, Systems, values, and direct relations matter? |
| Fit and joining | Which interfaces, configuration, integration, and enabling branches can reverse the choice? |
| Reliance | Which evidence, uncertainty, assurance, authority, and protected conditions matter? |
| Continuation | Which support, maintenance, change, custody, recovery, and exit conditions remain? |
| Consequences | Which resources, capabilities, opportunity costs, and affected Systems change? |
| Premise status | What is supported now, proposed, or unknown? |

Keep a row for a common prerequisite when it determines whether any candidate can deliver. Two services requiring the same unavailable access both fail that condition. Remove a question when its answer affects neither eligibility nor the comparison the receiver needs. Add a local question when omitting it could change that conclusion. A candidate is complete enough when the receiving party can identify the means, dependencies, burdens and gaps that matter to this appraisal.

#### C.38:4.3 - Return the first result in plain language

For a short appraisal, the following form can carry the conclusion to its recipient.

> **Result and use** — [What was requested, by whom, for which situation, horizon and acceptance conditions?]
>
> **Proposals examined** — [What does each offer? Which names describe the same way? Which premises are supported, proposed or open?]
>
> **Basis for comparison** — [Apply the same receiving terms to each way. Give the differences in provision, burdens and gaps that matter here.]
>
> **Conclusion for this commission** — [Which distinct ways, if any, meet the terms? Give the grounds. Identify any open condition that could change the answer.]
>
> **Further work, when needed** — [A choice can go to C.11. For a request for another way, give the way obtained, the grounds excluding any further way in the permitted range, or the search still needed. For a way already chosen, use the direct domain Method or develop its first unsupported realization branch.]

An appraisal of named proposals can finish with its conclusion. If that conclusion relies on an actual capability, authorization, delivery or use, identify the evidence for it. The responsible participants can use the comparison to choose, arrange and perform a way when the commission calls for those actions.

#### C.38:4.4 - Retain the comparison without duplication

An immediate sufficient comparison can finish without a new record. When later use needs its basis retained, use the least burdensome arrangement:

1. If a domain result already carries the complete same-use comparison, keep it there rather than producing a second account.
2. Otherwise retain one ordinary `C.2.1` episteme with the one result-obtaining comparison as its EntityOfConcern and an effective reference scheme.
3. If a direct domain Method already returns the complete useful comparison more cheaply, use that Method and do not add a `C.38` account.

The episteme, table, document, or diagram that carries the claims does not, merely by carrying them, establish a world-side arrangement, selected Structure, architecture, plan, Work, or result availability. Use `A.22`, `C.30`, `C.29`, `E.17`, or another direct pattern only when its separate question is current.

#### C.38:4.5 - Separate recognition from assurance

- **Recognition.** Start with the receiving use. If the ordered result is already available, establish whether anyone still has a choice to make. When several ways remain and their labels conceal differences that matter to that choice, two parallel descriptions can suffice for a reversible decision.
- **Assurance.** Consequential use checks that every row seeks the same result on the same basis, every actual premise has direct support, modal premises remain modal, protected conditions remain visible, and no omitted burden can plausibly reverse the choice unnoticed. Domain acceptance, assurance, authority, realization, and release stay with their direct Methods.

### C.38:5 - Worked cases

#### C.38:5.1 - Greenhouse climate-control result

A project needs one accepted result: a named greenhouse configuration maintains the required temperature and humidity envelope through the next operating horizon. Its first list says *build*, *buy*, *provider*, and *AI*.

The team resolves those labels into three materially different ways: integrate internally owned equipment and control Work; obtain a configurable control System and perform local integration and operation; or obtain a provider-operated climate-control result with continuing sensing, access, support, and exit dependence. An AI Agent may perform forecasting or tuning Work inside any way; it is not a peer arrangement kind.

The same parity questions expose plant interfaces, commissioning evidence, response to sensor loss, maintenance, operating capability, data access, affected crops, support, and exit. The direct engineering pattern keeps the greenhouse-specific architecture and assurance. `C.38` supplies only the same-result formation and comparison, then hands the finite set to `C.11`. After a choice, the first unsupported realization branch returns to the direct Systems Engineering Method.

#### C.38:5.2 - A performance recording needs one usable artifact

A music project commissions a particular performance and a recording from which a later learner can recover when two groups entered and where they stood relative to one another. Before the performance it compares local capture, a specialist mobile team and the venue’s service for that same use. An earlier rehearsal can help test a proposed recording method. The commissioned performance still lies ahead.

For each way the team asks how the later learner could establish both relations, what they would have permission to use and which parts of the proposal still need testing. The team can send the alternatives to `C.11` before the performance.

After the performance the learner can retrieve two time-aligned tracks identified with that run and labelled by group. Both start before either group enters, and the first musical entry of each group can be identified on its track. Their times on the common clock establish the order and interval. The recorder took the two groups’ feeds directly and saved no positions. The same pair of tracks is compatible with either group on the left. A cue sheet recording the actual placements for that performance may settle the remaining question if the learner can match it to the tracks and access it on the agreed terms. If a single annotated recording was ordered, use the sheet to complete that recording. If the existing material already meets the agreed terms, the order needs no further production. If placement cannot be recovered, record that limit.

The production Method governs capture, use rights and acceptance of the recording. If the commission also calls for a learner who can interpret a later recording independently, examine that learner’s work. When a tutor identifies both entries, the learner may still measure the interval. The measurement leaves open whether the learner could identify the entries unaided. If devising a rule for future recordings is also commissioned, the learner can choose how to distinguish an entry from a tuning sound and justify when the rule applies.

#### C.38:5.3 - Direct Method-base stop

A Method Engineering team needs an enactment-support configuration for one bounded task set. Its direct Method already constructs candidate configurations on one basis, preserves evidence status and incomparability, tests retained routes, and returns a supported configuration, retained set, split, or gap.

Use that direct Method. Adding a `C.38` account for the same decision would duplicate its action and result. Open `C.38` only if a wider question appears—for example, several complete ways to make an independently named support result available to another receiver.

### C.38:6 - Precision restoration

| Source label | Recover before comparison | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *build* | Sought result, proposed Work, Methods, performers, Systems, enabling branches, evidence, support, and exit needed by this decision. | Internal capability, authorization, Work, or success. |
| *buy* or *lease* | Proposed provision, transfer, access, custody, ownership, support, and acceptance relations inside the whole way. | Purchase is the whole way or the result is available. |
| *provider* or *outsource* | Proposed provider Work, supplied result, interfaces, evidence, responsibility, support, change, and exit boundaries. | Provider label establishes capability, authority, service, delivery, or acceptance. |
| *internal* | Which Systems may perform which proposed Work under which support and capability premises. | Employment or organizational location is an arrangement kind or capability proof. |
| *reuse* | The already-available System or result, fit, permitted use, adaptation, integration, evidence, support, and recovery. | Prior existence proves present suitability or availability. |
| *AI* or *automation* | The Agent or tool's proposed contribution inside each way, plus capability, access, evidence, authority, stop, and recovery where needed. | A performer species is a whole way or receives decision authority. |
| *arrangement* or *option* | The complete-enough claim bundle for this one comparison and its premise modality. | A new universal kind, selected Structure, architecture, or obtaining world-side relation. |

**Bias check.** Compare the supported benefits, burdens and limits of each way for this result and situation, including any decision-relevant weakness. An adequate incumbent may remain preferable. Familiarity, novelty, popularity or institutional standing alone does not establish suitability.

### C.38:7 - Conformance and practical checks

A use conforms only when the checks needed by its claimed comparison pass:

1. The result, receiver, use, horizon and acceptance conditions are explicit. Existing provision is assessed when it bears on this question. The assessment identifies the committing party when authority affects feasibility or an actual commitment is due. If the commission includes the receiver’s later capability, the comparison identifies the work that would establish it and the assistance allowed.
2. The alternatives address the same receiving result. Ground their material differences, what rules any out, and any claim that the permitted range is exhausted.
3. Every decision-bearing premise is visibly supported now, proposed, or unknown; proposed or unknown premises establish no actual capability, authority, Work, provision, delivery, acceptance, availability, or use.
4. The same decision-changing parity questions are applied to every way; an omitted group is justified by irrelevance to this choice.
5. A decision-reversing unknown remains a gap or probe. Protected conditions are not hidden inside one unexplained scalar.
6. `A.19.CPM` or `A.19.SelectorMechanism` is used only when its evidence-gating, incomparability, abstention, or set-return contribution is needed.
7. Include only complete-enough whole ways in a finite `OptionSet`. The commissioned appraisal can finish when it establishes what the proposed ways would deliver. When a decision is due, follow it to the holder or procedure empowered to make it. An adviser can already deliver the comparison and the disposition supported by `C.11` while adoption is pending.
8. The rule for retaining the comparison prevents a duplicate episteme when an owning domain result already carries the complete comparison.
9. A retained way names any first unsupported realization branch or the direct Method that can proceed; the comparison itself claims no realization or later Work.
10. The reopen condition names a changed premise or result that can alter the comparison or choice.

**Recognition check.** The practitioner can recover one common result from a *build/buy/provider/AI* list and identify a decision-relevant hidden difference or duplicate. An arrangement taxonomy is unnecessary.

**Assurance check.** For a consequential use, inspect each actual premise under its direct evidence and authority patterns, examine any plausible omitted burden that could reverse the choice, and verify that the selected domain assurance and acceptance Methods remain outside `C.38`.

### C.38:8 - Anti-patterns

- **Labels as option kinds.** Build, buy, provider, internal, reuse, or AI are compared as peers without resolving their contents.
- **Different results in one table.** One row supplies a System, another a service, another a capability, and another an episteme, while all are scored as if equivalent.
- **Universal arrangement schema.** A local parity view becomes a mandatory ontology, graph, taxonomy, or workflow for every domain.
- **Modal-to-actual leap.** A possible-future row is treated as capability, authority, Work, provision, purchase, delivery, acceptance, or availability.
- **Score before parity.** Numbers conceal different assumptions, missing burdens, or protected conditions.
- **Endless generation.** Open-ended candidate invention is kept inside `C.38`. Use `C.39` for a missing obtaining explanation or `C.40` for feasible variation and examination; add `C.18` for archive/front claims and `C.19` for live-pool policy only when those contributions are needed.
- **Choice by formatting.** The most detailed or first row is treated as selected before `C.11`.
- **Domain takeover.** The common Method absorbs architecture, procurement, contracting, finance, legal, artistic, operational, or assurance decisions.
- **Duplicate account.** A domain Method already returns the full comparison, but a second generic episteme is added anyway.

### C.38:9 - Consequences and trade-offs

| Consequence | Practical effect |
| --- | --- |
| Truthful alternatives | Candidate rows become whole ways to obtain the same result rather than a mix of labels and objects. |
| Earlier burden visibility | Integration, evidence, support, capability, custody, consequence, recovery, and exit can change the choice before commitment. |
| Preserved modality | Plans and candidate rows keep proposed world-side relations distinct from independently supported current ones. |
| Better choice input | `C.11` receives a finite complete-enough `OptionSet` and visible gaps instead of being asked to repair its own candidates. |
| Domain autonomy | Subject Methods keep architecture, provision, contracting, acceptance, realization, and assurance. |
| Additional preparation cost | The team must resolve familiar labels and ask comparable questions before scoring or choosing. |
| Bounded completeness | The parity basis is sufficient only for this decision and must reopen when a decision-changing premise changes. |

### C.38:10 - Rationale and SoTA use

The practice question is how to construct several decision-ready ways to obtain one named result without implying a choice, treating possible futures as facts, or imposing a domain-specific arrangement schema. Begin with the receiving result and use, expand each candidate under consideration into a complete-enough possible-future way, and expose the same decision-relevant burdens and limits. The resulting finite `OptionSet` is an input to C.11 when local choice is current.

An initial table may contain labels such as internal, provider, buy, build, partner, procurement vehicle or lifecycle stage. Scoring those labels before recovering their contents can mix unlike alternatives and hide integration, evidence, support, capability, custody, consequence, recovery and exit burdens. `C.38` repairs that defect by fixing one result and receiver, resolving every label into a whole possible-future way, applying a bounded parity basis, marking unknowns without inventing current relations, and stopping before local choice or actual Work.

The common comparison depends on its domain inputs. Systems Engineering supplies the engineered-System conditions, specialist criteria, realization and assurance; the recording Method supplies artistic, participatory and production requirements. Those differences determine what a complete way must include. When a domain Method already returns the sufficient comparison, use that result directly.

In formal appraisal, the expected result of continuing current arrangements supplies a baseline even when it falls short of the objective. In procurement, the buyer can commission knowledge and skills transfer alongside the immediate deliverable and specify how transfer will be assessed. These practices answer different questions in the comparison. [HM Treasury, The Green Book, chapter 4](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026) · [Cabinet Office, The Consultancy Playbook, Knowledge and skills](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-consultancy-playbook/the-consultancy-playbook-html).

Reopen the affected candidate or parity question when a decision-changing premise changes. Reconsider the comparison approach when an available direct Method supplies the needed result more economically or when evidence shows that the current comparison hides a consequential burden.

### C.38:11 - Relations

- **Builds on:** the direct pattern for the sought result; `A.10` for evidence and bounded reliance on actual premises; `A.19.CPM` and `A.19.SelectorMechanism` only for comparison mechanisms that are needed; and `C.2.1` when a standalone comparison episteme must persist.
- **Hands to:** `C.11` only after a finite complete-enough `OptionSet` exists. `C.11` owns choose, reject, probe-again, and reroute results.
- **Coordinates with:** `C.39` for a missing obtaining explanation; `C.40` for material development and its conditional coupled problem-and-solution entry; `C.18` for archive, front and possibility-space claims; `C.19` for pool policy; `C.32` for architecture-candidate synthesis; `A.22` and `C.30` only when a separate Structure or architecture question is current; and direct domain Methods for realization, production, provision, acceptance, assurance, and use.
- **Question-change boundary with A.15.9:** use `A.15.9` only for one missing or unqualified bounded result from another practice inside a way. Stay in `C.38` when the question is several complete ways to obtain the same receiving result. A contribution request or return is never the whole way by implication.
- **Keeps outside:** universal arrangement kinds, graphs, taxonomies, schemas, workflows, authority transfer, open-ended search, local choice, and all actual Work or obtaining relations inferred only from possible-future rows.
